## 2. Five-layer infrastructure rule

Every infrastructure, maintenance, repair, restoration, and public-service
example must keep these five layers distinct:

1. **Physical construction accounting** - measured material, energy, stock,
   loss, service, land, time, maintenance, and other declared physical effects.
2. **Certified field update** - evidence and certification that justify a new
   version of a physical field or its capacity, efficiency, regeneration,
   storage, route, or topology properties.
3. **Authorization** - the rule and authority that permit, reject, defer, or
   condition an action; P1C permission remains separate from EBU sign.
4. **Institutional responsibility and settlement** - the organization or
   account that carries a physical or contractual settlement line.
5. **Technician or worker compensation** - wages, access, services, benefits,
   reduced obligations, professional entitlements, or another declared
   institutional mechanism for the people performing the work.

The required non-equivalence is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical burden}
\neq
\text{personal blame}
\neq
\text{institutional responsibility}
\neq
\text{compensation}
}
\]

In plain language, a construction receipt may be negative without making the
technician personally liable. The receipt records the action's represented
physical consequence. It does not decide who should finance the action, carry
the settlement line, or compensate the worker.

A government, municipality, hospital, infrastructure owner, cooperative, or
another institution may carry settlement responsibility. Compensation may use
access guarantees, services, benefits, reduced obligations, professional
entitlements, wages, or another declared institutional mechanism. The future
revision must compare these as institutional choices and must not freeze one
political or constitutional solution.

Only immediate measured effects and the declared construction burden enter the
current action quote. Predicted future benefit does not generate speculative
immediate positive EBU. A planning model may compare possible futures in a
separate layer, but it must not rewrite the action quote or receipt.

## 3. Versioned field-update rule

### 3.1 Explanatory sequence

The future revision must define and illustrate this order:

\[
\boxed{
\text{repair action and receipt}
\rightarrow
\text{measurement}
\rightarrow
\text{certification}
\rightarrow
\Theta^m\to\Theta^{m+1}
\rightarrow
\text{future actions use }\Theta^{m+1}
}
\]

The repair's current physical effects and construction burden settle under the
field version valid for that action. Measurement then tests whether a genuine
persistent physical property changed. Certification, not the worker's forecast
and not a preferred EBU sign, authorizes the next field version. Only later
actions may use that new version.

The versioned field \(\Theta^m\) may include evidenced physical efficiency
\(\eta\), carrying capacity \(K\), regeneration law \(g\), storage, route
capacity, topology, or location-derived physical consequences. Other properties
may be added only with units, measurement method, uncertainty, validity period,
causal scope, provenance, and recertification conditions.

Old quotes and receipts are immutable historical records. They must never be
recomputed under \(\Theta^{m+1}\). A correction record may address an actual
error under separately declared rules, but recertification does not make the
old field retroactively false.

### 3.2 Physical change versus valuation manipulation

A genuine physical change in \(\eta\), \(K\), \(g\), capacity, storage, route
performance, or topology must be distinguished from changing valuation
parameters such as \(\alpha\), \(\beta\), \(L\), or \(U\) merely to obtain a
preferred EBU result. The former requires physical evidence and certification.
The latter is parameter manipulation unless separately justified by domain
measurement, governance, provenance, and a prospective epoch rule.

The field-update record must therefore state:

- old and new field identifiers and versions;
- the physical property claimed to have changed, with units;
- measurement and calibration records;
- uncertainty and identification limits;
- certification authority and decision;
- effective time, expiry, and recertification rule;
- affected regions, routes, actions, and boundaries;
- whether a valuation parameter also changed and why; and
- an explicit statement that prior receipts remain under their original field.

### 3.3 Location rule

Location has no automatic EBU value. It matters only through measured physical
or service consequences such as route length, transfer loss, delay, capacity,
energy, risk, reliability, deadline feasibility, or another declared effect.
Moving an institution nearer a recurring need is not credited because
"nearer" is intrinsically good; later transactions reveal whether the measured
route and service consequences improved.

The required replacement explanation is:

> A stock-only state cannot observe a capacity change. That model invisibility
> does not imply physical worthlessness. The repair's current burden remains
> recordable. If measurement and certification establish a genuine physical
> change, the field is updated prospectively without paying for a forecast.
> Future transactions reveal improved efficiency or feasibility as effects
> occur. The actor performing the repair need not personally bear the
> institutional settlement line, and the same actor is not guaranteed to
> capture all future benefits. Public infrastructure may therefore require
> collective provision under a separately declared institutional rule.

O1-M, O1-I, and O1-E remain unresolved. O1-Q and O1-F are explanatory rules,
not empirical claims that any particular repair has changed a field.

O2 family, causal-history programme, coalition-profit theorem, or other
substitute is created. V2.6 remains historical evidence that permission and
complete physical observation matter: within its certified scope P1C prevents
forbidden destructive trajectories, while the quote measures verified physical
effects. Profitable non-lethal improvement is not itself an exploit. Hidden or
omitted physical damage is an accounting or measurement limitation, not a
reason to cap all later actor profit. Part IX already owns intentional damage,
fraud, sanctions, eligibility, liability, compensation, and withholding; this
plan does not claim that P1C, measurement, or governance is perfect.

The established physical result remains the fixed-epoch cycle identity. For
each receipt under one certified field and parameter epoch,

\[
E_r=V(x_r)-V(x_{r+1})-C_r,\qquad C_r\ge0.
\]

For a boundary-complete cycle with no unaccounted drive,

\[
\sum_r E_r=-\sum_r C_r\le0.
\]

An equivalent damage-repair round trip therefore cannot manufacture positive
EBU: positive included costs make it strictly negative, while an idealized
genuinely zero-cost cycle produces zero. Here "equivalent" means restoration
of the complete relevant state and boundary account, not merely one visible
coordinate. The result follows from complete EBU state/boundary accounting and
telescoping, not from conservation of energy alone.

For example, action A may cause verified burden and receive `-10 EBU`, while a
later action B produces a genuine verified `+2000 EBU` improvement. The same
actor may perform both: actor identity does not reduce B's physical quote, and
net `+1990 EBU` is legitimate when B removes substantially more burden than A
created. If B were only the complete equivalent repair, the cycle identity
would make the combined result nonpositive. Subject to a complete and correct
account, a valid `+2000 EBU` result therefore means B is not merely an
equivalent repair. No historical actor netting, blanket profit cap, moral
judgment, or identity-dependent physical quote is introduced.

A delayed physical effect is measured in a later observation/accounting record
when it becomes observable under the applicable evidence rules; a valid
historical record is not retrospectively rewritten. Sensor failure, source
malfunction, finite precision, certification error, and latency use the
existing uncertainty, evidence, and certification treatment. An unidentified
or unobservable effect is recorded as a limitation or unresolved residual and
must not silently be treated as zero. Ordinary latency or imperfect
measurement is not automatically a new foundational EBU theory problem, and
this plan requires no causal-history subsystem or complete reconstruction of
who caused every later state.

## 8. Human-readable writing standard

The future revision must apply these rules throughout the complete manuscript:

1. Give the reason before the mechanism.
2. Define the physical object before introducing notation.
3. Follow every important equation with one plain-language explanation of what
   changes, what is held fixed, and what the equation does not prove.
4. Connect each section to the preceding chapter and state its exact role in
   EBU.
5. Do not use an unexplained "common model," named effect, theorem, or person.
6. Do not use a metaphor such as "the field cannot see" without naming the
   represented coordinates and the missing coordinate.
7. Split sentences that currently carry several distinct logical claims.
8. Retain worked examples and the equation-first/calculation style after the
   motivation and definitions.
9. Make no unsupported universality claim.
10. Do not use `Open problem` where the unresolved measurement, proof,
    identification, institutional rule, or epoch semantic can be stated more
    precisely.
11. Distinguish definitions, algebraic identities, theorems, implementation
    results, registered observations, hypotheses, analogies, and institutional
    choices in the prose as well as in summary tables.
12. Prefer direct causal wording over moralized or personalized physical
    debits. A worker performs an action; a person is not the physical burden.
