## Scientific boundaries

The illustrative core uses a homogeneous lossless scalar, nonnegative stocks,
fixed compatible references and positive declared scales. Potential is the
separable quadratic; it is not a physical stock or proof of normal observations.
Exact finite differences and their path/telescoping identities are taught under
stated assumptions. A marginal slope is not a finite quote or an actor policy.

Allee laws, L/U/R hinge penalties, P1C eligibility and timestep/error-control
certificates leave the new instructional core. They are not erased from history
or declared false for the models in which they were used. Ordinary measurement
uncertainty, sign checks, assumptions and numerical honesty remain necessary.

Simultaneous path attribution requires a declared path and interpretation; an
additive closure identity does not establish fairness, ownership, causal
entitlement or a real-world settlement rule. Prospective capacity and random
choice are identified as model proposals. No new recovery, institutional or
economic result is asserted.

Motivation uses dated primary sources and conditional projections. Monetary
systems have existing feedback, regulation and public institutions; their
limitations are not evidence that all such mechanisms are absent. EBU's social
purpose is a motivation, not an empirical benefit already demonstrated.
