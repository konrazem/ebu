# V3.0 Long-Horizon Exact-EBU Study — Decision Packet

**Status:** PROSPECTIVE DESIGN PACKET. This is **not** a preregistration, not an
execution authorization, and not a scientific record of any run. It records
three selected design decisions and holds every remaining decision open.

**Adopting a decision in this packet does not authorize** a model run, a
trajectory, a benchmark, an AWS action, a code implementation, a parameter
selection, or a scientific preregistration. Each of those is a separate
authorization boundary.

---

## 0. Selected decisions

Exactly three decisions are selected. Everything else in this packet is
**UNRESOLVED**.

| # | Decision | Selected value |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Topology and O3 handling | **Option B — shared endpoints permitted, no attribution** |
| **D3-2** | Simultaneous-action bound `m` | **`m = 2`** — at most two simultaneous actions per tick |
| **D2a-4** | Reserve regime | **R-b reserve-binding** — study worlds drive relevant sources toward the effective reserve boundary |

### D1 — Option B, in full

Shared endpoints are permitted. The physical transition and the exact joint EBU
are calculated **once**, on the atomic joint transition. The study records
`group_quote`, `naive_sum`, and `double_count`. Joint EBU is **not** allocated
among individual actions. O3 is **not** settled. **No causal attribution claim
is made.**

Authority basis: `CONSERVATION_AND_BOUNDARY_ACCOUNTING_FOUNDATION.md` §7 — the
physical transition is counted once, child effects evaluated from a shared
baseline must not be independently added, and "allocation among children is a
separate causal or institutional question."
`EBU_FUTURE_BOOKS_STRUCTURE.md` §563–565 — an interaction index is a named
endpoint comparison, not automatically a unique decomposition, and no
decomposition may be called causal without identification.

### D3-2 and D2a-4 — what they do and do not do

`m = 2` and reserve-binding are adopted **to remove two degeneracies**, not to
produce any particular result.

- With `m = 1` the joint transition is a single action, so `double_count` is
  **identically zero** and Option B would have nothing to measure. `m = 2` is
  the minimum bound that makes a joint transition non-trivial.
- With reserve-slack sources held in the flat band, the source potential
  contributes nothing to the endpoint difference and the interaction term
  vanishes by construction. Reserve-binding keeps the source potential live, so
  the interaction term can be non-zero and P1C screening is actually exercised.

**These selections prevent the Gate 1D-C flat-band / single-action degeneracy.
They do not predetermine an outcome.** `double_count` may still be measured as
zero, small, or large; the exact-EBU actor may serve more, equally, or less than
its matched comparators. Both directions remain fully reportable, and no
threshold, predicate, or expectation is registered here.

**Gate 1D-C is not design evidence.** Its `F = 4`, `m = 1`, 200-tick horizon,
flat-band sources and distinct destinations are its own registered choices. No
parameter, world, metric, threshold, or arm in the future study may be chosen
because Gate 1D-C produced a favourable outcome.

## 1. Non-negotiable rules

1. **Physical/homeostatic admissibility is evaluated before EBU.** P1C
   classifies the source state and computes the export budget first; a
   non-positive budget yields an empty menu; every candidate is pre-capped
   before any ranking occurs. Unsafe actions are rejected **before** any EBU
   value is consulted. The sequence is fixed: **admissibility first, then exact
   EBU evaluation, then declared selection.**
2. **The governing EBU value is only:**

   `Δe(q) = V_loc(z) − V_loc(z + Δt·S_e·q) − C_a(q)`

   It must be evaluated as an **exact finite endpoint difference** over the
   affected cells. Never per unit. Never by sampled quadrature. Never by an
   invented surrogate objective or burden function.
3. **EBU is an evaluation quantity** — never a wallet, balance, market price, or
   causal allocation.
4. **Shared-endpoint interaction is measured, not attributed.** Record
   `group_quote`, `naive_sum`, `double_count`. Allocate nothing.
5. **O3 remains open.** Nothing in this study settles aggregate multi-edge
   settlement.
6. **No smaller study substitutes for the long-run result.**

**Scaling form used throughout:** `K = F × Σ_{s∈S} d_out(s) ≤ F × E`
(budgeting upper bound; the executed candidate count is lower after flux and
acceptance screening).

## 2. D2a — World family **[UNRESOLVED except D2a-4]**

### D2a-1 Node count and edge structure **[UNRESOLVED]**

| Option | Can test | Cannot test | Exact-EBU / homeostasis | Scaling | O3 / Möbius |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W-a** Minimal shared-endpoint family | Whether interaction is non-zero and measurable at all | Scale effects, contention, degree heterogeneity | Smallest world admitting a joint transition | `Σ d_out(s)` minimal → smallest `K` | Measures O3 residual; no Möbius |
| **W-b** Heterogeneous degree (hub + periphery) | Whether exact-EBU starves low-degree destinations; degree-driven contention | Layered or long-path propagation | Hubs dominate `K`; homeostasis stress concentrates at hubs | `K` driven by hub `d_out`; linear in `E` | Same |
| **W-c** Shared-destination structure (multiple in-edges to one cell) | A second interaction channel, never exercised by any completed study | Source-side contention if sources are not also shared | Interaction arises at the destination potential | Independent of `d_out` | Same |

Note: every completed V3 world shares only a **source**. Shared **destinations**
are untested.

### D2a-2 Degree distribution **[UNRESOLVED]**

Uniform out-degree isolates horizon effects from heterogeneity; non-uniform
tests contention but confounds the two. `K` is set by `Σ d_out(s)` over deciding
sources, so this choice directly sets cost.

### D2a-3 Shocks **[UNRESOLVED]**

| Option | Can test | Cannot test | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S-a** No shock, steady demand | Homeostasis maintenance | Recovery, robustness | Cannot answer the recovery clause of the study question |
| **S-b** Single impulse | One relaxation/recovery episode; relaxation timescale | Whether recovery repeats or degrades | Minimum for a recovery claim |
| **S-c** Repeated shocks | Whether recovery completes before the next shock; cumulative drift or ratchet | Response to unmodelled shock types | Requires horizon ≥ several inter-shock intervals |

A reserve or stock shock is distinct from a demand pulse and is untested.

### D2a-4 Reserves — **SELECTED: R-b reserve-binding**

| Option | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **R-a** Reserve-slack (flat band) | **not selected** | Would reproduce the degenerate regime; interaction vanishes by construction |
| **R-b** Reserve-binding | **SELECTED** | Sources are driven toward the effective reserve boundary; P1C screening is exercised; the source potential stays live so interaction can be non-zero; homeostasis is genuinely falsifiable |
| **R-c** Mixed across worlds | **not selected** | Reserve regime as a declared factor remains available to a later, separately authorized study |

P1C/homeostatic admissibility continues to reject unsafe actions **before** any
EBU ranking. Reserve-binding increases how often that rejection is exercised; it
does not weaken, bypass, or reorder it.

## 3. D2b — Horizon and run structure **[UNRESOLVED]**

### D2b-1 Ticks **[UNRESOLVED]**

| Option | Phenomenon it must observe | Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| **H-a** ≥ one relaxation time | A single complete recovery after one shock | Repeatability; drift |
| **H-b** ≥ several relaxation times | Whether recovery completes repeatably between shocks | Slow cumulative drift |
| **H-c** ≫ relaxation time | Long-run drift, ratchet, slow reserve erosion | Anything outside the declared family |

The horizon must be justified by a **relaxation-timescale sufficiency argument**
derived statically from the world parameters — never by a compute budget.

### D2b-2 Worlds, arms, timesteps, replicates **[UNRESOLVED]**

- **Worlds** — count follows from the D2a factor structure.
- **Arms** — see D3-3.
- **Timesteps** — one certified `dt` (no dt-sensitivity claim) or two (supports a
  timestep-consistency hypothesis). Certificates must be recomputed fail-closed.
- **Replicates** — the V3 implementation is fully deterministic with no seed.
  Replicates can therefore only be parameter or initial-condition variations.
  Stochastic replicates would introduce noise semantics absent from V3 and would
  require their own registration.

Total trace scales as `R × T × B_row`. A trajectory is irreducibly sequential,
so the horizon alone sets the wall-clock floor.

## 4. D3 — Candidate menu and concurrency **[UNRESOLVED except D3-2]**

### D3-1 Fraction ladder `F` **[UNRESOLVED]**

| Option | Can test | Cannot test | Exact-EBU implication | Scaling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F-a** Coarse | Whether a low-resolution menu suffices | Fine preference structure | Risks artificial ties; the exact-EBU rule may be unable to express its preference | `K` smallest |
| **F-b** Medium | Preference expression at moderate resolution | Near-threshold discrimination | Balanced | Linear in `F` |
| **F-c** Fine | Near-threshold behaviour | Little beyond F-b | Best resolution of `Δe(q)` over `[0, q_acc]` | `K` largest |

`Δe(q)` is exact at every `q`. Resolution affects only which quantities are
*offered*; it never affects the accuracy of the value. There is no quadrature
parameter.

### D3-2 Simultaneous-action bound — **SELECTED: `m = 2`**

| Option | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **M-a** `m = 1` | **not selected** | Degenerate under Option B — `double_count` identically zero |
| **M-b** `m = 2` | **SELECTED** | Minimum bound admitting a non-trivial joint transition; cost enters linearly |
| **M-c** `m` = all feasible | **not selected** | Available to a later, separately authorized study |

Under Option B, `m` enters cost **linearly**: settlement commitments scale as
`4m` and the joint quote is `O(cells involved + m)`. The exponential `2^m` term
belongs to per-action attribution (Option C) and is **not** incurred here.

### D3-3 Matched non-EBU comparator arms **[UNRESOLVED]**

| Option | Contents | Can test | Cannot test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A-a** Minimal | baseline + exact-EBU actor | The primary comparison | Observational neutrality; instrument sensitivity |
| **A-b** Standard | baseline, observational-quote twin, exact-EBU actor, stock-blind control, capability reference | Byte-identity of observational quoting; certified instrument sensitivity; capability cost | — |
| **A-c** Extended | A-b plus regime or degree variants | Factor effects | Cost multiplies with arm count |

**Implementation consequence of `m = 2`:** the matched non-EBU baseline must
itself select up to two actions per tick, or capability is unmatched and the
comparison is invalid. No multi-action baseline selector exists today. Building
one is a separate authorization and is **not** granted by this packet.

## 5. D4 — Evidence policy **[UNRESOLVED]**

Two independent knobs, frequently conflated:

- **Commitment granularity** (CPU): whether every candidate receives a committed
  quote schedule. Evaluating `Δe(q)` requires only the endpoint difference over
  the affected cells; per-candidate cryptographic commitments are an
  implementation choice, not a requirement of the governing value.
- **Trace granularity** (storage): what is written per tick.

| Option | Can prove | Cannot prove | Storage | CPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E-a** Full per-tick, commit every candidate | Complete re-verification of every considered candidate | — | `R·T·B_row`, largest | Commitments scale with `K` |
| **E-b** Per-decision | Action ticks fully; rested ticks compressed | Rest decisions at equal fidelity | Lower in low-activity regimes | Unchanged unless commitment policy also changes |
| **E-c** Sampled | A registered sample of ticks | Per-tick re-verifiability | Lowest | Unchanged |
| **E-d** Two-tier: full receipts for settled actions + thin per-tick trace | The settled actions and the full `group_quote` / `naive_sum` / `double_count` triple; state, service, unmet per tick | Which non-selected candidates were considered, unless commitments are retained | Middle | Lowest if candidate commitments dropped |

**Constraint imposed by D1 and `m = 2`:** the `group_quote` / `naive_sum` /
`double_count` triple must be recorded at full fidelity on **every**
two-action tick. No sampling scheme may sample away multi-action ticks, or the
central measurement of the selected topology is lost.

Evidence policy is evidentiary, not cosmetic. It must be registered before
execution and never tuned afterwards.

## 7. Study ladder

Four rungs. **Every rung requires separate authorization.** No rung authorizes
the next, and **no smaller study substitutes for the long-run result** — a short
study cannot answer a question about repetition over a declared horizon.

| Rung | Validates | Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — 1–2 tick smoke study** | Process entry, environment, plan-hash enforcement, world construction, one complete decision cycle, artifact publication, and that the `group_quote` / `naive_sum` / `double_count` triple is emitted on a two-action tick | Any scientific claim whatsoever. No homeostasis, recovery, service, reserve or interaction property. Plumbing only |
| **L1 — short integration study** | End-to-end behaviour with positive controls, observational byte-identity, request-shaping identity, and certified instrument sensitivity | Long-horizon behaviour; drift; recovery repeatability |
| **L2 — medium shock/recovery study** | That relaxation is observable within the chosen horizon; that the reserve-binding regime yields a measurable `double_count`; one complete recovery episode | Long-run drift, ratchet, or cumulative reserve erosion |
| **L3 — long-horizon AWS study** | The registered long-run question over the declared horizon | External validity beyond the declared family; O3 settlement; any theorem |

**L0 caution:** a 1–2 tick run is still a model execution. It requires execution
authorization and must be registered as its own study with its own artifacts. It
must never be treated as a rehearsal of a later study, or single-attempt and
no-rerun discipline is compromised.

**L2 is not optional.** It is where the separability behaviour under
reserve-binding either holds or fails, and that determines how the long run's
aggregate accounting must be reported. Running L3 without L2 risks discovering
mid-run that the interaction term is material.
