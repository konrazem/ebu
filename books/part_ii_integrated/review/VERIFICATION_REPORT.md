Status: author-review manuscripts. This is an editorial completion record,
not acceptance of a scientific runtime, peer review, or publication.

## What changed

Former Parts II and III are combined into one current Part II, with main
Chapters 33-62 following Part I's Chapters 1-32. The order puts domain and
units before Gaussian geometry, joint value before attribution, permission
before execution, and the historical engine before its evidence. One contents
list and continuous new folios replace the two abbreviated books.

The new Part II has **285 pages**: **122 newly typeset pages and 163 retained
historical source pages**. Its extracted text contains approximately 80,819
whitespace-delimited words; this is a PDF extraction count, not a linguistic
word count or a completeness proof. Part I has **284 pages**; its final byte
identity is in the accompanying manifest.
Both current volumes exceed the requested 200-page floor at the original
450-by-666-point trim and unchanged body size. No blank-page insertion or
enlarged headings was used to reach that floor.

Part I's front matter and final bridge now lead directly into the merged
volume. The shared teaching style retains smaller headings, worked examples,
navy/teal boxes and the original proportions. A final visual pass removed a
short isolated closing-page fragment rather than keeping it as extra extent.

The Gaussian laboratory now includes shared-source and relay calculations,
subset diagnostics, signed child attributions, missing support, recalibration,
receipt reconstruction, the pump-seal example with explicit limits, eight
proof exercises and ten failure diagnoses. Expanded sections explain vectors,
reference geometry, analytic Onsager-type dynamics and software interfaces.
The earlier force/mobility/flux history and primary Onsager references remain.
