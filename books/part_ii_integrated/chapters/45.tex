\chapter{Feasibility, routes and the long-run boundary}\label{ch:longrun}
An action account can be exact while a world is unable to meet its needs.
A potential can have a unique minimum while a process never reaches it.
These are not paradoxes. They follow from the distinction between a
landscape, a physical domain and a transition rule.

\section{The historical preservation certificate}
P1C addresses a specific regenerative-source export question. In its
historical applicable state and typing, a robust export-rate budget has the
form
\begin{equation}
 Q_{\max}=\frac{[(x-\varepsilon_x)+\Delta t(u-\varepsilon_u)
                      -R_{\mathrm{eff}}]_+}{\Delta t}.
\end{equation}
$R_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is a stock quantity. It is not the old homeostatic
reserve $R$, the Gaussian scale, or the new capacity balance $B$. Under
the certificate's feasibility and uncertainty assumptions, capping aggregate
export protects its stated successor boundary. It does not move material
toward a destination that lacks it.

The source classifier matters as much as this arithmetic. The preserved
P1C design does not grant preservation-safe export from every finite stock
merely because the expression is positive. Its regenerative-source premise
cannot be removed to create a permission law for the closed finite Gaussian
world. The historical reading in this chapter retains the original proof at its own scope.

\section{A route is a sequence of physical transformations}
Take a three-cell teaching state $(18,10,2)$, reference $(10,10,10)$,
and equal scales two. Its potential is sixteen. Move four units from
Ridge to the hub: the endpoint is $(14,14,2)$ and the potential is twelve.
Then move four from the hub to Vale: $(14,10,6)$ has potential four.
The first edge contributes four and the second eight, totaling twelve.

The intermediate hub matters even though its final stock is unchanged.
A direct transfer, if it existed, would have the same field endpoint value
in this lossless example, but need not have the same physical costs, timing
or permissions in a fuller world. One cannot erase an intermediate fuel,
electricity or storage transformation simply because endpoint algebra is
convenient.

\section{Planning is not local valuation}
A planner might ask which sequence of actions reaches a target at minimum
cost. Bellman reasoning compares present choices and future continuations.
That can be a legitimate separately declared problem, but it introduces
state, horizon, objective and information requirements beyond a present local
quote. A random-affordable actor must not secretly call that planner and
still be described as choosing without target-directed optimization.

There is also a difference between a route's total favorable field value
and its affordability at every intermediate point. A useful route can begin
with a negative-valued action. End-to-end positivity does not fund that first
step under zero balances without an explicitly different policy. This is a
possible limitation to report, not a reason to add silent borrowing.

\section{Boundedness is built into the closed domain}
Since $0\leq x_i\leq M$,
\begin{equation}
 V(x)\leq\frac12\sum_i
 \frac{\max\{(x_i^*)^2,(M-x_i^*)^2\}}{\sigma_i^2}<\infty.
\end{equation}
Both an EBU-filtered arm and a physical-random control inherit this bound
if they stay in the same simplex. Showing that one arm's potential remains
finite cannot discriminate their usefulness. A long-run study must ask
about quantities that can genuinely differ: distance occupation, recovery
time, cycling, refusal, distribution or declared service.

\section{Invariance, stability and attraction}
An invariant domain contains every allowed successor. Stability of a
reference means sufficiently small departures remain small under the
specified dynamics. Attraction means departures approach the reference.
Stationarity is a property of a probability law under a specified transition
kernel. These statements answer different questions.

Strict convexity alone proves none of them for an arbitrary actor process.
A finite menu can trap the system away from reference; a capacity policy
can permit cycling; a directed graph can block useful routes. Even if the
physical state has a stationary distribution under continuing forcing, the
audit $J$ and balances can drift. A stationary claim about the augmented
process $(x,B,J)$ needs its own argument.

\section*{An adverse case worth keeping}
Consider two cells with odd integer stocks and only even transfer quantities.
If the reference requires even stocks, every reachable stock remains odd:
adding or subtracting an even number preserves parity. The reference can
be mass-compatible but unreachable under that menu. This exact obstruction
is not a Gaussian calibration result and should not be removed after a
study reveals it. It is a reason to state the menu before testing recovery.

\section*{Chapter recap}
Permission, route reachability and long-run dynamics are separate from exact
valuation. Keeping them separate makes negative results intelligible rather
than embarrassing exceptions to be hidden.
