\chapter{What is the glue?}\label{ch:continuity}

\section{One comparison carried through several languages}
We began with an ordinary concern: how can an account of action say something about the physical conditions that keep life and service possible? The answer developed here is deliberately specific. Declare a state, a fixed potential and a complete physical transition. The before-minus-after difference is its EBU. Every later mathematical layer either represents that same difference or supplies a declared way to construct its endpoints.

The glue is therefore not a metaphor about balance. It is the preservation of one mathematical object across changes of representation. The chain rule connects endpoints to a path integral. Telescoping connects successive transitions. Möbius inversion connects group values to irreducible finite interactions. Smooth response and potential derivatives explain those interactions when their hypotheses hold. A canonical density can express the same values as log ratios, and the accepted thermal benchmark can express them as medium entropy changes.

Each arrow has conditions. Keeping those conditions visible is what makes the continuity rigorous rather than merely persuasive.

\section{The central continuity statement}
\begin{theorem}{Fixed-field EBU continuity hierarchy}\label{thm:central}
For a complete represented transition $x_0\to x_1$ and one finite, fixed potential $V$, the defining account is $E=V(x_0)-V(x_1)$. Under the additional assumptions stated beside each expression below, this same number has the following representations.
\end{theorem}

\begin{lawbox}{Central continuity: preserve the assumptions}
\textbf{D: definition.} One fixed field and complete endpoints:
\[
 E=V(x_0)-V(x_1).
\]
\textbf{P: regular path.} Theorem~\ref{thm:path}:
\[
 E=-\int_\gamma dV=-\int_\gamma\nabla V\cdot dx.
\]
\textbf{Q: canonical density.} Fixed measure, positive endpoints, $p=Z^{-1}e^{-V}$:
\[
 E=\ln[p(x_1)/p(x_0)].
\]
\textbf{T: accepted P4 scope.} Fixed conservative overdamped single-reservoir equilibrium, same temperature and no omitted work or entropy channels:
\[
 E=\Delta s_{\mathrm{med}}/k_B,
 \qquad \Delta s_{\mathrm{sys}}=-k_BE.
\]
\end{lawbox}

The proof consists of the already established chain rule, log-density identity and conditional heat balance. It does not remove any premise when the results are placed together. In particular, D does not imply Q or T. A stock account can satisfy D and P without being a thermal canonical system.

\section{The interaction form of the same continuity}
For a nonempty action set $T$, use one complete coalition table and write $G(z)=V(x(z))$. The exact finite statement is
\begin{lawbox}{Central interaction continuity}
\textbf{B: complete Boolean table.}
\[
 m_E(T)=\Delta_TE(\varnothing)=-\Delta_TG(0).
\]
\textbf{A: additive response and $C^{|T|}$ potential.}
\[
 m_E(T)=-\int_{[0,1]^{|T|}}D^{|T|}V(x(t))[h_i:i\in T],dt.
\]
\textbf{Q: canonical density at every corner.}
\[
 m_E(T)=\sum_{S\subseteq T}(-1)^{|T|-|S|}\ln p(x_S).
\]
\textbf{T: accepted P4 scope for every entry.}
\[
 m_{\Delta s_{\mathrm{med}}}(T)=k_Bm_E(T).
\]
\end{lawbox}

The derivative formula is one specialization of the general composite difference. With nonlinear smooth response, integrate $\partial_T(V\circ x)$ instead and use its partition chain rule. With no smooth extension, the finite table identity still stands. With a restricted feasible poset, use the applicable incidence inversion rather than assume a missing Boolean cube.

\section{What the generator contributes}
The generator interface declares a law of motion, checks well-posedness and supplies $x_S=\Phi_{\chi_S}^\tau(x_0)$. The finite definition then values those endpoints. The interface does not sit between potential and definition as a mandatory foundation; it is one way of specifying what an action does.

This placement prevents two errors. First, a valid finite action account does not need to invent a universal microscopic motion law. Second, a proposed motion law does not acquire empirical credibility merely because its endpoints can be valued exactly. The response and the field each need their own evidence.

\section{What is conserved, and what merely closes}
Physical carrier conservation concerns quantities such as mass or energy within a declared boundary. EBU telescoping concerns differences of a state function. Probability normalization concerns total probability mass. An institutional balance identity concerns a chosen update rule. These are four distinct statements.

A lossless water transfer can conserve litres while lowering a potential. A closed state cycle can have zero total fixed-field EBU while consuming fuel outside a too-small boundary. An exact aggregate attribution can coexist with unfair individual shares. A canonical density can be normalized while its dynamics carry nonzero current. The continuity hierarchy does not collapse these differences.

The benefit is that each failure has somewhere to be located. A missing litre is a physical-account problem; a reused baseline is a comparison problem; a changed scale is a field-version problem; an unjust entitlement is an institutional problem. Keeping those questions distinct makes the whole system more inspectable.

\section{What this does not prove}
The hierarchy does not prove that a chosen $V$ captures every ecological consequence, that all resources share one denomination, or that a potential minimum is an attracting state. It does not prove decentralization, fair ownership, causal entitlement, universal scalability or a successful currency. It does not turn a probability identity into reversibility, or a thermal benchmark into universal entropy accounting.

These are substantive remaining questions. Some need additional theorems, some need physical measurement, and some need explicit public choices. They should not be treated as small implementation details after the equations are finished. A system intended to improve society must confront them directly.

\section{What the continuity does offer}
The sequence provides a disciplined common language for local measurements, joint physical outcomes and their conditional statistical interpretation. Exact cancellation can prevent duplicate claims. Interaction coefficients can expose dependencies that isolated quotes miss. Response models can distinguish a changing mechanism from a changing valuation. Uncertainty can be carried through the same finite contrasts.

If these capabilities are implemented and validated, they could help identify useful maintenance, coordinate complementary infrastructure and reveal when an apparent gain shifts harm outside the boundary. Their social benefit depends on access to those opportunities, protection of essential services and governance of the measurements. The laws supply information and consistency constraints; they do not choose society's aims.

The next chapters therefore return to people and institutions. We will first distinguish the joint physical result from any rule for dividing it, then explain what a trustworthy implementation and a future experiment would have to establish.
