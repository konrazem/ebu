\chapter{The planet inside the account}\label{ch:planet}

\section{A changing inheritance}
It is possible to disagree about economic policy and still begin from measurements. The IPCC concluded in its 2023 synthesis that human activities had unequivocally caused global warming, with the global surface temperature in 2011--2020 about $1.1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ above 1850--1900. It also assessed widespread changes in the atmosphere, ocean, ice and living world, together with unequal contributions and unequal exposure.\cite{intro-ipcc} The number names a period and a comparison baseline; it is not a reading from this morning's thermometer.

The IPBES global assessment estimated that roughly one million animal and plant species were threatened with extinction. \emph{Threatened} does not mean already extinct, and an estimate is not a census of every species. It is nevertheless a serious account of pressure on the living systems on which human activity also depends.\cite{intro-ipbes} The International Resource Panel's 2024 outlook describes rising material use and a conditional path on which global extraction could increase by almost sixty percent from 2020 to 2060 without urgent concerted action.\cite{intro-resources} That is a scenario under stated assumptions, not fate.

These assessments concern different things. Temperature is not biodiversity; extraction is not a local stock of clean water; a global average is not a patient's access to care. No honest account obtains one answer by adding the headline numbers together. Their shared message is that human activity can change the conditions it relies on, and that some changes last far longer than the sale that helped cause them.

\section{The short return and the long consequence}
Consider a company drawing water from a catchment. Its revenue can arrive this month, while damage to groundwater, soils or downstream ecology may develop over years. The company may have legal duties, insurance and public scrutiny; it is not necessarily blind to the future. Still, if the consequence is not observed or assigned to any responsible decision, the near-term return and the long-term condition can diverge.

Three clocks are operating at once. The transaction closes, the physical stock changes, and the supporting system responds or fails to regenerate. We cannot infer the third clock from the first. A forest takes time to regrow, a reservoir takes time to refill, and a damaged species relationship may not be recoverable on a planning horizon. Some resources circulate; others are dispersed or degraded. The physical form of the process matters.

The question for future generations is therefore not whether we can keep every present quantity unchanged. It is whether their world will retain the capacity to sustain lives and choices. A planet is not a machine with one dial called health. But the absence of a universal dial is no excuse to ignore measured stocks, flows, damage and renewal.

\section{A projection is a conditional warning}
The IPCC reports different projected outcomes under different emissions pathways. A pathway is not assigned a probability merely by appearing in a table; it describes a set of assumptions about future activity. The point of examining alternatives is that choices matter. A warning about a damaging future is useful precisely when that future can still be avoided.\cite{intro-ipcc}

The same discipline should be applied to any new economic proposal. A diagram showing a balanced state does not establish that real actions will keep the world there. A model can prove a mathematical identity without proving that its variables cover every important harm. We will take those distinctions seriously, but we need not repeat them at every turn. The positive task is to design accounts that make consequential physical changes visible soon enough to guide action.

\begin{workedbox}{Read the year and the condition}
The UNEP/IRP projection of almost sixty percent more extraction by 2060 refers to a scenario measured against 2020 and conditioned on a continuation without urgent concerted action. It does not say that 2060 extraction has already been observed, or that all resources increase by the same amount.
\end{workedbox}

\section{What is missing from the dashboard?}
Suppose a district reports rising income while its aquifer falls, its clinic loses reliable service and its waste accumulates. The financial report may be true. The physical reports may also be true. What fails is the expectation that one report substitutes for the others. At planetary scale the same error can be harder to see, because harms cross borders and generations.

We need a way to describe what an action does within an explicit physical boundary and how that change bears on continuing function. That does not require calculating the entire planet before opening a valve. In fact, such a calculation is impossible for an ordinary local action. The next question is more practical and more surprising: how can a large world behave consistently when no single participant computes its whole state? Physics offers examples of local relations that produce exact consequences, and those examples will lead us toward the account this book develops.
