\chapter{From laws to a coherent model}\label{ch:experimental-bridge}

The preceding chapters establish a family of connected results. To use them together, we must specify one model whose states, actions, response and potential are mutually compatible. This chapter assembles that theoretical specification and follows a maintained water service through it. It provides the foundation that the later books use for their designs and results.

\section{Begin with the function to be maintained}
The clinic needs usable water when care requires it. That service statement guides the choice of stocks, routes, capacities and time description. It does not yet assign a potential or select a controller. A model begins by identifying which physical conditions determine whether the function is available.

For the simplest instantaneous lesson, two usable stocks and a lossless edge were enough. For a maintained delivery, the source, pending stock, receiver, consumption and relevant losses belong in the physical account. Energy and quality enter when the claim concerns their consequences. The level of representation follows the function being described.

This is the first source of efficiency in the theory: represent what the service requires, and use exact cancellation to avoid recomputing what an action leaves unchanged. Simplification comes after the dependency is understood, rather than by omitting the dependency at the start.

\section{State the domain and the transformations}
Let $\mathcal X$ be the admissible state space. It includes nonnegative stocks, conservation relations and any relevant capacity or service bounds. Let $\mathcal A(x)$ describe the transformations available at state $x$. An action has a complete carrier direction or a more general response rule, with units that match the state.

For a pending route, dispatch and arrival are distinct transformations. The directions $s_D=(-1,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}$ and $s_A=(0,-1,\eta,1-\eta)^{\mathsf T}$ in $(S,Q,X,W)$ preserve the complete carrier. Consumption or external replenishment has its own boundary term.

A group can share source stock or equipment even if its members have individually valid requests. The model therefore states how joint permission is resolved. If a resolver changes accepted quantities, that dependence belongs in the response map used by every coalition row.

The result is a consistent physical language. Each proposal can be interpreted as a possible transformation before its numerical value is considered.

\section{Declare one field for one comparison}
Choose a finite potential $V$ on the states to be compared, with its reference, scale, factorization and units. Under the standardized Gaussian construction, $V$ is dimensionless and its differences are expressed in the declared EBU convention. A native burden $V_B$ retains its own unit until an explicit normalization is supplied.

The field is fixed across one finite receipt and across one coalition table. A later revision is a separate field-change event. This condition is what makes the path identity, telescoping and interaction contrast refer to a single object.

A quadratic is useful because it gives exact finite curvature and a low degree under affine response. A more general field may be appropriate for a declared function, but its increased complexity should represent a physical or functional dependence. Adding a term merely to force a desired score changes the account without explaining the service.

\section{Choose how the endpoint is supplied}
The response may be immediate, $x'=x+\delta$, or generated through time. In the latter case the model states the initial state, background inputs, control family, boundary rules and horizon $T_f$. It supplies a unique admissible endpoint for each required action setting.

The stock-and-pipeline model illustrates a complete choice. The receiver gains $Q/\tau$ and loses consumption $h$; pending stock gains shortage-driven dispatch and loses arrival. Its operating point, memory kernel and roots follow from those equations. Changing dispatch to use pending stock is a new controller, with a new operating point and characteristic equation.

This theoretical completeness is useful even before an institutional rule is attached. It explains what is pending, which timescale governs correction and whether a chosen interval remains inside the physical branch. The endpoint can then enter the same finite account used for an immediate transfer.

\section{Fix the alternatives and the background}
For an action set $N$, each required subset $S$ has an endpoint under the same initial state, horizon, field and background. If the no-action system moves, retain both its raw interval change and the relative action table:
\begin{equation}
 E^{\mathrm{rel}}(S)=V(x_\varnothing(T_f))-V(x_S(T_f)).
\end{equation}
The relative table starts at zero. Its nonempty coefficients agree with those of the raw table because the background difference is constant across rows.

This declaration makes a coalition comparison meaningful. A pair of repairs is compared with the same repairs alone under the same background, not with unrelated starting conditions. Pending stock is part of that background, as are fixed gains and source assumptions.

An infeasible row is a missing physical alternative, not a zero-valued action. One can restrict the motif, declare an extension or use the actual feasible poset. The appropriate inversion follows the structure of the declared alternatives.

\section{Use the strongest exact simplification available}
Once the composite endpoint value is specified, the interaction hierarchy is determined. Möbius inversion reconstructs the table exactly. Additive response and smoothness permit the repeated-FTC representation. Polynomial degree and affine response can impose a finite ceiling.

The maintained linear service is an instructive case. Suppose each action adds an input $g_i$ to one common linear operator $F(t)$. Variation of constants gives $y_S=\bar y+\sum_{i\in S}h_i$ at the horizon. A fixed quadratic potential then requires only singleton and pair terms, regardless of whether $F$ has oscillatory modes.

If actions change $F$, activate saturation or alter a shared resolver, this simplification must be reconsidered. The exact table still exists where its endpoints do, and its coefficients remain $-\Delta_T(V\circ x)$. The general theory therefore survives when the simple ceiling does not.

For an EBU system, this is a practical rule for allocating effort: use proved structure to simplify, and retain the full composite dependence where structure requires it.

\section{Keep timing and value connected but distinct}
A horizon fixes the endpoint comparison. Peak shortage, settling time and time-integrated burden describe the path leading to it. Their units and definitions remain separate. The same endpoint EBU can accompany different interruptions of service.

A controller's storage function provides another description. It can establish decay of deviations while the valued receiver burden rises toward a driven operating point. The two functions answer different questions, so their agreement is not assumed.

This lets a service design pursue several compatible improvements: lower resource burden, less overshoot, fewer duplicate orders and reliable access at the required time. The finite account keeps its exact meaning while the broader physical model describes the function people need.

\section{Add the probability branch only with its structure}
When a canonical physical model supplies $p=Z^{-1}e^{-V}$ in a fixed reference measure, finite EBU becomes a log density ratio and each interaction becomes the corresponding log contrast. Full-support Gaussian assumptions additionally give inverse-Hessian covariance. The specified P4 model adds the medium-entropy scaling.

These are complete theoretical bridges with named hypotheses. The deterministic delivery model does not require them to have a valid endpoint account. Conversely, a stationary density alone does not supply reversibility or a heat balance.

The advantage is a foundation broad enough to describe different physical services while precise about each interpretation. A thermal benchmark and a maintained delivery can share finite accounting without being forced into identical dynamics.

\section{Attach an institutional rule explicitly}
The physical model supplies a group value. An attribution rule divides it. A capacity policy may use the attributed history to affect future choices. Rights, essential access and public investment determine how that policy serves people.

The aggregate balance identity constrains a declared capacity update, but several distributions can satisfy it. A household's limited opportunity to make a repair is a property of its feasible set and resources, not a defect in the finite difference. An institution can use the account to identify where collective support creates a better option.

This separation gives the theory a constructive social role. It can make restoration legible, expose shared burdens and guide investment while allowing the institution to protect service during the transition to more efficient arrangements.

\section{A complete foundation for the later books}
The model now has a physical domain, feasible transformations, a fixed valuation, a response and horizon, a coalition comparator, exact interaction laws and explicit institutional interfaces. Its optional canonical and thermodynamic branches have their own conditions. Its uncertain inputs have a defined propagation rule.

These declarations are the objects to which subsequent designs, simulations and results refer. Book 1 establishes the theory and its meaning. The next chapter returns to the economy as a whole and explains how the pieces can form a useful system around the functions it is meant to keep alive.
