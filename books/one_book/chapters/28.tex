\chapter{What the theory establishes}\label{ch:evidence}

Book 1 has developed a complete theoretical foundation for the declared EBU framework. Its central object is a finite physical comparison, and its later laws explain how that comparison composes through paths, histories, coalitions, dynamics and canonical probability. We can now state the result positively and see why the connections matter.

\section{One definition holds the argument together}
For a fixed potential and complete endpoints,
\begin{equation}
 E=V(x^{\mathrm b})-V(x^{\mathrm a}).
\end{equation}
This finite definition does not depend on an initial-slope approximation or on one chosen motion law. A regular path integrates to it. A sequence of joined transitions telescopes to its net value. A complete cycle under one field has zero total.

These results make an account stable under changes of representation. Dividing a transfer into smaller records does not create extra value. Choosing a convenient evaluation path does not alter the total. Keeping the negative half of a damage--repair history prevents the positive half from being mistaken for net creation.

The practical consequence is a coherent history of physical change. Local work can be recorded, combined and inspected without losing the relation to the whole represented outcome.

\section{Cooperation has an exact mathematical description}
A coalition table specifies the alternatives under a common comparison. Möbius inversion extracts the contribution associated with each subset and reconstructs every group value exactly. Its recursion explains each higher order as a change in a lower-order relationship.

The exact multilinear representation gives the finite analogue of a Taylor hierarchy. Repeated integration connects additive physical increments to continuous mixed derivatives. Polynomial degree imposes a ceiling when the endpoint is affine. A quadratic then needs no terms above pairs.

These statements give shared projects a clear account. They distinguish complementarity from repeated claims on the same improvement, and they show when a compact explanation is complete. A larger group need not receive an unexplained bonus merely because its members act together; its extra contribution has a precise comparator and a precise place in the total.

\section{Time enriches the response without changing the unit}
A generator supplies endpoints at a stated horizon. Pending stock can create memory when it is eliminated from the visible state. A shortage-driven pipeline can have a stable operating point with nonzero burden and can approach it through damped oscillations.

The derived roots describe temporal behaviour. The storage function describes stability. The finite potential difference describes EBU. Their roles remain distinct. Under one common linear operator with additive inputs, endpoint responses superpose and the quadratic pair ceiling survives at every admissible horizon.

Nonlinear response can create higher orders even with a quadratic or linear potential; it can also cancel under composition. The exact object is always the finite difference of $V(x(z))$. This tells a designer where complexity enters instead of equating every oscillation with synergy or every smooth response with independence.

For a service system, the benefit is concrete: pending commitments remain visible, duplicate correction can be reduced, and timing can be evaluated alongside the physical endpoint consequence.

\section{The physical probability bridge has a defined scope}
In the specified canonical normalization, relative surprisal equals the dimensionless potential. Finite EBU is then a log density ratio, and interaction coefficients are the corresponding alternating log contrasts. Under full-support Gaussian assumptions, covariance is the inverse Hessian in the accessible coordinates. Under the accepted P4 conditions, medium entropy contrasts scale by $k_B$.

The assumptions identify exactly where each equality applies. A driven deterministic controller supplies no canonical density by itself, and a stationary density can coexist with probability current. Keeping these distinctions explicit allows the theory to connect to established physics without forcing unlike systems into one interpretation.

This gives EBU both breadth and precision: a common finite language for declared physical changes, with stronger interpretations where the physical model supplies their conditions.

\section{A trustworthy institution can build on the separation}
The theory distinguishes a physical result from its attribution and from a permission to spend. A group total can be exact while several allocation rules remain possible. A capacity invariant can hold while access rules differ. The institution therefore states its choices openly rather than presenting them as consequences of a derivative.

That separation is useful to society. It permits an honest account of resource burdens while protecting essential care, supports shared maintenance without double-counting its result and allows public investment to improve the options available to people with limited resources. The physical account can expose a problem without turning its recipient into the problem.

Versioned fields, complete boundaries and explicit corrections make learning compatible with a durable history. A community can improve its understanding of function while retaining the meaning of work already performed.

\section{Three levels of the completed argument}
The first level is established mathematics. Potential differences, telescoping, finite-difference inversion, linear feedback and canonical logarithms give exact conclusions under their stated hypotheses. An experiment is not needed to make the cancellation in a telescoping sum valid.

The second level is the EBU synthesis: these results are connected into one architecture of complete transitions, response, interaction, registration and persistent history. The book explains the definitions and choices that make that connection coherent. It does not claim that the classical mathematics originated with EBU.

The third level is the empirical and institutional programme. Whether a chosen state is adequate, whether a proposed physical denomination transfers across its intended fields and how people should use the resulting account remain substantive questions. Their study begins with the theory established here. Keeping the three levels visible lets a proved theorem be used confidently without turning it into an unsupported conclusion about an economy.

\section{What belongs to the later books}
The mathematical results established here stand with their stated assumptions. The later books specify physical and institutional applications, measurement and test designs, simulations, implementation checks and results. They use this theoretical foundation rather than asking experiments to prove algebraic identities anew.

The distinction also keeps the present book readable. Its task is to explain what each object means, how each law follows, where the laws connect and which system features they enable. Detailed protocols and outcome records have their own place in the series.

\section{What an economy must keep alive}
An economy succeeds through the conditions it continues to make possible: usable water, energy, repair, food, health, ecological function and access to the arrangements that sustain them. Transactions help coordinate those activities, but their number or price alone does not describe the physical future they leave behind.

EBU makes a precise contribution to that problem. It brings complete physical change into the account of action, connects local work to shared outcomes and preserves the information needed to distinguish restoration from displacement of burden. Its laws can support less redundant calculation, less duplicate correction and more visible maintenance.

Homeostasis is not motionless perfection. A clinic consumes water while it serves patients. Equipment requires repair while it continues to operate. A community adapts its references and infrastructure as its conditions change. The theory developed here is built to describe that active continuity: states change, actions interact, responses take time, and the account remains connected to what the system must keep functioning.

That is the purpose of the complete foundation. It gives the subsequent work a coherent language for designing institutions and physical services whose efficiency helps people meet present needs while preserving the conditions on which future life depends.
