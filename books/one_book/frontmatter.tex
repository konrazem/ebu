\chapter*{Preface}
Every generation inherits a world that makes its activity possible. Money helps people exchange, coordinate and plan within that world. Yet a completed sale cannot, by itself, tell us whether the water line will still work, whether a field will still produce food or whether a community can absorb its next disturbance. Those are physical questions. This book asks what an economy would look like if its account of action answered them as well.

We begin with the reasons money arose and the incentives it creates. From there the argument moves through light, energy, thermodynamics and the living body's continuous work of homeostasis. These comparisons help us distinguish local action from the larger conditions that make it useful. The recurring clinic and water examples keep that question close to ordinary experience.

The Energy Balance Unit enters once the problem is clear. We declare a physical state, a complete change and a potential expressing a functional comparison. Finite EBU is the decrease of that potential between the endpoints. The rest of the book develops the consequences of taking this definition seriously. Its central question stays the same: how can society account coherently for the state-changing consequences of action, including shared effects and delayed response, while keeping physical description and institutional choice clear?

A path integral explains why the marginal changes during a finite action. Telescoping explains how receipts compose into a history. Coalition tables and M\"obius inversion explain how joint work differs from isolated proposals. Discrete Taylor structure, continuous curvature and composite response connect the same comparison at several mathematical levels.

Dynamics then gives the account a time dimension. Water already in transit becomes part of the present state. Feedback can create memory and oscillation, yet a common linear response retains an exact pair-only interaction structure under a quadratic potential. Nonlinear response explains where that simplification ends. Canonical probability and the specified entropy relation provide further physical interpretations under their own assumptions.

At each stage, the law has a practical meaning. It can prevent duplicate claims, reduce unnecessary calculation, make a shared bottleneck visible or explain why another delivery would be premature. These features matter because an economy should make useful maintenance and reliable service easier to support, while preserving access for the people who need them.

Book 1 contains the theoretical foundation needed for that argument. The later books develop test designs, simulations, applications and results. Established mathematics is presented as such, with the assumptions that give it its exact scope. The institutional chapters then explain how the information can be used while keeping rights, attribution and access explicit.

We can begin where people already make their choices: with money, the work it does for us and the physical conditions it cannot describe by itself.

\section*{The reading route}
Chapters 1--13 establish motivation, physical state, reference, potential and local field. Chapters 14--24 connect finite actions to paths, permission, locality, history and joint value. Chapters 25--30 build the interaction hierarchy from coalition tables through exact discrete Taylor structure and degree bounds.

Chapters 31--35 connect that hierarchy to motion, generators, feedback and nonlinear response. Chapters 36--42 develop dependency structure, canonical probability, entropy, changing fields, uncertainty and the central continuity theorem. Chapters 43--49 assemble attribution, routes, institutional rules and a complete theoretical system. Five appendices contain the longer proofs and worked comparisons.

Each chapter opens with its purpose and returns the mathematics to an EBU capability. The detailed contents can be used as a reading map; the symbol guide provides a compact reference. Readers can follow the main argument before returning to the appendix proofs.

This is an author-review manuscript integrating the independently cleared R-stage, S-MG and feedback sources. A separate independent book audit remains required. The edition note records provenance and scientific status in one place.
