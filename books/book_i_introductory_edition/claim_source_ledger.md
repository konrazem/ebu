## Editorial, illustrative and normative claims

The clinic, household, tank, maintenance and all numerical action examples are
invented teaching situations, not observations, study parameters, or forecasts.
Ethical commitments to access, disability support, avoiding preventable harm,
transparency and contestability are stated as commitments. No moral conclusion
is derived from a scalar score. The research gap is a proposal, not a literature
claim of priority. The manuscript claims no novelty or superiority.
