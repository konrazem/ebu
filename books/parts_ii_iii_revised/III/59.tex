\chapter{What a long-run study could show}\label{ch:interpretation}
The future study should be capable of finding that the proposed mechanism
does not help. If every unfavorable outcome is reclassified as an invalid
world or repaired by changing the actor, the programme ceases to test its
own question. Interpretation must therefore be designed before the outcomes
are visible.

\section{A single disturbance and continuing forcing}
The roadmap distinguishes a response after one declared disturbance from
a continuously driven stochastic regime \cite{roadmap}. After one shock,
forcing stops and the external audit remains fixed. The physical potential
and total capacity can exchange subject to the conditional invariant.
The process may recover, cycle, become trapped or refuse all available actions.

Continuing forcing changes the question. Even if physical deviation appears
to stabilize, external audit and accumulated capacity may drift. A claim
of stationarity must name its object: the physical state, potential,
selected observables, balance distribution, or the entire augmented process.
A flat-looking plot of one coordinate does not establish stationarity of all.

\section{Boundedness is not the benefit metric}
Finite conserved mass and positive fixed scales already bound the Gaussian
potential on its domain. The physical-random control has that same bound.
Therefore ``the EBU arm did not diverge to infinity'' is not a discriminating
success claim for this first closed model.

Potential occupancy, time away from reference, recovery after disturbance,
refusal, cycling and distribution are possible families of observables.
They are not frozen metrics here. Their definitions, aggregation, uncertainty
treatment and falsifiers belong to a later preregistration. Service belongs
in the interpretation only if the plant actually defines and measures it.

\section{Physical validity precedes performance}
A trajectory that violates conservation or declared physical constraints
cannot support a performance claim merely because its potential is low.
Similarly, an invalid comparator undermines a comparative claim differently
from a valid comparator that misses its own target.

It is helpful to retain raw facts separately: physical validity, each arm's
measured outcomes, and any oracle or feasibility verdict. Derived labels
should not force unresolved cases into success or failure by default.
If a claim requires an oracle that was not resolved, report that inference
as inconclusive while preserving the observed facts.

\section{No-action is a scientifically meaningful possibility}
At reference with zero genesis, the proposed account blocks nonzero-net
negative-valued departures. After a disturbance, however, menu or ownership
structure may still leave no affordable action. A refusal can therefore be
an intended consequence of the declared rules, a reachability limitation,
or a specification defect. The records must distinguish them.

Adding a free initial balance, refill, loan or target-directed rescue to
avoid refusal would change the experiment. Such alternatives can be studied
later, but must not be inserted after seeing an inconvenient result and
reported as though the original policy succeeded.

\section{From finite evidence to broader claims}
A finite registered study can support statements about its tested regimes
and uncertainty. It does not establish unlimited-horizon safety or economic
validity. A theorem may establish an invariant for all permitted events,
yet leave the quality of the resulting dynamics unresolved. Evidence and
proof can complement one another without substituting for one another.

The strongest report includes adverse and null comparisons, actual exposure,
software violations, missing data and protocol deviations. It distinguishes
what was observed from the causal explanation offered for it. Where the
design cannot separate explanations, the report should say so.

\section*{Chapter recap}
The long-run question remains open because accounting identities constrain
but do not choose behavior. An honest study needs registered observations
that can distinguish mechanisms, not a success label guaranteed by geometry.
