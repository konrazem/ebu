\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Historical Appendix H-III: original evidence chapters}
The following forty-five pages preserve original Part III Chapters 53--59,
original PDF pages 66--110. They contain the original accounts of D1--D10,
Gate 1B, adversarial/service-alignment work and O14. Original equations,
outcomes, qualifications and folios remain; every page gains a historical
margin banner, and chapter titles are displayed smaller. This is a
source-preserving archive, not new experiments.

The old potential contains lower/upper/reserve hinge terms. Its natural
drive, P1C permission and selector choices are model-specific. They have not
been replaced inside these records by Gaussian formulas. Nulls, failures and
limitations are as important to preserve as favorable findings.

Publication-era words such as ``current'' remain historical. Chapter 57
separately identifies the later finalized Gate 1D-C source and its narrow
scope. Original cross-references lead to the preserved original volume;
they are not revised-edition chapter references. Source reproduction is not
a fresh independent reanalysis of every archived study.
