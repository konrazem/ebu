## 1. Executive conclusion

**The current EBU mathematics has physically meaningful realizations in restricted models already supported by established physics. It does not establish a universal physical measure of environmental or economic value.** The clearest existing realization is a correctly specified canonical system with an independently measured energy landscape and temperature. In that setting, the potential drop is an energy difference in local thermal units and, relative to the declared reference measure, a log probability-density ratio. This relationship is standard statistical mechanics, not a new EBU physical law.

A useful physical EBU field needs more than that identity. It needs a physically identified scalar, a reproducible multiplicative scale, an adequate present state, a separately justified action-response law, measurable consequences, and uncertainty bounds on its claimed comparisons. Recursive use requires the retained state to predict the next relevant state **and value**, conditional on the declared action and external inputs. Combining fields requires a physical joint model, including interactions and reservoirs once. Common numerical units alone do not establish that model.

There are definite conditional obstructions. A scalar on a state space cannot reproduce nonzero circulation around a closed state loop. A reduced endpoint potential cannot represent all dissipated work or entropy production in cycles returning that reduced state to its initial value. Two thermal normalizations at different temperatures cannot simultaneously be one fixed joule conversion for arbitrary independent energy changes. Hidden memory can defeat a proposed state; unidentifiable scales can defeat calibration. None of these proves that every enlarged or restricted EBU field is impossible.

**The bounded literature comparison identifies no incremental physical prediction, beyond established physics plus the declared model assumptions, in the present canonical root claim.** Known experiments already examine closely related energy–fluctuation, probability–energy, work–free-energy and information–heat relationships. Repeating one can be useful if it qualifies EBU's own independent calibration chain or enables a subsequent prospective field test. Recomputing a potential from a distribution and recovering that same distribution adds no independent evidence.

The plausible near-term scientific scope is controlled equilibrium models and explicitly bounded storage or resource subsystems. Engineered networks require further evidence on composition and memory. Broad ecological valuation and a general economic denomination remain unresolved. Physics alone supplies neither ownership nor fairness, welfare or allocation rules. The previously refused W1 complete-action candidate remains refused for its requested signs; the possibility of a scoped water factor is not a certified field. The refused optical realization likewise does not establish universal physical infeasibility or a T/U contradiction.

## 3. Definition and physical requirements of a root

### 3.1 A denomination needs a measurand

F defines a dimensionless finite value

\[
E=V(z_{\rm pre})-V(z_{\rm post}).
\]

That defines arithmetic after a potential is supplied. It does not identify the potential's physical origin. A physical root must specify the **kind of quantity**, its orientation, unit/normalization, reference context, admissible comparisons and independent realization. A quantity can be dimensionless and still have a specific physical meaning. Two dimensionless ratios need not be interchangeable.

Possible contracts include an energy change divided by a specified thermal scale, an independently derived entropy contrast divided by an identified constant, or an independently justified free-energy/availability contrast. They are alternatives requiring physical bridges, not synonyms. A family of realizations can share one denomination when each realizes the same declared kind and their constitutive bridges are physically established. There is no need for one material apparatus everywhere; there is a need for one defensible meaning on the comparisons being combined. JCGM's distinction between traceability and comparability is directly relevant. [L02]

A chosen unit always includes a convention. Scientific objectivity does not mean eliminating conventions such as the joule or the natural logarithm. It means that, **after the quantity and unit are declared**, independent laboratories cannot freely change the multiplier to fit desired outcomes. A root contract without a specified kind is incomplete; a reproducible unit conversion is not itself a new law.

### 3.2 Scale, gauge and calibration

For a fixed comparison context,

\[
V'=cV+d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E'=cE.
\]

A constant offset is harmless for differences. An unknown positive multiplier preserves ordering but leaves quantitative values undetermined; a negative multiplier also reverses physical orientation. Measuring only a flat potential cannot identify its scale. Nonconstant contrasts or another independently calibrated reference are needed.

For the thermal root, independently realized energy and thermodynamic temperature, together with the SI value of Boltzmann's constant, fix the multiplier. Since 2019, the SI fixes \(k_B=1.380649\times10^{-23}\ {\rm J\,K^{-1}}\) exactly. Temperature and energy measurements still have uncertainty. Using a thermometer based on the very fluctuation law being tested requires identifying that dependence; an exact defining constant is not a universal escape from circular experimental calibration. [L03]

If calibration maps between representations of the same scalar are positive affine, their composed slope around a closed calibration loop must be one; their composed offset must vanish when common **levels** are compared. For contrast-only comparisons, constant offsets do not matter. These are compatibility consequences, not proof that unrelated quantities share a physical scalar. SFE Theorem 10 and M U3 already supply this distinction.

If \(d=d(\theta)\) varies with reservoir or field context, it cancels within one fixed field but not across different contexts. Appending a context label to a state does not establish a physical cross-context potential. Such changes require a justified extended potential and reference alignment.

### 3.3 What the same number does and does not mean

| Comparison | What must be established |
|---|---|
| Same physical denomination | Same declared kind and compatible calibrated realizations |
| Same joules | A specified energy conversion; thermal units at different temperatures generally differ in joules |
| Same finite action value | Same calibrated potential drop for the declared complete event, not just similar action labels |
| Same statistical significance | A specified test, uncertainty, sampling and alternative; equal E alone is insufficient |
| Same environmental consequence | A causal environmental model including affected quantities and context |
| Same human benefit or welfare | Additional evaluative and institutional assumptions; not supplied by thermal physics |

The useful restricted root claim is reproducibility of a physical contrast. It should not be inflated into a claim that physical systems, environments or people are interchangeable.

## 4. Existing canonical-equilibrium root and its limitations

### 4.1 The established relation

At fixed context \(\theta\), let \(T_\theta>0\), let \(U_\theta\) be the appropriate energy for the declared configurational measure \(\nu_\theta\), and suppose the physically justified canonical law is

\[
p_\theta=\frac{dP_\theta}{d\nu_\theta}
=Z_\theta^{-1}\exp[-U_\theta/(k_BT_\theta)],\qquad 0<Z_\theta<\infty.
\]

Choose a fixed reference energy \(U^*_\theta\) and define

\[
V_\theta=\frac{U_\theta-U^*_\theta}{k_BT_\theta},\qquad
J_\theta=-\log p_\theta=V_\theta+C_\theta.
\]

Therefore \(\beta_{\rm bridge}=1\), whereas the inverse thermodynamic temperature is \(\beta_{\rm th}=1/(k_BT)\), with different units and meaning. At positive finite endpoint densities,

\[
E_\theta=\frac{U_{\rm pre}-U_{\rm post}}{k_BT_\theta}
=\log\frac{p_\theta(z_{\rm post})}{p_\theta(z_{\rm pre})}.
\]

This is the standard canonical construction, consolidated by R and S; Gibbs is an early primary source. It is not a new derivation claimed by this report. One unit is one local thermal-energy unit of the specified energy drop and one nat of the specified log-density ratio. A density at a continuous point is not a point-event probability. [L01]

Canonical systems at different temperatures can share this **thermal-normalized meaning**. They do not thereby share a fixed joule value, environmental effect or statistical detectability. The reference measure and physical coordinate interpretation must accompany the number.

### 4.2 Separate premise layers

1. **Density layer:** correct ensemble, energy/measure pair, support, positive temperature, finite normalizer and fixed context. Gaussianity is unnecessary.
2. **Equilibrium dynamics layer:** suitable invariant dynamics and appropriate time reversal/detailed balance. A canonical-looking stationary density alone does not establish reversibility.
3. **Sampling layer:** observations represent the intended distribution; temporal dependence, exposure, noise and drift have justified treatment. Ergodicity concerns inference from a record, not the logarithm identity itself.
4. **Entropy/heat layer:** the stronger P4 assumptions below. Neither density agreement nor a fitted slope imports them automatically.
5. **Independent test layer:** energy and temperature must not be inferred by imposing the same density relationship being compared. T/U make this separation explicit.

These are sufficient premises for the scoped benchmark, not a universal necessity theorem about every distribution that happens to have exponential form.

### 4.3 Measure, degeneracy and coarse-graining

If a density against a chosen flat coordinate measure has a density-of-states factor \(g(x)\), then

\[
p(x)\propto g(x)e^{-U(x)/(k_BT)},\qquad
J(x)=U(x)/(k_BT)-\log g(x)+C.
\]

Ignoring a nonconstant \(g\) breaks the bare-energy bridge. A coordinate change requires transforming the reference measure consistently. Recomputing a flat-coordinate density in the new coordinates can add a Jacobian term; that is not merely rewriting the same scalar gradient.

For hidden coordinates \(y\), the equilibrium marginal is represented by a potential of mean force,

\[
W(x)=-\log\int e^{-V(x,y)}\,\nu(dy\mid x).
\]

Its entropic and coupling terms generally differ from the bare energy at one detailed state. A reduced density can be correct while a detailed endpoint energy drop or a reduced Markov dynamics is wrong. M U6 gives the exact contrast residual discussed in §7. At strong system–bath coupling, the Hamiltonian of mean force is an established construction; it does not justify treating a reduced bare Hamiltonian as the complete thermodynamic energy. [L04]

For a global positive-definite quadratic on full affine support with the appropriate flat measure, \(\Sigma^{-1}=H\), where H is the Hessian of the dimensionless V and \(\Sigma\) is the covariance in the accessible coordinates. Truncation, constraints or unaccounted hidden-state terms can destroy this covariance identity even where a local curvature statement survives. A Gaussian model is a useful special case, not the physical definition of EBU.

### 4.4 The restricted entropy and work interpretations

Under S's static conservative overdamped single-bath P4 model, with fixed temperature and field, stationary canonical endpoint ensemble, proper stochastic heat convention and **no omitted driving work**,

\[
Q_{\rm bath}=-\Delta U=k_BT E,\qquad
\Delta s_{\rm med}=k_BE,\quad
\Delta s_{\rm sys}=-k_BE,\quad
\Delta s_{\rm tot}=0.
\]

These are different quantities, with different signs and roles. A registered action that externally drives the system is not automatically a no-work P4 transition. In a fixed mechanical conservative potential, the work **by that conservative force** equals \(k_BT E\); external work, total work and heat need separate balances. The trajectory distinction is standard stochastic thermodynamics. [L05]

The foundation's affine entropy ansatz \(S=S_{\rm eq}-\kappa V\) is not a universal physical derivation; F leaves the meaning and universality of \(\kappa\) open. S's constrained-macrostate/finite-reservoir result also has separate assumptions and a finite-bath remainder. It must not be silently identified with stochastic system entropy or total entropy production.

### 4.5 Extensions supported by existing physics, not adopted here

| Construction | Restricted physical basis | What it could supply | Additional boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finite reservoir | Reservoir entropy determines subsystem statistical weight; the constant-temperature exponential is a large-reservoir approximation | A justified joint entropy/energy description | Reservoir state and coupling must be retained; a fixed \(k_BT\) need not remain valid |
| Nonequilibrium free energy | At fixed bath temperature and declared measure, \(\mathcal F[p]=\langle U\rangle_p-TS[p]\); \(\mathcal F[p]-F_{\rm eq}=k_BT D(p\Vert p_{\rm eq})\) | A scalar on a **distribution state**, potentially predictive with specified dynamics | Not generally a scalar of one observed configuration; no automatic identification with trajectory entropy production [L06] |
| Work fluctuation relations | Jarzynski/Crooks connect work statistics and equilibrium free-energy differences under their preparation/dynamical assumptions | Existing benchmark relations for driven processes | They do not say that each realization's work is its endpoint free-energy change [L07], [L08] |

For the nonequilibrium free-energy row, \(S[p]=-k_B\int p\log p\,d\nu\) is the Gibbs–Shannon entropy of the declared classical representation, and \(D\) is Kullback–Leibler divergence against its canonical equilibrium law. The same fixed energy function and reference measure are used throughout; the energy, entropy and divergence must be finite for the displayed finite-valued identity. This specifies the statistical quantity rather than identifying it with every thermodynamic entropy used elsewhere. [L06]

If one proposed \(V=B/(k_BT_0)\) for exergy \(B\), the frozen drop convention would give positive E for exergy depletion. This is an implication of that proposed identification, not a verdict that depletion benefits society. A source meaning, compatible root bridge and joint physical account would still be required. No such extension is adopted by this report.

### 4.6 Other ensembles and noncanonical observations

Pressure and chemical-reservoir conditions change the physical ensemble, not merely a label on the same formula. For an isothermal–isobaric ensemble at fixed particle number, the energy weight contains \(U+p\mathscr V\), where \(\mathscr V\) is physical volume; its reference measure and normalizer must also be specified. In a grand canonical ensemble at fixed temperature, volume and reservoir chemical potentials, the weight contains \(U-\sum_i\mu_i^{\rm chem}N_i\). Here chemical potentials are not EBU's differential components. These established ensemble constructions do not authorize freely replacing the canonical energy in an already frozen EBU comparison. A new scoped bridge and boundary interpretation would be required. [L01]

If a separately observed distribution is not the canonical distribution predicted from the independently calibrated U, the canonical bridge can fail in that domain. One must first distinguish nonstationarity, measurement/model error, wrong energy or measure, and genuine failure of the qualified model. Defining a new statistical scalar \(V_{\rm stat}=-\log p\) always recovers the logarithm identity on positive support, but does not establish an independently measured energy potential, reversibility or a universal thermodynamic extension. Thermodynamic free energy is an ensemble state quantity; it is not generally the energy of one sampled configuration.

## 5. Conditions for a common cross-field denomination

A composition certificate has three separate jobs: identify the same kind of quantity, fix its scale, and justify its assembly into one joint potential. Success at one does not imply the others.

For weakly coupled classical subsystems in a common canonical context, a physically identified total energy

\[
U_{AB}=U_A+U_B+U_{\rm int}
\]

supplies \(V_{AB}=(U_{AB}-U^*_{AB})/(k_BT)\). The interaction belongs once in the complete account. Independent subsystems give additive potentials; interacting subsystems generally require a coupled term. Overlapping boundaries or a common finite bath cannot be counted independently by each participant and then summed as if disjoint. This is the physical content required beneath SFE/M's factor algebra.

When two systems are maintained independently at fixed \(T_A\ne T_B\), a product canonical law can legitimately have

\[
V=U_A/(k_BT_A)+U_B/(k_BT_B)+C.
\]

Thus different temperatures do **not** by themselves rule out a joint dimensionless scalar. They do prevent identifying it with one fixed multiple of total energy for arbitrary independent energy changes (§9.3). Once heat exchange, driving or shared finite reservoirs are introduced, the product law and fixed temperatures require rejustification.

For classical joint distributions relative to a consistent product measure,

\[
S_{AB}=S_A+S_B-k_B I(A;B).
\]

Ignoring correlations can therefore invalidate an entropy sum even when separately measured marginal entropies are correct. Dynamical subsystem entropy balances can require information-flow terms, as established for specified bipartite Markov systems; that restricted result is not a certificate for arbitrary networks. [L11]

A field-extension bridge must declare which actual comparisons it preserves: potential levels, endpoint contrasts, probability ratios, source diagnostics or response predictions. A constant calibration loop is insufficient if the proposed factor omits a state-dependent reservoir contribution. A calibration map that depends on the action or hidden context may be a constitutive model, not a unit conversion.

For EBU, the practical benefit of a successful composition certificate would be reproducible totals and an explanation of interaction effects without issuing the same contribution twice. It would not prove a resource, social or economic benefit from making the total large.

## 6. Necessary conditions for an admissible physical field

The minimal finite mathematics needs finite single-valued values at the compared endpoints and a declared event. Smoothness, a Gaussian distribution, a finite-dimensional state and deterministic dynamics are **not necessary** for that finite subtraction. Physical admission is a stronger scientific claim.

A field description must specify the boundary and exchanged carriers; the relevant state and context; a physically identified joint potential and root bridge; the permitted action and passive response maps or kernels; accessible observations; and the domain/horizon on which all these are adequate. “Complete” means adequate for the claimed outputs, not necessarily a microscopic enumeration of the universe.

For a differentiable field and action extent \(q\), the coordinate components \(\mu_j=\partial V/\partial z_j\) are components of the differential, and \(s_a=\partial z/\partial q\) is the action direction. Then

\[
f_a=-dV(s_a),\qquad f=-S^{\mathsf T}\mu.
\]

This covector–direction pairing is coordinate invariant when the same physical scalar and action are transformed consistently. The raw magnitude of a gradient in arbitrarily rescaled coordinates is not a common action value. It is not a mechanical force unless an additional physical conversion establishes that identification. UNI already resolves this issue.

For a single-valued \(C^1\) potential on a neighborhood of an admitted piecewise smooth path,

\[
E=-\int_\gamma dV.
\]

These are convenient sufficient classical regularity conditions. An already supplied global potential does not additionally require simply connected support. Conversely, reconstructing a global potential from measured marginals requires an integrability test (§9.1). If context changes, the total differential includes context derivatives; integrating only the state coordinates omits physical terms.

A fixed quadratic gives exactly

\[
E=-\nabla V(z)^{\mathsf T}\Delta z-\tfrac12\Delta z^{\mathsf T}H\Delta z.
\]

Freezing the local slope drops curvature. For a nonlinear action map, higher action interactions may arise even if V is quadratic. MG's pair-only result also requires an affine endpoint map in action indicators. Coalition and source decompositions diagnose one complete finite action value; they do not supply additional physical settlements or new conservation laws.

## 7. Sufficient state, action response and joint potential

### 7.1 Deterministic and stochastic sufficiency

For a complete modeled state \(z\), retain \(\theta=\pi z\). Exact deterministic recursion requires that states on the same fiber have the same available actions and, under the same conditioned inputs, the same projected action/passive updates and all claimed action values. Potential descent \(V=v\circ\pi+c\) is sufficient for value descent, but stronger than necessary for a restricted comparison family. A reward can be fiber-constant without being the difference of a reduced potential: M U5 supplies an explicit reduced self-loop with nonzero reward.

For stochastic systems, the condition is equality of the **joint conditional law of claimed reward and retained next state**, with intermediate states and passive outputs included when claimed. Equal conditional mean reward alone is insufficient. Equal separate marginals need not preserve the dependence needed for subsequent predictions. The claim is equality of laws, not identical random outcomes from matched states.

If histories \(h_1,h_2\) produce the same admitted current state and conditioned inputs, a persistent difference in the next output law contradicts that state's sufficiency. This is a physical hypothesis about a representation. It is neither a universal axiom that real systems must forget nor a requirement to replay historical valuations. Stochastic state aggregation/bisimulation supplies close mathematical prior art, with its own specified transition/reward setting. [L12]

### 7.2 Static elimination is not dynamic closure

For the marginal W in §4, put \(R(z)=V(z)-W(\pi z)\). M U6 establishes

\[
V(z)-V(z')=W(\pi z)-W(\pi z')+R(z)-R(z').
\]

The reduced and complete endpoint drops agree on a comparison family exactly when its residual contrast vanishes on those comparisons. For example, \(V(x,y)=(x^2+y^2)/2\) has a valid Gaussian x marginal, yet the detailed transition \((0,0)\to(0,1)\) has E = −1/2 while its x-only drop is zero. Holding y fixed gives a different, potentially compatible comparison family.

A correct static marginal also supplies no Markov closure. Eliminating variables can introduce memory kernels and correlated fluctuations; Mori's projection theory is a direct antecedent. [L13] U §5.7 already records the practical E1a consequence: a lateral Gaussian density after eliminating an axial coordinate need not have the retained two-dimensional exponential covariance kernel. Fast hidden relaxation is not, by itself, an error bound.

### 7.3 Responses and prediction

A potential does not choose the action map. Mechanical, electrical, chemical or other response laws must be supplied independently, including action duration, controls, disturbances, losses, boundary exchange and availability. For deterministic response A, a prospective claim is

\[
E_{\rm pred}(z,a)=V(z)-V(A_a(z)).
\]

For a stochastic response K, predict the distribution of \(V(z)-V(Z')\), or a named functional with its uncertainty. Calling only its expectation “the realized value” would change the claim. The registered realized value still uses actual admitted endpoints.

Parameter fitting to earlier observations can be legitimate. Testing requires predictions on independently reserved **future** comparisons, with no outcome-based adjustment of scale, preprocessing or action meaning. This report inspected no held-out programme observations. Established system identification already studies the separation between model structure, input/output information and validation. [L14]

### 7.4 Factor identity and complete valuation

If independently justified factors satisfy \(V=b+\sum_\alpha\phi_\alpha\), their endpoint contrasts sum to E. Overlap of variables is allowed; duplication of physical contributions is not. Replacing \(\phi_1\) by \(\phi_1+g\) and \(\phi_2\) by \(\phi_2-g\) leaves V unchanged but can change source contrasts. Source identity needs independent physical constraints, or an explicit identification set. A harmless source ambiguity is one invariant on the relevant action comparisons, not every possible refactorization.

SFE C1–C11 is a sufficiently strong source-embedding certificate, not a proof that all its implementation choices are mathematically necessary. A claimed sign adds an independently specified physical label and uncertainty-qualified inequality. It does not choose a new E. The distinction prevents a favorable source diagnostic from replacing an unfavorable total.

## 8. Conservation, dissipation and irreversibility

Use the convention \(\Delta U=W_{\rm on}+Q_{\rm in}\) for a closed material subsystem; open-system balances also include the relevant transported energy and other ports. Energy, charge and the appropriate material inventories retain their own physical balances. Chemical species can have reaction terms, while suitable elemental combinations may be conserved. At a reactor-related boundary, the energy/material representation must include transformations appropriate to that regime; separate conservation of every species or rest-mass inventory is not presumed.

EBU's telescoping relation is a different statement. With registered endpoint \(\widetilde z_n\), passive endpoint \(z_{n+1}\), and one fixed or validly extended V,

\[
E_n=V(z_n)-V(\widetilde z_n),\quad
D_n=V(\widetilde z_n)-V(z_{n+1}),\quad
\sum_n(E_n+D_n)=V(z_0)-V(z_N).
\]

If a ledger C changes by E alone, then \(\Delta(C+V)=-D\). Adding a passive audit accumulator can close an accounting invariant, as M U13 proves. It does not turn C into entropy, energy, a physical carrier or a conserved resource. Passive recovery cannot automatically be credited as an actor action; historical entries are not recomputed from a later potential.

Dissipation is compatible with an endpoint potential if the potential is honestly identified. The problem arises when one requires that endpoint drop to equal all dissipated work, total entropy production, or human benefit without the necessary physical bridge. Established storage-function and port-based system theories explicitly distinguish stored quantities, supply and dissipation. A storage inequality is not EBU's drop equality and does not automatically select a unique physical scalar. [L15], [L16]

For open chemical networks, reservoirs, reaction affinities and exchange terms have established thermodynamic treatments under specified kinetics. Their existence supports restricted modeling possibilities, not automatic admission of arbitrary industrial or ecological networks. [L17]

W1 is a concrete warning about interpretation: its selected complete water–drive–bath potential gives \(E=c_b\log(1+L/u)>0\) for both lossy withdrawal and lossy refill when \(L>0\). Reversible transfer gives zero in that model. These consequences refute the requested complete-action sign claim for that candidate. They do not invalidate the subtraction law, prove all water models impossible, or certify the proposed scoped water factor.

## 9. Conditional obstructions and impossibility statements

The following deductions are **non-controlling and unaudited**. Where R, S, RF, SFE or M already establish the result, it is consolidated with its premises rather than presented as a novel theorem. “State extension may help” never means “a feasible measured extension has been proved.”

### 9.1 Integrability and topology

**Premises:** a continuous proposed marginal one-form \(\omega\) on a connected open comparison domain, with all piecewise smooth comparison paths available. A global differentiable scalar with \(\omega=dV\) exists exactly when every closed-path integral vanishes. Necessity follows by endpoint cancellation. For sufficiency, define V by an integral from a fixed reference; vanishing closed integrals makes it path independent and continuity gives the differential locally. This is R §4/M U4's distinction between supplying V and reconstructing it.

For a \(C^1\) one-form, symmetric cross-derivatives are locally necessary and sufficient on sufficiently small balls. Globally, the punctured-plane form

\[
\omega=\frac{-y\,dx+x\,dy}{x^2+y^2}
\]

has zero curl away from the origin but integral \(2\pi\) around the unit circle. A global single-valued potential therefore fails on that domain. Restricting to a branch domain or retaining a winding coordinate changes the representation and can remove this example's obstruction. It does not establish an independently calibrated EBU scalar. Empirical loop residuals must be compared with uncertainty before declaring a physical violation.

### 9.2 Cyclic dissipation cannot be an endpoint drop on a returning state

**Premises:** the represented state returns exactly to its initial value, V is single-valued there, and a proposed identification requires E to equal a nonzero multiple of total dissipated work or entropy production for every such cycle. E must be zero; a cycle with positive dissipation contradicts the identification. A frictional working subsystem operated through a cycle while transferring energy to its surroundings is a physical class with this property. The surroundings do not return merely because the working coordinate does.

This obstructs the **combined premises**, not the existence of any endpoint scalar. Including reservoir states or an accumulated transfer variable removes the assumed closed loop and can represent some previously hidden quantity. Merely appending the number being explained as a new state is not independent physical validation. Whether a finite, observable, recursively adequate extension exists is a separate question.

### 9.3 Two thermal scales versus one joule conversion

**Premises:** systems A and B have positive, fixed, unequal temperatures; their independent energy drops can vary; each keeps its canonical normalization; and a proposed joint account also requires one constant \(\lambda\) such that

\[
\frac{\delta U_A}{k_BT_A}+\frac{\delta U_B}{k_BT_B}
=\lambda(\delta U_A+\delta U_B)
\]

for every pair of drops. Setting one drop to zero in turn forces \(\lambda=1/(k_BT_A)=1/(k_BT_B)\), a contradiction. Here \(\delta U\) means pre-minus-post.

The obstruction is to **simultaneous preservation of local thermal normalization and one temperature-independent joule factor**. It does not contradict the legitimate product-density scalar in §5, a properly modeled entropy account, or a separately proposed common-reference exergy construction. Enlarging the state can model temperature changes but does not make these incompatible fixed-scale requirements identical.

### 9.4 A stationary canonical density need not be reversible

R/S's dimensionless diffusion example has \(V=(x^2+y^2)/2\), \(p\propto e^{-V}\), drift \(b=(-x-y,-y+x)\), and diffusion operator the Laplacian. Then

\[
j=bp-\nabla p=(-y,x)p,\quad \nabla\!\cdot j=0,\quad j\ne0.
\]

The density is stationary but carries a circulating current. Thus density/scale agreement alone cannot certify reversible overdamped equilibrium or the P4 entropy interpretation. This is an analytic counterexample, not a simulated EBU system. Measuring appropriate temporal currents can distinguish the models; simply collecting more samples of the same stationary density cannot.

### 9.5 Hidden-state collision

**Premises:** an underdamped physical system is represented only by position q, although velocity affects its next response. Two histories with the same q and opposite velocities generally give different next positions under the same force and elapsed time. A deterministic q-only update fails. Under stochastic preparation, different conditional velocity distributions can similarly give different next-state laws.

Including velocity repairs this particular idealized collision. Fluid, material or reservoir memory may require further variables or a history function. Failure of one finite representation does not prove that no sufficient representation exists. Conversely, formal existence of an infinite state does not prove usable observation or calibration. Mori's theory and observed hydrodynamic memory support treating this as a real physical issue, not solely bookkeeping. [L13], [L18]

### 9.6 Dynamical fit need not identify the valuation scale

**Premises:** only a deterministic trajectory law \(\dot z=-M\nabla V\) is observed, and M has no independently fixed physical scale. For any \(c>0\), \(V'=cV\), \(M'=M/c\) gives the same law while multiplying every E by c. Thus response fitting alone cannot fix the denomination in this model class.

An independently calibrated force, mobility, energy or other constitutive standard can remove this ambiguity. Additional stochastic observations can also help **if** their noise/temperature relation is independently justified. The example does not show nonidentifiability for all calibrated stochastic models. More generally, observationally equivalent candidate models that disagree on target action contrasts defeat unique valuation; additive gauges and action-invariant differences do not.

### 9.7 Arbitrary scalar weighting does not follow from conservation

Suppose two independently meaningful variables a and b have no supplied physical conversion law. After choosing numerical representations, suppose the candidate aggregations \(V_\lambda=a+\lambda b\), \(\lambda>0\), leave all specified carrier balances and response observations unchanged. For an action with nonzero change in b, these models predict different E. The given evidence therefore fails to identify \(\lambda\).

This is an underdetermination result, not a theorem that every environmental scalar is impossible. A constitutive law, common physical potential or explicit normative objective could fix a weighting. The latter would be an accounting/evaluative convention and must be identified as such. Dimensional normalization, optimization and fitting desired signs do not by themselves supply the missing physical law.

### 9.8 Conserved total energy can make a chosen potential uninformative

If a complete isolated system conserves \(U_{\rm total}\), and one chooses \(V=U_{\rm total}/q_R+c\) with fixed \(q_R\), every allowed complete transition has E = 0. That follows by substitution. It defeats any simultaneous requirement that this particular V produce a nonzero value for those transitions. It does not rule out physically justified subsystem, entropy or free-energy comparisons with different states, boundaries and meanings.

## 10. Measurement, identifiability and practical limits

A sensor reading is not automatically a sufficient physical state. Structural observability asks whether different states can be distinguished by allowed inputs and outputs within a model. Identifiability concerns competing parameters or models. Practical inference additionally depends on noise, excitation, bandwidth, conditioning and duration. Standard nonlinear observability theory supplies conditional criteria; one convenient rank test should not be mislabeled a universal global necessity. [L19]

Admission need not recover every microscopic coordinate. It must identify the quantities needed for the claimed value and future output law, or bound their ambiguity. A set of indistinguishable models is acceptable for a bounded value claim only if its value/response spread stays inside a declared tolerance over the relevant domain and horizon. Naming a posterior distribution does not independently establish its coverage or remove model inadequacy.

An endpoint potential approximation with \(|V-\widehat V|\le\varepsilon_V\) at both admitted endpoints gives \(|E-\widehat E|\le2\varepsilon_V\) for exact endpoint states. Measurement and response errors add through separately justified sensitivity or other bounds. M U5b shows how recursive defects propagate; small local errors do not imply indefinite accuracy. A state-dependent residual cannot be renamed harmless noise without a model.

Calibration errors shared across fields remain shared. Repetition can reduce some random errors but not a common scale ambiguity. Distinguish stochastic parameter uncertainty, corrected bias with uncertainty, deterministic model residuals, finite-sample uncertainty and numerical error. Nonlinear propagation must not count a stochastic Taylor remainder a second time as an unrelated physical bias. JCGM guidance supports an explicit measurement model and dependencies, not any universal EBU error threshold. [L20]

### 10.2 Theoretical existence versus observable feasibility

A complete state may formally exist while no practicable sensing scheme identifies it at the claimed resolution or horizon. Conversely, a coarse state may be adequate for a restricted action family without being sufficient for all possible interventions. Saturation, inaccessible reservoirs, irreversible material change, long memory and unbounded tails can each restrict admission. The defensible result is therefore a domain-, action- and horizon-specific certificate, possibly a refusal, not a declaration that finite-dimensional physics is mandatory everywhere.

### 12.1 Review method and boundaries

Classification meanings:

- **A — ALREADY ESTABLISHED SCIENCE.** The relevant mathematical/physical relationship is established under specified assumptions.
- **B — REPLICATION OR METROLOGICAL QUALIFICATION OF AN ESTABLISHED RESULT.** Useful new evidence could qualify a particular realization without discovering a new law.
- **C — POTENTIALLY NOVEL INTEGRATION OF EXISTING SCIENTIFIC RESULTS.** A precise combination may be useful; this label does not establish priority, empirical advantage or novelty of its components.
- **D — POTENTIALLY NEW FALSIFIABLE PHYSICAL CLAIM.** Requires an explicit prediction not already implied by the cited established model and a discriminating test.
- **E — NOVELTY OR PHYSICAL VALIDITY UNDETERMINED.** The physical bridge, prediction or literature priority remains unresolved.

**No current root assertion is assigned D on this review's evidence.** Proposed field sufficiency, physical embedding and cross-domain compatibility are falsifiable model hypotheses, but falsifiability by itself does not make them new physics. To obtain a D candidate, the programme would have to specify a genuinely additional, distinct prediction and its scope. This report neither invents one nor changes the foundation to obtain novelty.

### 12.4 Novelty and replication verdict

The basic root relation, endpoint calculus, thermodynamic balances, finite-set inversion and conditional state reduction are established science or direct applications. The EBU-specific object is presently a **formal integration proposal**: root-calibrated physical fields, complete action responses, diagnostic decompositions, recursive state updates, passive accounting and preserved historical actor entries.

That integration can be useful without a new law. Its possible novelty is **C, with priority undetermined**, and its broad physical reach is **E**. The reviewed exergy, thermoeconomics, emergy, storage/control and system-identification precedents prevent claiming that physical accounting, energy-based valuation or recursive modeling first appear in EBU. No protocol-level experimental originality is established by this report.

A replication has incremental scientific value when it quantifies the accuracy and independence of a new calibration chain, challenges a specified model range, resolves an identified systematic effect, or establishes prerequisites for a distinct subsequent field test. A relabeled textbook identity or self-calibrated agreement alone supplies none of those. Existing publications should be reused as scientific references and protocol benchmarks; their numerical performance and external success cannot be imported as an EBU admission result.

## 14. Minimum falsifiable evidence for physical validation

### 14.1 Four separate claims and their dependencies

| Claim | Independent physical content | Dependency and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| A. Root denomination is physically recoverable | Independent energy/other quantity calibration predicts a separately observed physical contrast with the specified scale. | Can be tested before a general recursive field. A pass is scoped metrological/model evidence, not discovery of the canonical law. |
| B. One field predicts action valuations | A fixed potential and independently identified response model predict later endpoint/value observations. | Response accuracy can be tested under a provisional scale; a **root-denominated** field claim additionally requires A and its scope. |
| C. Current state is recursively sufficient | Different histories with matched admitted state/inputs have the same claimed subsequent output law within a stated bound. | State sufficiency can be challenged without a universal root. Full EBU recursive qualification also needs the value/response content of B. |
| D. Multiple fields couple consistently | Joint transitions, boundary terms and interactions agree with one calibrated joint model without duplication. | Requires constituent meaning/calibration and an additional composition test. A–C on isolated fields do not establish D. |

These are distinct scientific questions, not four names for one beta estimate. Failure of C can call for a different state without overturning A; failure of D can expose an omitted coupling without refuting either constituent field.

### 14.2 The minimum observable that avoids tautology

For the canonical root, an independently established energy difference and temperature predict

\[
r_A=(U_{\rm pre}-U_{\rm post})/(k_BT).
\]

A separately characterized equilibrium distribution, under the same declared reference measure, supplies the independently testable contrast \(r_B=\log(p_{\rm post}/p_{\rm pre})\). For continuous states, observations estimate densities or integrated bin probabilities through a specified observation model; they do not measure point probabilities directly. The predicted relation is \(r_B=r_A\), with a joint uncertainty statement and alternative scale/shape discrepancies that the later design can discriminate.

The potential must not be defined as \(-\log p_B\), its stiffness must not be set to the tested inverse covariance, and a force standard constructed by the same equipartition law must not be presented as independent. Shared coordinate standards are permissible if their dependence is accounted for; scientific independence does not require a false diagonal covariance matrix.

This is the smallest **kind of root-related independent observable** identified here: a nonconstant physical contrast measured by two differently grounded routes, with enough independently specified conditions to identify scale rather than absorb it in a free fit. It does not select instruments, sample counts, performance tolerances or an apparatus. Testing only a fitted intercept or a single automatically normalized state cannot identify the scale.

For a later field claim, an independently qualified physical V may be used with measured endpoints, but the additional prediction must come from the pre-action state and a locked response map. Computing E from the post-state is an accounting identity; predicting that post-state or the value distribution before observing it is physical content. A second independent physical observable, such as a justified conservative-force integral, can test the potential's interpretation where applicable; general dissipated work is not an interchangeable substitute.

### 14.3 Desired sequence and where falsifiability enters

1. Independently observe the current physical state, context and relevant input uncertainty.
2. Establish and lock the calibrated potential using physical information independent of the comparison outcome.
3. Specify an admitted physical action, its boundary and duration/conditions.
4. Record the predicted finite value or full named value law before its outcome is available.
5. In a separately authorized future stage, execute the action under a qualified protocol.
6. Independently measure the post-state and any comparison observable; preserve shared metrology dependencies.
7. Compare the fixed prediction with observation, including uncertainty and model-validity predicates.
8. Update the current physical state and preserve earlier event entries.
9. Predict the next action from that updated state.
10. Later repeat across different matched-state histories and, only with a joint model, interacting fields.

Steps 4/7 test response and physical interpretation; steps 9/10 test recursion and composition. The subtraction within steps 4 or 7 is mathematical. A disagreement can falsify the specified field model; its cause must be separated from invalid calibration, unmodeled context or observation failure. A qualified residual may challenge the adopted root/model combination, but does not automatically identify a violation of all statistical mechanics.

An adequate later design must predeclare its precision, meaningful alternatives, false-positive control, model-qualification checks and possible refusal/inconclusive outcomes. Those are requirements for interpretable evidence, not frozen experimental choices in this report.

## 16. Direct scientific feasibility answers

**1. Does the mathematics admit physically meaningful realizations?** Yes. Established fixed-context canonical energy models, with correct measures and independently calibrated energy/temperature, realize the dimensionless potential and endpoint contrast. Ideal conservative storage models also supply physical state functions on their scoped domains. Actual EBU field admission additionally requires response, observation, root and uncertainty evidence; the repository record does not establish a general executed system.

**2. Is one common denomination justified across all intended domains?** No. Thermal-normalized meaning is justified within canonical families; compatible joint composition has restricted constructions. Equal units do not establish a physical bridge to arbitrary environmental quantities or a universal economic value. Theoretical compatibility of selected models is not all-domain proof.

**3. Are there fundamental obstructions to universality?** There are conditional impossibilities: nonzero circulation versus one endpoint scalar; positive cycle dissipation versus a returning-state drop; unequal local thermal scales versus one fixed joule factor; and nontrivial valuation from conserved complete-system energy. There are also underdetermination and hidden-state failures. These refute specific bundles of requirements, not every possible universal construction. No universal existence or impossibility theorem has been established here.

**4. What is the narrowest plausible noncircular test domain?** One stationary, fixed-temperature canonical domain with a nonconstant independently measured energy landscape, a declared measure/support, independently qualified observation, and no unaccounted reservoir or hidden-coordinate contribution. One resolved degree of freedom may suffice for such a scoped contrast; a quadratic or optical system is not required. This is a scientific domain description, not an apparatus selection.

**5. What is hardest in environmental systems?** Identifying a complete enough measurable state, capturing delayed and externally driven responses, choosing a physically justified joint scalar, tracing its scale, bounding omitted interactions, and connecting the scalar to the claimed environmental consequence. A water level or material amount alone generally does not establish all these.

**6. What precedes an engineered resource network?** Individually qualified physical quantity/root relationships; state and response models adequate for the intended actions; a joint boundary and interaction account; shared-reservoir and calibration treatment; and prospective coupled predictions. Existing port and thermodynamic frameworks can be reused, but do not supply EBU-specific qualification automatically.

**7. What precedes an economic application?** All the relevant physical prerequisites, followed by explicit institutional definitions of actors, ownership, rights, allocation, exchange and governance, plus independent evidence about economic and social effects. Physical theory alone does not select them or imply fairness or welfare improvement.

**8. What is the most important unresolved requirement?** The independently justified, measurable joint physical potential and its preserved meaning across complete actions and field composition. For the canonical root this becomes a concrete calibration/qualification question; for broad environmental extension, the existence and identity of the required scalar remain unresolved.

**9. What smallest falsifiable experiment would advance the programme?** A later authorized comparison of independently calibrated physical contrasts with separately observed equilibrium contrasts, within a qualified nonconstant fixed-context model, using prospective uncertainty and discrimination criteria. It advances **root recoverability**. A subsequent action-prediction and matched-history extension would address field and recursive claims; the root comparison alone cannot do so. No equipment, counts or design freeze are selected here.
