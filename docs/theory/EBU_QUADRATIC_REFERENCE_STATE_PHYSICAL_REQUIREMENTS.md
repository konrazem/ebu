## 3. The working model and its exact consequences

### 3.1 The landscape comes first

Let x describe the physical operating state on a declared domain, r = x* its reference, and theta the fixed context. The deviation y = x − r records both amount and direction of departure. The quadratic V = yᵀHy/2 gives a specified joint geometry of that departure. A water coordinate might describe inventory deficit; an energy coordinate might describe a separately identified storage deviation. These names alone do not establish a common physical quantity or their cross coefficients.

Only the symmetric part of a real matrix contributes: yᵀHy = yᵀ(H + Hᵀ)y/2. Hence all gradient and Hessian claims here use symmetric H. On a full linear state space, H positive definite makes V nonnegative with a unique zero at r. Positive semidefinite H permits flat directions; strict negativity of E for displacement from r requires ΔᵀHΔ > 0. Indefinite H does not supply the intended bowl. These restrictions serve the selected model, not the general endpoint definition.

For H positive definite, define d_H(x,r)² = (x − r)ᵀH(x − r). Then E > 0 precisely when the post-state is closer in this geometry. It is not a claim that every coordinate improved. Nor is it ordinary distance: with H = diag(1,100), moving from (1,0) to (0,1/2) halves Euclidean distance but changes V from 1/2 to 25/2, giving E = −12.

**EBU consequence:** once the physical geometry is qualified, a mixed action can receive one reproducible total while its favorable and adverse components remain inspectable. The total does not erase those components or prove that no critical limit was crossed.

### 3.2 Actions and finite value precede the gradient interpretation

For fixed r and H, put Δ = x_post − x_pre and y = x_pre − r. Expanding the quadratic yields the already-established F/B result

\[
E=-y^{\mathsf T}H\Delta-\tfrac12\Delta^{\mathsf T}H\Delta.
\tag{Q1}
\]

Only after identifying this complete finite change do we write

\[
\mu=Hy,\qquad \nabla^2V=H,\qquad f_a=-\mu^{\mathsf T}s_a.
\tag{Q2}
\]

The last expression pairs the differential with the actual action direction. It does not select that direction or prescribe a motion law. The phrase “steepest direction” additionally needs a specified coordinate metric; raw gradient magnitudes in unlike units are not directly comparable. UNI supplies this distinction.

For the steering example, r = 0, H = [[4,1],[1,2]], x_pre = (0.4,0.2), x_post = (0.2,0.25). Direct exact arithmetic gives V_pre = 11/25 = 0.44, V_post = 77/400 = 0.1925 and E = 99/400 = 0.2475. The first-order estimate is 0.32; the curvature correction is −0.0725. The matrix is positive definite, with leading principal minors 4 and 7. The example demonstrates the calculation, not physical calibration of these illustrative numbers.

At the reference, μ = 0 and E = −ΔᵀHΔ/2. First-order valuation is zero there, although a finite displacement can have strictly negative value. An underdamped object can pass through its potential minimum with nonzero velocity. Therefore “no local slope” cannot be replaced by “no movement.”

### 3.3 What quadraticity actually resolves

The declared V is smooth and single-valued. Its differential is exact on every admitted path, so no separate topological existence proof is needed for this declared polynomial. If independently measured candidate marginals are not consistent with Hy, the physical identification fails; changing their name to H does not repair them.

For coalition endpoints x_S = x_0 + sum_{i in S} Δ_i with fixed increments, the quadratic has only first- and second-order Möbius terms, with pair coefficient −Δ_iᵀHΔ_j. If the actual response is nonlinear in the actions, that order bound does not follow. For example, scalar V = x²/2 and endpoint x_S = |S|² give coalition values −|S|⁴/2 from x_0 = 0, with third-order coefficient −18 for three actions. This consolidates M/MG's existing counterexample.

**EBU consequence:** exact finite evaluation can avoid needless local approximation and, where the additive-response premise holds, simplify interaction accounting. It does not justify simplifying a nonlinear resource response to force a pair-only result.

## 4. What must establish the reference state

### 4.1 A physical reference is more specific than a preferred condition

Four kinds of reference must be separated: an independently characterized physical equilibrium; an operating state maintained by measured inputs; an engineering reference associated with a declared physical service or limit; and a chosen social or institutional target. A reference may have more than one role, but that overlap requires explanation.

Under the intended architecture, an actor does not choose r to improve its score. A field contract must identify the physical criterion, boundary, context and observations that fix the reference. A physically reproducible operating setpoint can still be a design choice; reproducibility does not prove it is the uniquely best state or the minimum of an independently identified thermodynamic quantity. The metrological distinction between specifying a measurand and realizing it is relevant [N05].

Changing coordinate origin so that r has numerical value zero is harmless if it describes the same physical state. Choosing a different physical r is not an origin change. For fixed H and Δ, replacing r by r + a changes E by aᵀHΔ. It can change values even though both potentials have zero at their own references. Thus the reference needs a physical basis alongside the scale.

### 4.2 Constraints, reachability and target regions

For affine conservation constraints Cx = b, choose accessible coordinates x = x_0 + Bq, where B has full column rank and CB = 0. Then

\[
V(q)=\tfrac12 q^{\mathsf T}H_Tq+\ell^{\mathsf T}q+c,
\quad H_T=B^{\mathsf T}HB,\quad \ell=B^{\mathsf T}H(x_0-r).
\tag{Q3}
\]

When H_T is positive definite, the constrained quadratic minimum is q* = −H_T^{-1}ell. If r lies on the accessible affine space, the model can be centered there and ell removed. If it does not, merely declaring r does not make it reachable. Additional action bounds may make even the constrained minimizer dynamically unreachable. Curvature in inaccessible directions is not identified by measurements confined to this affine space. A flat observed direction and an unobserved direction are different cases (B §5).

For nonlinear constraints, differentiating the restricted potential also involves the embedding's second derivatives; BᵀHB alone is not generally its Hessian away from an appropriate stationary point. The affine formula above is deliberately not claimed for arbitrary manifolds.

A continuum of acceptable reference states is not represented exactly by a single strictly positive-definite bowl with one zero. A positive semidefinite quadratic can represent a flat affine minimum set; a nonlinear acceptable region may require a different construction. Likewise, surplus and shortage of the same magnitude receive equal isolated quadratic cost about r, even where the actual physical consequence is asymmetric. This limits the global model; it does not prevent a justified local use.

**EBU consequence:** making constraints explicit prevents an unreachable reference from being mistaken for an attainable restoration endpoint. Movement toward such a reference can still lower V; the account must not confuse that improvement with proof that the reference can be reached. An enlarged state or local chart can be useful, provided its physical meaning and domain are explicit.

## 5. What must establish curvature and its scale

### 5.1 Identification before certification and discipline afterward

H may be the scaled Hessian of independently measured conservative energy, a local curvature of another justified physical state quantity, a covariance precision, a constructed stability matrix, or an institutional tracking weight. Those are different origins. The intended physical EBU architecture accepts only an origin with the relevant independent physical and root bridge. The other constructions are comparisons and possible sources of confusion, not proposed replacements for that architecture.

Before qualification, suppose only the action endpoints are known. For the example in §3, replacing the illustrative H by diag(1,10) gives E = −21/400 = −0.0525. Both matrices are positive definite and share r = 0. Endpoint observations and positive definiteness alone therefore do not identify even the sign of that mixed action. **After a physical contract selects and calibrates one H, the other is not a permissible actor choice under that contract.** The counterexample diagnoses absent identification; it does not reintroduce free weights.

The same distinction applies to scale. With an unidentified mobility, the response model xdot = −M H(x − r) is unchanged by H → cH and M → M/c, c > 0, although E is multiplied by c. Once the quantity/root scale or mobility is independently fixed, this transformation is no longer observationally equivalent under the complete measurement contract. It is not a permanent obstruction to calibration.

### 5.2 Response, stability and probability do not independently select H

The dynamic Jacobian describes how motion changes with state; H describes the curvature of the scalar. Their dimensions and roles generally differ. An independently known response law can constrain H if its mobility and constitutive bridge are also justified. Observing stronger response after depletion alone does not show that H increased: mobility, forcing, geometry, boundary conditions or hidden state may have changed.

A quadratic Lyapunov function can certify stability without being physical energy. For an asymptotically stable linear matrix A and chosen positive Q, AᵀP + PA = −Q has a positive solution P. Changing Q generally changes P. Thus a stable response can admit multiple candidate bowls. This established control-theory construction is useful evidence about stability, not a physical selection rule for EBU curvature [N03]. Quadratic optimal-control criteria likewise include a specified objective and response model; an endpoint EBU drop is not automatically the LQR objective or optimal policy [N01, N04].

Setting H to the inverse measured covariance defines a covariance-distance score. It is not an independent test of whether a separately calibrated physical H equals that inverse covariance. The density relation, if claimed, requires its own premises (§9).

### 5.3 Units, coupled sources and identification sets

For dimensionless V, [H_ij] = ([x_i][x_j])^{-1}. Under an invertible affine coordinate change x' = Ax + b, take r' = Ar + b and H' = A^{-T}HA^{-1}. Then V and E are unchanged. In one coordinate, using litres instead of cubic metres multiplies the coordinate by 1000 and divides its curvature by 10^6. This is not an altered physical sensitivity. General nonlinear coordinates preserve the scalar but need not preserve a constant quadratic matrix.

Off-diagonal entries depend on coordinates. In a fixed physically meaningful chart, they encode mixed curvature. Their presence does not alone establish pumping causality, transport efficiency or a directional force law. A joint energy or other identified source can establish such coefficients; a story that water and energy are related cannot determine their numerical values.

Independent identification need only resolve the contrasts claimed on the admitted domain, not every unobservable matrix entry. Let I be the physically consistent parameter/model set after calibration and observations. A quantitative claim can be bounded if the spread of E over I is bounded on every admitted comparison. Additive constants and contrast-invariant ambiguities are harmless there. A sign is certified only when every allowed realization yields that sign, with the named uncertainty treatment. A point estimate or the label “certified” is insufficient by itself.

**EBU consequence:** a documented identification set permits honest finite resolution and comparison across laboratories without pretending that every microscopic coefficient is known. It also makes unsupported reweighting detectable against the agreed physical contract.

## 6. The root contract and the scarcity mechanism

### 6.1 Four levels of claim

| Level | What is established | Additional obligation for the next level |
|---|---|---|
| Declared quadratic geometry | E follows uniquely from specified r, H and endpoints. | Establish what physical contrast it represents. |
| Reproducible operational index | Specified measurements and conventions reproducibly evaluate the index. | Independently justify the claimed physical kind and constitutive bridge. |
| Qualified physical contrast | A named quantity, orientation, root scale and domain are independently supported. | Establish compatibility and composition with other fields/contexts. |
| Common physical denomination | Linked realizations preserve the named kind and calibrated contrasts across their certified comparison domain. | Every further extension needs its own physical bridge; universality does not follow. |

These distinguish scientific claims, not alternate settlement definitions. The user's intended system requires the physical/root levels. An actor-chosen score cannot be passed off as their substitute. A unit convention remains legitimate: objectivity requires fixing a specified quantity and convention reproducibly, not abolishing all conventions [L02, N05].

For a correctly specified canonical harmonic system, independently measured energy U − U* = yᵀK_U y/2 and T yield H = K_U/(k_BT). This supplies an established restricted realization. General root-compatible source fields must supply their own bridge. The withdrawn constant-B0 E1 design is not reinstated, and no fixed joule-per-EBU unit is introduced.

### 6.2 Scarcity can change value without changing curvature

Consider an illustrative deficit coordinate d ≥ 0 measured from an independently justified reference, fixed curvature h > 0, and further deficit q > 0. If the qualified scalar really is V(d) = hd²/2, then

\[
E(d\to d+q)=-h d q-\tfrac12h q^2,
\qquad \frac{\partial E}{\partial d}=-hq<0.
\tag{Q4}
\]

Thus the same additional depletion becomes more adverse as the existing deficit grows, even with constant h. Restoration d → d − q with 0 < q ≤ d gives E = hdq − hq²/2 > 0. Overshooting the reference requires evaluating the full formula. These are conditional statements about the chosen physically qualified deficit potential; they are not proofs that all water depletion has this physical root interpretation.

If real field change produces H_new = 100H_old under the same root, coordinate system and reference, then the same endpoint pair has E_new = 100E_old. That is a change of landscape, not of unit. If the reference, endpoint response or other context terms also change, the factor of 100 alone does not determine the new E. Neither case implies that every environmental consequence is multiplied by 100; only the identified scalar contrast has that conclusion.

This distinction is already explicit in S §17, “The same unit does not mean an unchanged action value,” with a harmonic stiffness/reference/temperature example. The new steering emphasizes an existing conditional result; it is not a new denomination theorem.

| Situation | Scientific treatment |
|---|---|
| Same root, independently established new physical curvature | A real context change; use the correct qualified field for each event and account for the field transition. |
| Better calibration corrects an earlier estimate | A revised estimate with uncertainty and provenance; not a new physical event merely because the estimate changed. |
| Units or coordinates change consistently | Same V and E; transform states, references and curvature together. |
| Operator multiplies H without an admitted physical or representation change | Invalid under the existing physical contract; neither scarcity evidence nor calibration. |

**EBU consequence:** sensitivity to a worsening physical state can emerge from the qualified field itself. This can make deterioration visible without discretionary weights. It establishes neither market prices nor penalties; the value measures the declared physical contrast. Attribution rules and institutional consequences remain separate.

### 6.3 Calibration is an evidence chain, not a label

The proposed chain is physical system → observations and constitutive model → independently identified quantity/reference → compatible root realization → H and V → measured finite E. Independent response evidence is part of that chain where used; response alone need not identify a scalar. The qualification must expose its measurement equations, standards, reference, uncertainties, scope and contrast-identification limits.

Deliberately altered sensors, omitted waste streams or undocumented multipliers can violate this contract. Physical analysis can identify inconsistency, missing fluxes or invalid calibration. It does not infer intention from a residual or determine legal fraud and sanctions. Even a valid estimate has uncertainty; “physical truth” in an operational record must not imply infallible observation. No legal classification or enforcement design is made here.

## 7. Physical fields, response, recursion and composition

### 7.1 A scalar is not the complete physical state

The retained state must support both the claimed value and future response under the permitted actions, inputs and horizon. Two histories at the same current water level can differ in soil saturation, contamination, groundwater, temperature, infrastructure condition or stored momentum. Whether these differences matter is a physical question. Four named coordinates do not establish sufficiency, while four coordinates are not ruled out for a suitably restricted claim.

RF/M give the deterministic criterion: detailed states represented by the same retained state must have the same available actions, projected updates and claimed values under the same conditioned inputs. For stochastic recursion, the appropriate requirement is equality of the joint law of the claimed value and next retained state, together with any additional intermediate outputs being asserted. Equality of means or separate marginals alone is insufficient. Approximate sufficiency requires an output-error bound on a declared horizon, not the assertion that hidden variables are probably small.

An energy model may require velocity as well as position, and a resource model may require memory or distributional state. If the named macroscopic x is only a projection of a larger state z, both value and response must be shown to descend appropriately. This is unchanged by the quadratic working choice. Known projection and identification theory remains relevant [L12–L14, L19].

### 7.2 Independently specified action and passive response

An action model supplies x_post = A_a(x_pre, inputs) or a conditional kernel. A prospective claim uses that independently identified response to predict the finite drop. After the action, the registered value uses the observed admitted endpoints and their measurement model. These are distinct: agreement between two computations from the same endpoints tests arithmetic, not prospective response.

Passive evolution remains a separate physical event. Natural replenishment is not automatically the factory's contribution; an actor's recorded event boundary and a counterfactual causal contribution are not interchangeable. Causal attribution, if claimed, needs its own specified comparison and cannot be inferred from the quadratic's sign. F's attribution convention and M's passive accounting remain intact.

**EBU consequence:** the architecture can use a compact current physical state without replaying historical prices only where the response/value sufficiency criterion holds. That is a tractable target for a restricted field, not a general property conferred by a matrix.

### 7.3 Joint curvature and hidden coordinates

For deviations a and b, a physically identified joint quadratic with symmetric diagonal blocks A and B can have

\[
V(a,b)=\tfrac12a^{\mathsf T}A a+a^{\mathsf T}C b+\tfrac12b^{\mathsf T}B b.
\tag{Q5}
\]

The cross term belongs once in the total. Summing independently calibrated diagonal blocks omits it. Splitting that cross term between source diagnostics is not necessarily physically unique; changing a decomposition while preserving the total cannot issue additional E. Physical source identities require SFE's embedding and contrast-identification conditions, not just a choice of matrix blocks.

For B positive definite, completing the square gives

\[
V=\tfrac12 a^{\mathsf T}(A-CB^{-1}C^{\mathsf T})a
+\tfrac12(b+B^{-1}C^{\mathsf T}a)^{\mathsf T}
B(b+B^{-1}C^{\mathsf T}a).
\tag{Q6}
\]

Minimizing over b yields the Schur potential. A Gaussian integral over the full hidden space with fixed flat measure yields the same a-dependent quadratic plus a determinant-dependent constant. This is the established B/SFE/M result, not new mathematics. Constants can matter when field parameters or the integration measure change. Physical minimization, thermal marginalization and actual hidden-state evolution are different operations.

Write the second term as R(a,b). The complete realized drop differs from the reduced drop by R_pre − R_post. Equality holds for the admitted comparison precisely when that residual contrast vanishes, or within a bound when it is bounded. Static elimination does not guarantee recursive closure. For V = (a²+b²)/2, the transition (0,0) → (0,1) has complete E = −1/2 and reduced a-only E = 0. The quadratic form itself therefore does not eliminate the hidden-state objection.

### 7.4 Compatibility across fields

One denomination requires a common identified kind, compatible calibration and a physical joint model. These are distinct obligations. At one canonical temperature a known total U_A + U_B + U_int can supply a joint thermal potential, counting interaction and reservoir terms once. Equal numerical dimensionless values alone do not establish that model.

Independent canonical systems at different fixed temperatures can legitimately have the product-density scalar U_A/(k_BT_A) + U_B/(k_BT_B) plus an offset. They cannot also realize one fixed multiple of total joule change for all independent drops unless the temperatures coincide. Letting each drop vary separately proves the incompatibility of those simultaneous requirements. That conditional obstruction survives a harmonic specialization.

Joint entropy additionally contains correlation terms; shared finite reservoirs and ports must be represented consistently [L11, L16, L17]. The selected quadratic form can express some interactions economically in parameters, but provides no physical license to aggregate unrelated fields with arbitrary coefficients.

## 8. Changing fields, physical balances and historical records

### 8.1 Separate movement in a field from movement of the field

For a differentiable path with y = x − r, symmetric time-dependent H and a declared common scalar meaning,

\[
\dot V=(Hy)^{\mathsf T}\dot x-(Hy)^{\mathsf T}\dot r
+\tfrac12y^{\mathsf T}\dot H y.
\tag{Q7}
\]

The first term is state movement at the instantaneous field. The other terms account for reference and curvature change. This is the chain rule, not a new conservation law. If different fields lack a common physical meaning or aligned levels, subtraction across them first needs that bridge; writing Q7 formally does not establish it.

For a discrete protocol that applies action and passive evolution at theta_n before changing to theta_{n+1}, define

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_n&=V_{\theta_n}(x_n)-V_{\theta_n}(\widetilde x_n),\\
D_n&=V_{\theta_n}(\widetilde x_n)-V_{\theta_n}(x_{n+1}),\\
G_n&=V_{\theta_n}(x_{n+1})-V_{\theta_{n+1}}(x_{n+1}).
\end{aligned}
\]

Then E_n + D_n + G_n = V_{theta_n}(x_n) − V_{theta_{n+1}}(x_{n+1}), and adjacent terms telescope. With a ledger increment E_n alone, Δ(C + V) = −D_n − G_n. This separation is protocol-dependent; simultaneous field/action changes need the actual extended path or event convention. It neither credits parameter changes as physical restoration nor permits repricing historical actor entries.

### 8.2 State-dependent curvature is not constant curvature

If theta is independently fixed during each action, the fixed-field equation applies. If theta is itself determined by the changing state and is eliminated from the description, its derivatives must be included. For a constructed scalar W(x) = (x − r)ᵀH(x)(x − r)/2 with fixed r and differentiable symmetric H(x),

\[
\partial_i W=[H(x)(x-r)]_i
+\tfrac12(x-r)^{\mathsf T}(\partial_i H)(x-r).
\tag{Q8}
\]

Thus H(x) need not be the Hessian of W. In one dimension H(x) = 1 + x² gives W = x²/2 + x⁴/2, W' = x + 2x³ and W'' = 1 + 6x². Substituting H(x)x as the derivative is wrong. The same issue is stronger when r also depends on x.

Conversely, an independently measured field of local Hessians must be compatible with a common potential if a global reconstruction is claimed. Under sufficient smoothness, third-derivative symmetries are among the local compatibility conditions; global marginal/path consistency and reference data are also needed. Positive definiteness at each point alone is not a global constitutive reconstruction. A local Taylor model may be valid without making the whole domain one exact quadratic.

**EBU consequence:** a genuinely changing scarcity landscape is admissible as a scientific possibility, but continuous state dependence cannot be disguised as repeated arbitrary resetting of a constant-H formula. Explicit context and remainder treatment preserve the meaning of finite values.

### 8.3 Conservation, dissipation and corrections

Energy and material balances retain their own quantities and boundary terms. Telescoping E is not a first law. A cycle returning the represented state and context has zero endpoint drop even if external driving dissipated work into an omitted reservoir. Adding the appropriate physical reservoir state can change that representation; merely appending the desired answer is not independent validation.

Calibration correction differs from physical field change. A new estimate may show that an earlier reported value was uncertain or wrong; it does not show that nature changed at the time of recalibration. Preserve original records and associate any subsequent correction/qualification notice with its provenance. Whether a later institution issues an accounting adjustment is outside this study; no retrospective replacement of historical receipts is authorized here. Permanent entries do not establish that two fields have the same unit or that old measurements were infallible.

## 9. Optional probability and thermodynamic bridges

### 9.1 A separate hypothesis after the physical model

For an independently established quadratic V, one can ask whether measured states have a density p proportional to exp(−beta_bridge V) relative to a specified measure. If the accessible coordinates q span an entire affine space, the measure is flat there, H_T is positive definite and beta_bridge > 0, completing the square gives a Gaussian with

\[
\Sigma_q^{-1}=\beta_{\rm bridge}H_T.
\tag{Q9}
\]

For a reference at the accessible minimum its mean is that reference. Full-space covariance need not be invertible under constraints. Bounded support, truncation, nonconstant density-of-states factors, exposure, observation noise or unaccounted hidden terms change what covariance measures. Positive semidefinite curvature on an unbounded flat direction does not normalize this density without additional confinement or a restricted space. These are S's existing scope conditions.

Observing Gaussian fluctuations is not sufficient to identify H with the precision. In a one-dimensional supplied process dX = −aX dt + sqrt(2D) dW with a,D > 0, the stationary variance is D/a. A separately declared V = hX²/2 has inverse covariance h only if a/D = h. Stationarity gives this variance from the second-moment balance; no simulation is needed. The distinction survives in the matrix Lyapunov equation and is already covered by M's OU branch.

Even equality of the density to exp(−V) does not establish detailed balance: the parent report's V = (x²+y²)/2 with drift (−x−y,−y+x) and unit diffusion has stationary current (−y,x)p. Additional temporal premises are required for equilibrium/entropy claims.

### 9.2 The known canonical realization

For fixed T > 0, a correct canonical energy/measure pair and finite normalizer,

\[
p=Z^{-1}e^{-U/(k_BT)},\quad
V=(U-U^*)/(k_BT),\quad
\log(p_{\rm post}/p_{\rm pre})=E.
\tag{Q10}
\]

This established relation needs no quadratic assumption [L01]. Harmonic U adds Q9 with beta_bridge = 1 and H = K_U/(k_BT). The inverse thermal temperature 1/(k_BT) has different units and meaning from the dimensionless bridge coefficient. Continuous densities are defined against a measure; they are not point-event probabilities.

If a macrostate density contains degeneracy g(x), then −log p = U/(k_BT) − log g + constant. A potential of mean force obtained by integrating hidden variables can therefore differ from bare energy. A correct marginal need not preserve detailed endpoint drops or dynamics [L04, L13]. Independent energy and temperature calibration must not impose the same probability relationship subsequently described as its test.

The SI fixes k_B exactly, while realized temperature and energy still have uncertainty [L03]. The chosen thermal normalization does not equate different-temperature E values to the same joules or environmental impact. Fixed-context offsets cancel; changing-context offsets can affect cross-context levels.

### 9.3 Other physical interpretations remain scoped

Under S's stationary, static, conservative, overdamped, one-bath P4 conditions at fixed temperature, with no omitted work, heat delivered to the bath is k_BT E, medium entropy is +k_B E, stochastic system entropy is −k_B E, and total stochastic entropy change is zero. Driving or a different preparation requires its own balance [L05]. A factory's imposed action cannot be assigned this interpretation merely because its field is quadratic.

The entropy-deficit candidate S_eq − S = kappa V remains conditional on an independently identified entropy, positive constant kappa, fixed context, spatially constant S_eq and appropriate reference. Near a constrained entropy maximum, a local quadratic excess can follow with a third-order remainder; global equality and a universal kappa do not follow from the Taylor expansion.

Nonequilibrium free energy can be a scalar on a distribution state, with an established relative-entropy relation at fixed energy, temperature and measure [L06]. Work fluctuation theorems concern distributions of work and equilibrium free-energy differences [L07, L08], not equality of every trajectory's work and endpoint drop. Exergy depends on its environment and constraints [L09, L21]; identifying V with positive exergy would make depletion a positive E drop, not automatically environmental restoration. These are possible physical constructions requiring separate bridges, not interchangeable interpretations of the selected deficit bowl.

**EBU consequence:** the physical field can stand on a valid nonstatistical constitutive contract. A failed Gaussian bridge challenges that bridge and its premises, not every possible quadratic field. A successful bridge qualifies only the tested regime and does not complete the physical economy model.

## 10. Finite-domain accuracy and metrology

### 10.1 Exact model versus local physical approximation

Let Phi be an independently identified root-scaled physical scalar, C³ on a convex neighborhood in fixed, unit-declared coordinates. Suppose its first derivative vanishes at r on the accessible coordinates, and H is its Hessian there. If the third derivative has operator norm at most M throughout each required segment, Taylor's theorem gives

\[
\Phi(r+y)=\Phi(r)+\tfrac12y^{\mathsf T}Hy+R(y),
\quad |R(y)|\le M\|y\|^3/6.
\]

Hence, for exact endpoints,

\[
|E_{\Phi}-E_{\rm quad}|
\le \frac M6\bigl(\|y_{\rm pre}\|^3+\|y_{\rm post}\|^3\bigr).
\tag{Q11}
\]

This is a sufficient, possibly conservative bound. A correlated residual contrast can be much smaller. Norms and M depend on the declared coordinate scales. At a nonstationary reference the linear term cannot be discarded; on a nonlinear constraint manifold the Taylor expansion must be made in the correct restricted coordinates. Exact quadraticity means the residual is identically constant for contrasts, not merely that a few finite-difference checks were zero.

A nonzero third-order action interaction challenges the combined quadratic/additive-response model, not quadraticity alone. Multiple local fits cannot be glued into a single globally exact field without compatibility checks.

### 10.2 Joint uncertainty in endpoints, reference and curvature

For one fixed field, write y_0 = x_pre − r, y_1 = x_post − r, mu_i = Hy_i. First variation gives

\[
\delta E=
\mu_0^{\mathsf T}\delta x_{\rm pre}
-\mu_1^{\mathsf T}\delta x_{\rm post}
+(\mu_1-\mu_0)^{\mathsf T}\delta r
+\tfrac12(y_0y_0^{\mathsf T}-y_1y_1^{\mathsf T}):\delta H.
\tag{Q12}
\]

The colon denotes the Frobenius contraction; a parameterization by independent symmetric entries must account for off-diagonal multiplicity consistently. Correlated estimates of H and r must remain correlated. For a justified locally linear measurement model with joint input covariance C and Jacobian J_E, the propagated variance is J_E C J_Eᵀ; this is not a universal exact nonlinear error law [L20, N05]. At zero first derivatives, second-order effects can dominate.

Separate corrected calibration bias and its uncertainty, random observation error, response uncertainty, deterministic model remainder, omitted boundary/state effects and numerical error. Do not count the same stochastic Taylor contribution again as an independent physical bias. Conversely, a missing physical remainder is not zero. Endpoint correlations and common-scale errors can cancel or reinforce; treating every entry as independent can misstate the result.

For uncertainty set I, a uniform positive margin requires inf_{I} E > 0. This is stronger than pointwise positivity on an arbitrary noncompact set. If an admissible realization permits zero or a negative value, strict positivity is unresolved at that stated uncertainty. No universal numerical acceptance width or coverage level is selected here. Root, shape, reference and response discrepancies need distinct diagnostic interpretations.

## 14. Minimum discriminating evidence

### 14.1 Independent physical identification before a probability claim

The smallest useful scientific object is a bounded physical contrast contract: a named measurable source quantity, its root bridge, the accessible state, reference, curvature and domain, together with enough independent physical observations to distinguish incompatible values. The arithmetic is not itself the experiment.

A later nonstatistical qualification could compare a prospectively fixed quadratic model with independent constitutive observations or physical contrasts over nonzero displacements. Where an independently justified generalized force is the derivative of the source quantity, its calibrated change can constrain curvature and its zero can locate a stationary reference. The force-to-potential/root conversion and reference accessibility must be supplied, not inferred from calling mu a force. Independent data can test a constitutive approximation without claiming that the underlying identity defining the measurand is a new law.

One accessible mode can test a nonconstant restricted reference/curvature/scale relationship, provided the chosen comparisons actually distinguish the unknowns. It cannot validate mixed resource geometry. Cross terms need independent accessible mixed directions or equally informative physical constraints. Observing one scalar trajectory or one endpoint difference generally cannot identify all entries of a multivariate H and r.

If the programme instead claims the canonical statistical bridge, the later comparison must keep the independently constructed energy/temperature branch separate from the density branch. It must include observation and shared-calibration dependencies. The quadratic simplification does not waive T/U branch independence for their existing experiment. These two kinds of evidence answer different questions; this report recommends physical identification first and selects no experimental branch or apparatus.

### 14.2 Failure meanings

A failure is scientifically interpretable only relative to a qualified measurement model and prospective discrepancy criterion. No observations or held-out programme records were inspected for these proposed challenges. This study chooses no sample size, threshold, experimental arm or equipment.

### 14.3 Practical benefit and scope of a pass

Successful physical identification would give independent users the same ruler for the admitted comparisons. Successful response qualification would let them estimate consequences before acting. Successful recursive qualification would support repeated updates without concealing relevant history. Successful composition would support complete totals across fields with shared effects counted once. These are concrete capabilities, with distinct evidence needs.

They could help resource operators distinguish restoration from deterioration within the qualified physical meaning, detect omitted burdens and compare interventions consistently. Whether a society benefits additionally depends on which consequences are covered, what actions are available, and how the information is used. No efficiency optimum, allocation rule, legal sanction or welfare theorem is derived from the scalar sign.

## 16. Direct scientific feasibility answers

**1. What becomes simpler?** A supplied quadratic gives exact finite values, an explicit gradient and constant Hessian, accessible convex geometry and integrability. Additive responses additionally give pair-only interactions. The requirements can target identifiable parameters and bounded remainders rather than an unspecified function class.

**2. Which mathematics is already settled?** Conditional endpoint subtraction, Q1/Q2, coordinate transformation, restricted quadratic minimization, Schur algebra, the appropriate Möbius specialization and field-change chain rule. Established theorems do not need fresh physical experiments to make their mathematical implications valid. Their proposed physical instantiation is the object of this study.

**3. What must establish x*?** A stated physical source/reference criterion, relevant context, accessibility or constrained-reference treatment, and measurements/derivation with uncertainty. A reproducible setpoint need not be a uniquely physical optimum. If the physical scalar is not stationary at the chosen reference, a centered pure quadratic approximation omits a real linear term.

**4. What must establish H?** An independently identified physical scalar and root bridge, correct coordinate units, enough observations or physical constraints to identify the claimed contrasts, and evidence for the exact/local domain. Positive definiteness, a covariance fit or stability alone cannot supply all of this. Once supplied, arbitrary reweighting is excluded under the contract.

**5. Can the model work without Gaussianity or equilibrium?** Yes as exact mathematics, and physically within an independently justified nonstatistical constitutive domain. A physically qualified conservative quadratic storage contrast is not logically dependent on Gaussian sampling. A driven system still needs a response and boundary model; the availability of V does not equate its drop with every work or entropy quantity.

**6. Which physical realizations and limits are established?** Harmonic canonical models and local constrained thermodynamic approximations supply restricted established or conditional realizations. Bounded storage accounts are plausible when their quantity/root interpretation is justified. A global resource economy with a unique natural reference, full joint curvature and sufficient measurable state is not established. Positive-definite quadratics are restrictive around thresholds, irreversible losses and multiple acceptable states.

**7. Is a common denomination established across all intended fields?** No. It is conditionally available within the named canonical family and within other explicitly compatible physical contracts if established. One root may have several realizations; equal-looking matrices or dimensionless values do not prove the required compatibility. True field changes are compatible with a common ruler and do not grant arbitrary operator scaling.

**8. Are there fundamental obstructions, and which are hardest environmentally?** The conditional cycle-dissipation, unequal-joule-scale, conserved-total-energy and hidden-state witnesses remain. A supplied quadratic removes scalar-existence uncertainty but not physical integrability agreement. Environmental difficulty centers on identifying one common quantity, defining its reference, capturing delayed/shared effects and proving a useful domain with manageable uncertainty. No universal impossibility theorem is claimed.

**9. What precedes networks and economic use?** Individually qualified physical fields; adequate action/passive and recursive descriptions; and a joint root/boundary/interaction model. Economic deployment additionally needs separately defined responsibility, rights, attribution and institutional consequences. No incentive or welfare property is inferred from physical valuation alone. The architecture can preserve a physical layer without allowing institutions to alter its certified coefficients.

**10. Does this repair T/U/V or W1?** No. Existing numerical and physical qualification failures are unchanged, no metrology assumptions are improved, and the 0.0005 failure is not removed. The new conditions do not demonstrate a T/U incompatibility. W1's refused candidate does not become admitted by naming another potential form. The conditions may justify a different future scientific target, under its own gate.

**11. What is the smallest unresolved scientific question?** Can one bounded intended physical source independently supply the root-compatible reference and curvature, with enough observable state and controlled approximation to identify meaningful action contrasts? For a known canonical source the conceptual bridge is available and the question becomes qualification; for the intended macroscopic extension the constitutive identification is itself unresolved.

**12. What is the smallest useful future test?** After that contract is fixed, compare a nonconstant, prospectively specified physical contrast against independent observations with joint uncertainty. A local curvature/finite-contrast test need not invoke probability; a thermal bridge test does. Mixed directions, recursive history and coupling require additional comparisons for their respective claims. No apparatus, sample count or design freeze is selected here.
