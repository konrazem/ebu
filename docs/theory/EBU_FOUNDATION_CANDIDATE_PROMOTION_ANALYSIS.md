## 1 Decision

**Recommend promotion of the invariant admission content of the four cleared candidates through one new foundation subsection, with no replacement of existing text.** This is a recommendation for a later authorized amendment, not the amendment itself. The candidates do not constitute four new independent laws of nature.

| Cleared candidate | Promotion classification | Smallest justified change |
|---|---|---|
| COMMON-DENOMINATION LAW | **FOUNDATION** | Make the physical denomination requirement explicit for historical and simultaneous scalar aggregation. It is explicit in the baseline and conditional theory, but only partly present in the frozen foundation. |
| NONCOMMENSURABILITY REFUSAL | **FOUNDATION**, as the admission consequence of the first principle | State the default of retaining tagged components; distinguish refusal of an unsupported physical total from action legality, affordability or a new settlement definition. |
| JOINT-FIELD INTERACTION LAW | **FOUNDATION**, with most of its content **already foundational** | Add only the physical joint-composition prerequisite. The endpoint rule, complete coupled-factor evaluation, no extra issuance and nonunique allocation already exist and should not be enacted again. |
| ROOT-CLASS / CALIBRATION-CLOSURE LAW | **FOUNDATION** at the invariant compatibility level | Define compatible realizations of one denomination and require consistent calibration on declared domains. Keep affine formulas and qualification procedures in theorem reports. |

The minimum amendment is **one insertion at the end of foundation §2**, adding proposed §2.1 below. No fifth law is needed. The frozen definitions of V, mu, E and f already require the relevant scalar and its gradient; the new physical-composition sentence makes clear that separately calibrated source marginals cannot bypass that requirement.

Four boundaries determine the recommendation:

1. Physical denomination is necessary for physical aggregation, but does not establish a joint physical potential.
2. A source may be mathematically declared before its physical origin is established; the foundation's conditional identities remain valid in that setting.
3. A root class organizes compatible representations of one declared denomination. It is not a theorem that every physical field has a root or that all roots are compatible.
4. Detailed scale, covariance, equilibrium, Gaussian and nonthermal-source claims remain at their present conditional or exploratory levels.

## 4 Common denomination

**Classification: FOUNDATION.** The invariant requirement is a condition on physical scalar aggregation. It does not depend on choosing a thermal root, a capacitor, a quadratic V or a statistical distribution.

| Commission question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Necessary for historical and simultaneous addition? | Yes for an unqualified **physical** scalar total. Pure arithmetic can add arbitrary numbers, but does not identify their physical meaning. Historical records also require a common denomination before being interpreted as one physical accumulation. |
| Already fully implied by F? | Only in part. F's formulas assume a declared V and its unit; §11 addresses incompatible carriers. Neither explicitly establishes cross-field EBU admission or excludes all dimensionless quantity-kind mismatches. |
| Present only in conditional theory? | It is explicit already in B §8 as a physical commensurability requirement, and formalized by RF and G. Promotion raises a general requirement, not the conditional success of any physical bridge. |
| Depends on one realization? | No. A root contract and the evidence for satisfying it remain source-specific. |
| Thermal/nonthermal, quadratic/nonlinear, many coordinates? | The requirement survives all of them. It does not assert that a valid normalization is available in each case. |
| Changing physical fields? | It survives, but context-dependent scale/reference effects must be accounted for; constancy of past records alone is insufficient. |
| Quantity kind or bridge? | Same quantity kind and compatible comparison meaning, or an independently justified physical bridge between kinds, are required. Same dimensions alone are weaker. |
| Dimensionlessness sufficient? | No. Normalized angle, energy and preference quantities are not physically interchangeable because they are written as dimension-one numbers. |
| Denomination sufficient for joint composition? | No. A common unit leaves physical factor identity, overlap, interactions, endpoints and existence of a consistent joint V unresolved. |

The rule governs the claimed quantity, not the numerical appearance of raw observations. A nonlinear sensor correction may recover a physical quantity; it is not necessarily a nonlinear transformation of the admitted EBU amount. Equally, a nonlinear potential V is compatible with a fixed denomination. These two meanings of “nonlinear” must not be confused.

For changing contexts, M §6 and G §17 give, for a corrected representation `V'=c(theta)V+d(theta)`, the complete drop `c_pre V_pre − c_post V_post + d_pre − d_post`. It is generally not one constant times the old drop. The proposed wording therefore names the context and comparison domain rather than freezing all fields or permitting arbitrary scale changes. F §17's changing-field terms remain untouched.

**Why explicit promotion matters:** it prevents mathematical homogeneity and permanent records from being presented as a physical calibration. It does not add a requirement for canonical equilibrium to the endpoint theorem.

## 5 Noncommensurability refusal

**Classification: FOUNDATION, as a stated consequence and default representation of candidate 1.** It is not a fifth mathematical valuation rule and not logically independent of the common-denomination requirement.

Suppose two well-calibrated local quantities give contrasts `(1,−1)`, but their relative denomination is unidentified. Positive relative weights `(2,1)` give total `+1`; weights `(1,2)` give `−1`. Neither result is selected by the separate calibrations. Even when all component contrasts have the same sign, the physical magnitude remains unidentified. The default is to retain the identified quantities rather than silently choose weights.

| Commission question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Prevent arbitrary weighting of magnitude/sign? | Yes. Calibration within each component does not identify relative conversion between components. |
| Correct vector default? | Yes: tagged components by compatible denomination/root class preserve valid information. The vector records unresolved aggregation, not a competing definition of each E. |
| Conflict with endpoint theorem? | None. Each admitted field retains its own endpoint contrast. No joint scalar has been claimed where none is justified. |
| Conflict with historical Capacity? | None. Existing signed attribution and permanence are preserved. The restriction concerns interpreting heterogeneous entries as one physically denominated scalar. |
| Scalar Capacity only within one common root class? | Yes for that physical interpretation, after any justified conversion into its declared representation. A mathematical historical sum within a chosen model remains a mathematical attribution object even before a physical bridge has been established. |
| Admission rather than settlement? | Yes. Refusal is of the unsupported aggregate physical claim; it neither changes E nor prohibits an action through affordability. |
| Individually calibrated components? | Still insufficient without relative denomination and, for a joint event, valid composition. |
| Institutional weights? | They may define an explicitly institutional score under F's layer separation. They are not physically calibrated EBU and may not modify V, mu or E backwards. |

For clarity, the root-class index is different from the actor index. If `E_(a,n)^(r)` denotes a signed entry already assigned under a declared closing allocation, then

\[
B_a^{(r)}=\sum_n E_{a,n}^{(r)}
\]

is an actor's accumulation in class r. A vector across r describes unresolved denominations. It does not replace the existing vector across actors, and no per-actor state-function or cycle-closure claim follows. F §18's historical path dependence is preserved. Calibration-path independence in candidate 4 is not actor-history independence.

No earlier record is retrospectively recalculated. If a bridge is later justified, an explicitly documented common-denomination view may accompany original records; it does not erase or reprice them. Promotion of the admission rule is not evidence that such a bridge already exists.

**Nonredundancy:** candidate 1 explains why an unsupported sum is inadmissible; candidate 2 states what remains reportable and prevents implicit weights from filling the gap. They belong in one subsection, not two supposedly independent physical axioms.

For `V=phi_1+phi_2`, changing the representation to `(phi_1+g)+(phi_2−g)` leaves V unchanged. The first attributed contrast changes by `g_pre−g_post`. This is SFE §6's cleared obstruction. It neither rules out independently identified physical components nor proves that all decompositions are nonunique; it shows why algebraic closure alone is insufficient to identify an owner.

“Complete” means complete **for the admitted valuation and the claimed event/boundary**. It does not require the entire universe to be valued, or every audit coordinate to have nonzero marginal. F §§7–8 expressly allow an audit-only sink and a valued sink with different scopes. The proposed clause preserves those distinctions: terms required by the declared physical valuation cannot be omitted, but an audit-only coordinate is not silently made valued. A capacitor-storage contrast can remain a correctly scoped source observable without becoming a complete driver–bath actor settlement.

The rule is also neutral about evaluation order. One may compute a complete total from changing factors without first materializing a global potential number; “joint” identifies semantic authority and compatible endpoints. It does not mandate an implementation architecture.

## 7 Root class and calibration closure

**Classification: FOUNDATION for physical compatibility and consistent conversion; detailed mathematical realization remains CONDITIONAL THEORY ONLY.** “Root class” is defensible as a compact name for compatible realizations of one denomination on a declared domain. It does not establish a unique physical root for all nature.

| Commission question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Equivalence class the right interpretation? | Yes at the level of exact, invertible, physically identified representation relations with compatible domains. It distinguishes a quantity's denomination from the apparatus realizing it. |
| Preferable to many independent roots? | For compatible realizations, yes. Genuinely unbridged candidates must still be allowed to remain distinct; terminology cannot make them compatible. |
| Minimum invariant content? | Same physical comparison meaning, compatible orientation/domain, consistent invertible conversion, calibration-path independence and independent physical identification. |
| Affine equations in foundation? | No. They are an important conditional realization of the invariant principle, already proved in RF §6, M §6 and G §9. |
| Path independence and no denomination drift? | Yes for representation changes of the **same comparison**, not arbitrary physical transport histories or changing contexts. |
| Scale-product and offset closure? | Reference the theorem-level criteria in this analysis. Do not copy them into the amendment; offset closure applies to levels, while within-chart contrasts can ignore constant offsets. |
| Empirical uncertainty? | Retain joint uncertainty, shared calibration dependence and validated domains. A noisy measured map is evidence about an exact underlying relation, not an exact equivalence defined by pairwise tolerance. |
| Neutral about selected physical root? | Yes. It neither selects a canonical device/scale nor admits a nonthermal source. |

For exact maps `C_ij(v)=c_ij v+d_ij`, with positive c on the same comparison domain, the retained formal conditions are

\[
c_{ik}=c_{jk}c_{ij},\qquad d_{ik}=c_{jk}d_{ij}+d_{jk}.
\]

A closed calibration walk has scale product one, and composed offset zero when levels are compared. Contrast-scale closure alone does not fix relative level offsets. These statements concern every relevant comparison walk, not just triangles in a sparse graph. They consolidate the cleared theorem; no new proof or acceptance threshold is needed here.

Closure remains insufficient physical evidence: arbitrary positive node numbers `s_i` produce `c_ij=s_j/s_i` with perfect loop closure. Likewise, pairwise tolerance is not transitive: `0`, `0.75 epsilon` and `1.5 epsilon` make adjacent pairs agree within epsilon while the ends do not. The new wording must preserve both limitations. With partial domains, class membership is scoped to the compatible common domain; the foundation need not acquire groupoid machinery or a claim of global overlap.

An unresolved calibration discrepancy blocks the affected common-denomination claim; it is not a proof that the endpoint definition is false. Conversely, forcing a fit to close algebraically cannot confer physical admission.

## 9 Adversarial check using unequal scales

Retain G §14's example, with positive scales and `k_x k_y > kappa^2`, on an open rectangle:

\[
\Phi(x,y)=\tfrac12 k_xx^2+\tfrac12 k_yy^2+\kappa xy,
\quad
\mu_x=\frac{k_xx+\kappa y}{\Lambda_1},\quad
\mu_y=\frac{k_yy+\kappa x}{\Lambda_2}.
\]

When `kappa != 0` and `Lambda_1 != Lambda_2`,

\[
\partial_y\mu_x=\frac{\kappa}{\Lambda_1}
\ne\frac{\kappa}{\Lambda_2}=\partial_x\mu_y.
\]

Thus there is no scalar V with these proposed marginals on that open domain. The counterclockwise unit-square integral is `kappa(1/Lambda_2−1/Lambda_1)`, not zero. The original Phi is a valid scalar; the refusal concerns the **proposed separately normalized marginals**, not the existence of all possible scalars or all physical models of coupled systems.

| Candidate | What it contributes to the refusal |
|---|---|
| 1 Common denomination | Requires a real common interpretation/conversion rather than accepting two dimensionless numbers automatically. Even granting that obligation for the adversarial check does not establish the assembled field. |
| 2 Noncommensurability refusal | Retains tagged values if the relative denomination is unidentified. If denomination has been established, this rule alone is not the integrability test. |
| 3 Joint composition | Requires one physically justified single-valued V consistent with the proposed marginals. The unequal cross derivatives directly refute this proposed assembly. |
| 4 Root class/closure | Prevents inconsistent representation conversions. Identity or perfectly closing calibration maps still cannot cure the missing joint potential. |

**No fifth law is necessary.** Foundation §3 already defines `mu=grad V`; RF §7 and M §7 supply the reconstruction criterion. The proposed candidate-3 sentence explicitly requires consistency with proposed marginals, making the existing definition effective as an admission check. There is no reason to add a new universal curl law to the foundation or to assume simple connectedness when V is already supplied.

Nor does an arbitrary redistribution of the interaction solve the problem. With `Phi=Phi_1+Phi_2+Phi_I`, separately normalizing an assigned fraction lambda gives

\[
V_\lambda=\frac{\Phi_1}{\Lambda_1}+\frac{\Phi_2}{\Lambda_2}
+\left(\frac{\lambda}{\Lambda_1}+\frac{1-\lambda}{\Lambda_2}\right)\Phi_I.
\]

Its dependence on lambda is `(1/Lambda_1−1/Lambda_2) Phi_I`. The scalar is then being changed by an arbitrary decomposition convention, and does not recover both proposed marginals. G's example `k_x=k_y=1`, `kappa=1/2`, `x=y=1`, `Lambda_1=1`, `Lambda_2=2` gives native energy `3/2` but `V_lambda=1+lambda/4`. Candidate 3 prevents treating that convention as independently identified joint physics.

Other adversarial checks preserve the limits: candidate 4 does not certify arbitrary closing node scales; candidate 2 does not reinterpret mixed denominations as a spending prohibition; candidate 3 does not duplicate the shared interaction; and candidate 1 does not assert a universal entropy bridge.

## 10 One declared root class

The programme preference is scientifically defensible with the quantifier **for one physically denominated scalar account**:

```text
one declared denomination and root class
    -> many compatible physical realizations
    -> many separately admitted source fields
    -> one denomination for their admitted values
```

There is an additional obligation before a particular simultaneous physical total can be formed: the relevant source contributions must compose one complete compatible joint field. That obligation is not another arrow supplied automatically by root membership.

“One” in this architecture means a common physical coordinate for the scalar account. It does not mean one apparatus, one geographical source, one physical temperature, one constant numerical stiffness, one functional form, or one physical object governing all systems. Nor does it mean that nature has only one possible root class or that every valid source will eventually join the selected class.

The terminology should distinguish:

- **Root contract:** the declared quantity meaning, orientation, domain and requirements for physical realization.
- **Root class:** physically compatible realizations of that denomination under the justified exact relations on their common domain.
- **Root realization:** a particular physical/calibration route implementing the contract, with its own qualification and uncertainty.
- **Root candidate:** a proposed contract or proposed membership not yet established.

This usage is preferable to calling mutually compatible instruments independent roots. It preserves the option of distinct unresolved candidates and vector accounts. No new foundation sentence should state that a unique universal root has been empirically selected. The candidate wording in §12 uses the weaker, sufficient statement that one declared denomination may have many compatible realizations.

## 12 Exact proposed foundation wording

The following is the **entire proposed insertion**, not an instruction executed by this task. The four labels identify the cleared candidate content inside one subsection; they do not create four new independent physical axioms. Detailed equations are intentionally left in the existing theorem reports.

```markdown
## 2.1 PHYSICAL ADMISSION AND COMMON DENOMINATION

These conditions govern claims of physical denomination and composition.
They do not change the conditional mathematics for a declared `V`, establish
its physical origin, or select a particular root realization, physical scale
or ensemble.

**Common denomination.** Historical or simultaneous contributions may be
combined as one physical scalar EBU account only after justified conversion
into its declared common denomination. The conversion must preserve physical
quantity meaning and orientation on the stated state, context and comparison
domains, through quantity-kind compatibility or an independently justified
physical bridge. Dimensionlessness alone is insufficient.

**Noncommensurability refusal.** Where a relative denomination is not
justified, retain tagged components by compatible denomination rather than
claiming an unqualified physical scalar EBU total. This is an admission and
aggregation boundary, not a new settlement definition or an affordability
condition. Historical entries remain as recorded. Institutional weightings
must be identified as institutional scores and must not modify `V`, `μ` or
`E` backwards.

**Joint physical composition.** Common denomination does not establish a
joint potential. Forming one joint physical action value from source
contributions requires one physically justified, single-valued `V` on the
declared complete valued state, boundary and context, consistent with any
proposed marginals. Every source, interaction and boundary contribution
required by that admitted valuation must be included once. Decomposition creates neither additional EBU nor a
unique actor allocation; the factor and accounting rules of §§13–18 apply.

**Root class and calibration consistency.** One declared denomination may
have many physically compatible realizations. At the level of exact,
invertible, physically justified representation relations on a declared
common comparison domain, these realizations form its root class.
Conversions of the same physical comparison must compose consistently and
be independent of calibration path. Algebraic closure does not establish
independent physical identification. Empirical qualification must retain
joint uncertainty and validity domains; pairwise agreement within tolerance
does not by itself define an equivalence relation.
```

This wording makes no affine formula mandatory for raw instruments, no quantum or thermal source universally admissible, and no prescribed physical outcome universally restorative. “Consistent with any proposed marginals” invokes the existing `mu=grad V`; it does not replace it or claim a mechanical response law.

The complete-composition clause is deliberately scoped to the admitted valuation. An audit-only coordinate permitted by F §7 remains audit-only. The final reference to §§13–18 preserves the existing factor/issuance/allocation mathematics without copying its proofs or introducing a second settlement system.
