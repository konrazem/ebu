# Open questions

These questions remain explicit inputs or research tasks. They should not become implementation defaults.

1. What physical or economic condition determines x* for a chosen resource?
2. What measurement or physical relation justifies K and its joule scale? A Gaussian fit identifies K only with the required coordinate, distribution and independently established energy scale.
3. Must references be exactly reachable with the selected whole resource units? What redesigns preserve the intended property?
4. What actual condition permits a new cell/reference to appear, or an empty cell to disappear, while retaining complete conservation?
5. Which physical design makes independent handling possible after division? How do its capability, source restrictions and minimum units scale with 1/K?
6. Which attribution is physically identified for a finite joint event? Complete endpoint closure alone does not assign individual credits.
7. Which actor choice and account-funding assumptions should a particular experiment use?
8. Under matched inputs, requests, budgets and access, when does one organisation enable greater complete beneficial contribution? Fibonacci remains a candidate to compare, not an imposed optimum.
9. Which additional represented state, if any, is physically justified for a separation incentive beyond subsequent useful actions?

The baseline can implement its specified mathematics while leaving these choices explicit. It should decline an unsupported lifecycle or attribution operation instead of guessing its meaning.
