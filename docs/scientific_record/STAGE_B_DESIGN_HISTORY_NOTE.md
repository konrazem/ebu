# Stage-B design history — index note

**STAGE-B DESIGN HISTORY EXISTS BUT IS PAUSED AND NON-AUTHORITATIVE.**

Exploratory Stage-B design work was produced earlier in the programme,
including a first Stage-B preregistration design, a Stage-B skeptic design
failure, an endpoint correction packet, a general V1 continuity theorem, and
p_B / backlog design explorations.

None of it is part of the canonical physical-foundation evidence chain, and
none of it is included in this bundle. It is deliberately excluded so that the
foundation evidence chain contains only Stage-A gates and foundation audits.

That work is valuable historical research and is **not** discarded. It can be
archived separately, under its own provenance, once the foundation bundle has
received repository authority.

Nothing here authorizes resuming, designing or executing Stage B.
