## 1. Purpose and evidence boundary

This candidate prospectively authorizes the design of the complete private
Stage E scientific-validation harness. It does not implement that harness and
does not execute scientific behavior.

Stage E exists to establish that the future scientific machinery is correctly
bound, deterministic, fail-closed, restartable, isolated, auditable, and
computationally understood before any registered scientific outcome is
created or inspected. Its permitted evidence is limited to:

1. mathematical theorem or derivation checks;
2. static or synthetic implementation tests;
3. numerical verification of harness conformance and algorithmic complexity;
4. outcome-blind performance measurements on frozen non-scientific inputs.

None of those is a registered scientific simulation, empirical observation,
scientific result, or independently audited interpretation. Stage E may not
promote its evidence into any stronger class.

During authority drafting, even the future Stage E operations remain
forbidden. No project runner, model, state advance, transform, DAG algorithm,
cache algorithm, benchmark, trajectory, simulation, Gate, registered
configuration, candidate outcome, result, figure, or book may be executed or
inspected. Only the six authority candidate files may be added.

## 8. Direct oracles and optimized-path equality

### 8.1 Boolean Möbius

The direct normative oracle is the independently readable exact subset sum

`I_raw(S) = sum_{T subseteq S} (-1)^(|S|-|T|) E(T)`

The optimized implementation is the in-place bitmask fast Möbius transform.
It must match direct coefficients and reconstruction exactly over all 488
accepted shared cases for `n=0..12`. Tolerance is forbidden. One mismatch
refuses the optimized path and every dependent Stage F route.

The frozen complexity grid contains dimensions `8..18`, five retained fresh
