## 4. F1-F5: atom, divisibility, coordinates, and generators

### F1 — EBU economic atom

An **EBU economic atom** is limiting differential participation in a declared
extent coordinate. For action family `a`, state `z`, extent coordinate `h`,
and positive increment `epsilon`,

\[
G_a(z;h)=\lim_{\epsilon\downarrow0}
\frac{T^a_{\epsilon,h}(z)-z}{\epsilon},
\]

when the right derivative exists in the declared topology.

It is not a smallest positive transaction, a divided person or institution,
or a replacement for a finite accepted transaction, receipt, or settlement.
If the limit does not exist, the finite transaction may remain valid while no
generator is declared.

### F2 — declared extent divisibility

Universal action divisibility is superseded. Divisibility applies only to a
declared extent:

- a typed carrier may be divisible when its domain declares a nonatomic
  carrier;
- physical time or process/route participation may be divisible under its
  coordinate contract;
- dimensionless participation may refine one frozen finite bundle;
- actor identity, permission, acceptance, receipts, institutional decisions,
  product identity, and discrete modes are not divided.

The one-action-per-source rule is not foundational. It may survive only as an
explicit special-case constraint, example, or study design.

### F3 — closed initial extent registry

| Coordinate family | Symbol and unit | Generator unit | Domain condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical time | `t`, `[T]` | state unit per `[T]` | Clock, event order, pending state, and horizon are declared |
| Process or route extent | `s`, declared process/path unit | state unit per `s` | Coordinate and orientation are declared; distance is not automatically burden |
| Typed carrier quantity | `q_r`, `[U_r]` | state unit per `[U_r]` | Carrier is nonatomic and the family is differentiable in `q_r` |
| Dimensionless participation | `rho` in `[0,1]` | state unit | Refers to one immutable finite bundle or process specification |

The registry is closed for this authority. A new coordinate family requires a
later authority amendment. Discrete counts, identities, modes, and acceptance
decisions are not generator coordinates.

### F4 — reparameterization

For a positive `C1` change `tilde h = phi(h)`,

\[
G_{\tilde h}=G_h\frac{dh}{d\tilde h}.
\]

Integrated finite change is invariant only when density and integration
limits transform together. Positive affine changes are automatic unit changes.
A nonlinear `C1` change requires an explicit witness and generally makes an
autonomous generator nonautonomous unless the clock is added to state.
Singular, non-`C1`, orientation-reversing, or non-invertible changes inherit no
generator claim. Orientation reversal requires a separately declared
two-sided or reversible flow.

### F5 — two generator roles

EBU distinguishes:

1. a **quantity-participation generator**, carrying typed carrier flow per
   declared extent; and
2. a **state-transformation generator**, carrying the derivative of the
   complete augmented state, including stock, conversion, loss, burden,
   commitments, delay, queues, and memory when applicable.

A declared constitutive/incidence map must link them. Neither is an
institutional generator; authorization, acceptance, causal assessment, and
settlement remain separate records.

## 5. F6-F8: scaling, hybrid actions, and augmented state

### F6 — loss and efficiency scaling

There is no unqualified implication `L(h) -> 0` implies `eta(h) -> 1`.

- If `eta(h)=1-L(h)/C0` for fixed positive `C0`, then `L(h)->0` is sufficient.
- If `eta(h)=1-L(h)/h`, unit efficiency requires `L(h)=o(h)`.
- If `L(h)=ell h+o(h)` under the second definition, then
  `eta(h)->1-ell`.
- For general carrier `C(h)`, the condition is `L(h)/C(h)->0`.
- An independently defined efficiency requires its own relation and
  continuity assumptions.
- A fixed activation loss is a discrete jump and is not divided through the
  continuous flow.

Every declaration must identify denominator, units, coordinate, limiting
regime, fixed-cost treatment, and whether loss is represented in state/burden
or in a separate non-overlapping process account.

### F7 — hybrid action

An admitted hybrid action declares a discrete mode `y`, a quantity domain such
as `q in {0} union [q_min,q_max]`, one-time activation burden `K(y)`, a
continuous within-mode transformation where regularity exists, exact jump-flow
ordering, and a no-duplication rule for fixed cost. Marginal and KKT reasoning
applies only within a fixed differentiable mode. Mode selection requires a
global, combinatorial, or mixed-integer certificate appropriate to the frozen
problem.

### F8 — commitment-aware state

The complete state must expose every variable that can change future behavior
under an admissible request history. This includes, when relevant, reserves,
commitments, queues, in-transit payloads, pending effects, delays, modes,
topology, clocks, and controller or policy memory. The obligation is
behavioral sufficiency, not a claim that one universal minimal state exists.

Two histories with the same declared state but different future boundary
behavior under the same inputs falsify state completeness.

## 6. F9-F10: lifecycle and recursive equivalence

### F9 — request-to-settlement lifecycle

The order is:

\[
\text{request}
\to\text{physical permission/reservation}
\to\text{quote}
\to\text{institutional acceptance}
\to\text{execution}
\to\text{measurement}
\to\text{physical/group receipt}
\to\text{causal assessment}
\to\text{institutional settlement}.
\]

Requested, permitted, accepted, executed, measured, delivered, causally
attributed, and settled quantities are not interchangeable. Continuous carrier
divisibility does not make acceptance or settlement continuous.

### F10 — exact history-wide boundary equivalence

Snapshot equality and equality of local generators at one state are
insufficient. Detailed subsystem `H` and parent representation `P` are exactly
boundary-equivalent only when, for every admitted request/observation history,
related hidden initial state, and declared horizon, they agree on:

- accept, reject, permission, and reservation behavior;
- typed flows, conversions, loss, outflow, and timing;
- completion, pending commitments, delayed effects, and memory;
- physical receipts and residuals; and
- settlement-visible records when settlement is part of the boundary.

The hidden-state relation must be preserved under evolution. A semiconjugacy
or bisimulation is a sufficient proof form; it is not the only possible
witness. Burden factorization, internal-transfer cancellation, conservation,
and non-overlapping process-account preservation remain separate obligations.

## 7. F11-F12: joint allocation and institutional settlement

### F11 — feasibility-first objective grammar

There is no universal scalar objective. The order is:

1. enforce physical feasibility, conservation, capacity, service, safety, and
   viability constraints;
2. if one scientifically compatible scalar boundary is declared, optimize the
   finite-horizon net group EBU or other named scalar;
3. otherwise retain a typed vector and use a preregistered Pareto,
   lexicographic, or epsilon-constraint rule;
4. declare horizon, uncertainty, existence assumptions, regularity, and a
   deterministic tie rule; and
5. use KKT or marginal equalization only as a conditional certificate for a
   suitable differentiable subproblem.

Proportional rationing is an optional baseline, not a consequence of
divisibility. Proportional fairness is a separate logarithmic objective.

### F12 — mathematical allocation ends before settlement

Physical/mathematical allocation may determine a feasible selected quantity
and mode, an optimization certificate, shadow prices or marginal values, and
an optional decomposition of a selected scalar. It does not determine
entitlement, priority, guarantees, liability, compensation, residual
ownership, or dispute resolution.

For differentiable scalar `C(q)`, the optional radial Aumann-Shapley
decomposition is

\[
S_i=\int_0^1 q_i\frac{\partial C(tq)}{\partial q_i}\,dt.
\]

When `C(0)=0`, its shares close by the chain rule. The path is a declared
accounting convention; it does not choose `q`, prove physical measurement,
identify causality, or create settlement entitlement.

## 8. F13: exact finite interaction

For a finite registered action set `N`, define `E(S)` for every required subset
`S subseteq N` under one frozen subset-evaluation protocol. The finite
interaction coefficient is

\[
I_S=\sum_{R\subseteq S}(-1)^{|S|-|R|}E(R).
\]

The exact inverse is

\[
E(S)=\sum_{R\subseteq S}I_R,
\qquad I_\varnothing=E(\varnothing).
\]

Therefore nonempty-only reconstruction requires either an explicit baseline

\[
E(S)=E(\varnothing)+
\sum_{\varnothing\ne R\subseteq S}I_R
\]

or normalized value `v(S)=E(S)-E(emptyset)`. `E(emptyset)=0` is not assumed
when natural drift, waiting burden, standing commitments, or fixed system cost
occurs in the evaluation window.

The subset protocol freezes the finite ground set, initial augmented state,
boundary, state and burden versions, units, horizon, exogenous history,
meaning of action removal, quantity-fixed or rule-replayed semantics,
constraints and shared-constraint resolver, active modes, losses,
commitments, fixed-cost placement, and complete non-overlapping process
accounts.
If a required subset is inadmissible or meaningless, Boolean-lattice
interaction is undefined and validation must refuse it. A feasible-set poset
may be proposed later, but it may not reuse the Boolean alternating-sign rule
without its own Möbius proof.

`FiniteSetInteractionWitness` is distinct from
`SameBaselineNonadditivityWitness` for `N_G` and
`SerialComparatorInteractionWitness` for `I_{G|pi}`. A Harsanyi/Möbius
coefficient is not automatically an aggregated interaction index.

The exact order-`k` truncation residual is

\[
R_{>k}(S)=E(S)-E(\varnothing)
-\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\ne R\subseteq S\\|R|\le k}}I_R
=\sum_{\substack{R\subseteq S\\|R|>k}}I_R.
\]

This is algebraic closure, not physical conservation.

## 9. F14: local mixed marginals and order witnesses

For continuous quantity vector `q`, the rectangular mixed difference is

\[
\Delta_i\Delta_jE(q)=
E(q+\delta_i e_i+\delta_j e_j)-E(q+\delta_i e_i)
-E(q+\delta_j e_j)+E(q).
\]

If `E` is `C2` on the whole rectangle,

\[
\Delta_i\Delta_jE(q)=
\int_0^{\delta_i}\int_0^{\delta_j}
\partial_{ij}E(q+s e_i+t e_j)\,dt\,ds.
\]

The Hessian cross term is local, not a globally exact finite interaction law.
At activation thresholds, topology changes, projections, discrete modes, or
active-set switches, the smooth witness is inapplicable unless a separately
authorized generalized derivative is declared.

With state-map sign convention,

\[
B_{ij}=[G_i,G_j]_s=DG_i\,G_j-DG_j\,G_i.
\]

For sufficiently smooth local flows,

\[
T_i^h\circ T_j^h(z)-T_j^h\circ T_i^h(z)
=h^2B_{ij}(z)+O(h^3).
\]

`MixedMarginalWitness` and `CommutatorWitness` are optional. Each must declare
coordinates, units, domain, active mode, regularity, tolerances, sign
convention, composition orientation where applicable, remainder meaning, and
process-account terms. A bracket vanishing at one state does not prove
neighborhood commutativity. Hybrid jumps, projections, and unbounded operators
require separate domain rules.

## 10. F15: shared constraints and interaction topology

Shared constraints are owned at the lowest complete common boundary whose
state and authority contain the complete constraint. This is an EBU
architectural axiom, not a theorem of optimization.

It is conditionally sufficient when the hierarchy has a unique least common
ancestor, that boundary observes every demand and relevant hidden state, units
and timing are compatible, authority is present, and child interfaces are
history-equivalent. In a DAG or overlapping authority structure, a unique
lowest boundary may not exist; a separately proved factor boundary or
distributed protocol is then required.

The prospective topology is a typed, state-dependent action hypergraph:

- vertices are registered action instances;
- pair edges carry pair interaction coefficients;
- hyperedges carry coefficients for subsets of size at least three;
- factor nodes carry shared capacities, commitments, or authority constraints;
- boundary nodes identify the scope of evaluation or hidden interaction.

Structural interaction topology records possible dependencies. Active
interaction topology records current nonzero interactions and binding factors.
Physical transport topology records actual carrier adjacency or propagation
structure. Institutional constraint topology records authorization, capacity,
or governance coupling and may exist without physical adjacency. None may be
silently substituted for another. A pure three-way term is sufficient to show
that a pair graph can be incomplete.

## 11. F16: boundary invariance and claim limits

If detailed subsystem `H` and parent `P` satisfy `E_H(R)=E_P(R)` for every
exposed subset under the identical initial-state relation, boundary semantics,
subset policy, horizon, exogenous history, active modes, flow/jump semantics,
burden factorization, commitments, losses, and complete process accounts, then
their Möbius coefficients agree for every exposed subset. This follows from
linearity and is not claimed as new mathematics. The empty-baseline
information is required: matching all coefficients including the empty
coefficient reconstructs matching subset values.

External or quotient interaction topology may be preserved while internal
topology is hidden. Internal child identity or factor structure is preserved
only when explicitly exported.

Mandatory nonclaims are:

- interaction is not causality;
- marginal interaction is not entitlement;
- an interaction coefficient is not a settlement entitlement;
- shadow prices are not automatically settlement prices;
- zero pair coefficients do not imply full separability;
- graph reachability does not by itself prove physical propagation;
- oscillation or complex eigenvalues do not establish a transport mechanism;
- higher-order interaction does not establish propagation;
- commutators and order effects are not automatically physical superposition;
- and `V` is a declared EBU burden, not thermodynamic entropy.

Electrical-voltage terminology and analogy are excluded from EBU's active and
future programme. They receive no framework declaration, validation feature,
book treatment, scientific protocol, or reserved route back into this
authority.

Wave and phase-interference models, tests, teaching obligations, and research
programmes are outside this authority. This amendment makes no such claim,
assigns no such work to a framework stage or book, and creates no requirement
to pursue it. Any later proposal would require new and separately authorized
scientific justification rather than continuation under this package.

This exclusion does not remove the parallel-action interaction programme.
F13-F16 retain exact finite nonadditivity, positive and negative pair
interaction, pure higher-order interaction, local mixed-marginal coupling,
generator-order effects, shared-constraint factor topology, hypergraph
topology, and boundary interaction invariance. These remain required
foundations for later interaction typology declarations and tests. In this
package, `phase interference` means a separately evidenced physical
superposition phenomenon; it is not a synonym for parallel-action interaction.

## 12. Narrow prospective I3C causality/settlement repair

The framework specification §13.5 correctly permits numeric institutional
shares when child causality is `UNIDENTIFIED`, provided the shares are not
represented as causal facts and an explicit residual closes the physical group
measurement. Current I3 authority, mechanical predicate, validation corpus,
and `src/ebu_framework/settlement.py` instead reject any nonempty share closure
under non-`IDENTIFIED` causality.

The prospective corrected rule is:

1. Non-`IDENTIFIED` causal status forbids numeric causal-contribution claims.
2. It does not forbid independently authorized institutional settlement
   shares.
3. Every settlement share must reference an institutional `rule_ref`; its
   evidence must not be typed or described as causal-identification evidence
   unless causality is actually `IDENTIFIED`.
4. Child `causal_contribution_ref` remains explicitly unresolved or not
   applicable when causality is not identified.
5. Child `settlement_share_ref`, group settlement linkage, numeric shares, and
   a present arithmetic closure may exist independently.
6. Measured group total equals share total plus explicit residual under the
   accepted unit and arithmetic rules.
7. `CAUSAL_ATTRIBUTION_UNRESOLVED` fires for a numeric or linked causal claim
   under non-identified status, or for a settlement record that falsely labels
   an institutional share as causal. It does not fire solely because an
   institutional share or present settlement closure exists.

This is a prospective semantic repair only. It does not modify the accepted
I3 implementation, contract, 544-vector validation corpus, failure ordering,
or the current fail-closed runtime. Until a later, separately authorized
implementation and audit complete, the current validator remains controlling
for executable framework behavior and the affected noncausal-share path
remains unavailable. No caller may bypass it.
