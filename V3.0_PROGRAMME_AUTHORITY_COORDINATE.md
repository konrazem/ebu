### E2 - Is there a registered per-action field decomposition at `m >= 2`?

**Proposition.** For an accepted simultaneous group `G` with `|G| >= 2`,
resolved to `rho(G)` with successor `x_G`, does a **registered** per-action
field receipt `R_a^V` exist with `sum_{a in G} R_a^V = V(z) - V(x_G)` exactly,
and under which declared path convention?

**Status today.** The identity is a **theorem at `m = 1` only** (Thm 8.2 under
(T3); Obs 6.12). At `m >= 2` the record offers scheme 2 (proportional to
`q_acc`, "candidate default") and scheme 4 (radial Aumann-Shapley, "registered
refinement, not required"), with the general case open as **16.O3**.

**Alternatives.** (a) adopt scheme 2; (b) adopt scheme 4 **and** record that its
path is a declared accounting convention, never a measured physical path, per
atomic F12; (c) register that `m >= 2` remains unauthorized, leaving Decision
10.3 in force.

**Consequences.** Under (a) or (b) a per-action receipt becomes implementable and
`O3` narrows. Under (c) — which requires no new mathematics and is available
