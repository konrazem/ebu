## 5. Canonical-equivalence candidate and theorem

### 5.1 Layered object

**[PA]** Define a versioned finite typed relational structure

\[
\Theta=(P,\mathcal C,\mathcal O,\mathcal H,\mathcal B),
\]

**[PA]** where \(P\) records prerequisites and admissible configurations; \(\mathcal C\) records shared-factor incidence; \(\mathcal O\) records precedence, concurrency, and accepted commutation evidence; \(\mathcal H\) records structural pair/higher-order support and references to frozen interaction declarations; and \(\mathcal B\) records parent, port, boundary, and dependency structure.

**[EA]** The five layers remain distinguishable in the byte schema even when the same vertex pair appears in multiple layers.

**[PA]** Local vertex names are the only labels permuted; semantic types, roles, units, versions, factor kinds, ordered child roles, boundary-port meanings, and referenced protocol digests remain colors and are never erased.

**[EA]** Measured subset values, EBU, interaction coefficients, benchmark outcomes, timing observations, or scores do not enter \(\Theta\) or its topology identity; a measured active-topology declaration may be referenced only as versioned structural evidence, not copied as performance.

### 5.2 Faithful encoding

**[PA]** Let `Serialize_v` be a prefix-free, injective encoding for schema version (v), built over ECJ-1-compatible normalized data: unordered semantic sets are deduplicated and sorted by canonical bytes, while genuinely ordered histories and ordered child roles retain their order.

**[PA]** Reject unknown fields, dangling vertices, duplicate relations, illegal self-relations, cycles where the relation is declared a strict order, layer/type mismatches, malformed versions, and noncanonical input rather than guessing intent.

**[PA]** For (n) permutable vertices define

\[
\operatorname{Can}_v(\Theta)
=
\min_{\pi\in S_n}\operatorname{Serialize}_v(\pi\Theta),
\]

**[IC]** with lexicographic byte order and exhaustive permutation only within a separately frozen small-(n) bound; larger-(n) algorithms are a future performance question and must return “unsupported” rather than a heuristic identity.

### 5.3 Proof obligations and result

**[CT]** **Relabelling invariance.** If \(\sigma\in S_n\), then

\[
\operatorname{Can}_v(\sigma\Theta)
=\min_{\pi\in S_n}\operatorname{Serialize}_v(\pi\sigma\Theta)
=\min_{\rho\in S_n}\operatorname{Serialize}_v(\rho\Theta)
=\operatorname{Can}_v(\Theta),
\]

**[CT]** because right multiplication by fixed \(\sigma\) bijects \(S_n\) to itself.

**[CT]** **Input-order invariance.** If two inputs differ only in the enumeration order of relations declared unordered, normalization produces the same sorted relation bytes before the minimum; fields declared ordered are intentionally not covered by this invariance.

**[CT]** **Isomorphism result.** If \(\Theta'=\sigma\Theta\) as a color- and layer-preserving isomorphism, relabelling invariance gives \(\operatorname{Can}_v(\Theta')=\operatorname{Can}_v(\Theta)\); if `Serialize_v` is faithful, equality of canonical bytes also yields an isomorphism by composing the two minimizing permutations.

**[CT]** **Schema separation.** If the encoded preamble includes an unambiguous topology domain and exact schema version, byte strings from different domains or versions cannot be equal under the schema’s disjoint prefix rule.

**[CT]** **Deterministic structural identity.** A digest of `domain || version || byte_length || Can_v(Theta)` is deterministic and structural provided the digest domain and algorithm are frozen; collision resistance is a cryptographic assumption, not mathematical injectivity.

**[PA]** Introduce a topology-specific typed identifier rather than directly reusing `compute_object_content_hash`, because the latter correctly includes ordinary object identity/schema material that may retain local names and therefore does not itself prove relabelling invariance.

**[EA]** Equality of topology identity means equality of the represented layered structure under the frozen schema; it never means equal feasibility, service, EBU, causal effect, institutional value, or benchmark performance.

## 6. Fibonacci component-count proof

**[PA]** Let \(M_0\) and \(M_1\) be distinct base motif definitions with one primitive occurrence each, and let

\[
M_{n+1}=\mathcal C(M_n,M_{n-1}),
\qquad
N(M_0)=N(M_1)=1,
\]

**[PA]** where \(\mathcal C\) is an ordered two-child composition and \(N(\mathcal C(X,Y))=N(X)+N(Y)\).

**[AM]** With \(F_1=F_2=1\), \(N(M_n)=F_{n+1}\) for every \(n\ge 0\).

**[AM]** The base cases are \(N(M_0)=1=F_1\) and \(N(M_1)=1=F_2\); if the equality holds at \(k\) and \(k-1\), then \(N(M_{k+1})=F_{k+1}+F_k=F_{k+2}\), completing induction.

**[AM]** Through level \(n\), the recursive definition DAG has \(n+1\) distinct motif-level nodes \(M_0,\ldots,M_n\), while the fully expanded occurrence tree for \(M_n\) contains \(F_{n+1}\) primitive occurrences.

**[EA]** Definition identity and occurrence identity must remain separate: changing a shared motif definition can affect every alias, while changing one physical occurrence requires a new instance or definition identity.

## 7. Conditional recursive-compression theorem

### 7.1 Exact assumptions

| Assumption | Required condition |
|---|---|
| **[PA] A1 — identity** | Every motif definition, instance, ordered child role, topology schema, boundary protocol, and composition rule is immutable and version-addressed. |
| **[PA] A2 — sufficiency** | \(B_n=B(M_n)\) is sufficient for every declared external query over the frozen initial augmented state, boundary, horizon, and admissible external history. |
| **[PA] A3 — complete composition** | \(\Phi(B_n,B_{n-1})\) deterministically preserves carrier and unit accounts, conversion and loss, reserves, commitments, queues, delays, memory, modes, burden, process accounts, receipts, residuals, and settlement-visible records. |
| **[PA] A4 — closure** | No undeclared cross-boundary factor, constraint, hyperedge, observation, or dependency reaches hidden internals; any such relation is represented in the exposed signature. |
| **[PA] A5 — interaction preservation** | If interaction reuse is claimed, the complete exposed-subset protocol, removal semantics, outcomes, and Möbius coefficients satisfy accepted F16 equality requirements. |
| **[PA] A6 — cache key** | The key separates topology identity, motif/instance identity, boundary and state versions, initial/history digest, horizon, numerical policy, composition version, and evidence/provenance; performance remains a cached result, not identity. |
| **[PA] A7 — dependencies** | A complete dependency DAG records definition aliases, occurrence instances, parent links, evidence, corrections, and invalidation status. |
| **[PA] A8 — equivalence** | Reuse is backed by complete history-wide boundary equivalence, not a snapshot, endpoint, local commutator, or visual motif resemblance. |

### 7.2 Theorem and proof sketch

**[CT]** Under A1–A8, if \(B_0\) and \(B_1\) are certified and

\[
B_{k+1}=\Phi(B_k,B_{k-1}),
\]

**[CT]** then \(B_2,\ldots,B_n\) can be computed by one new \(\Phi\) application per recursive level without reopening certified internal primitive calculations.

**[CT]** The proof is induction: A2–A5 make each certified child summary observationally substitutable for its hidden internals, A1/A6 select the same semantics, and A7/A8 preserve the dependency and equivalence evidence; hence the parent computation consumes only the two certified summaries, and the result is again sufficient by A3.

**[CT]** If composition cost at level \(k\) is \(c_k\), total new work after the bases is

\[
T(n)=T_0+T_1+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}c_k.
\]

**[CT]** If \(c_k\le C\), then \(T(n)=O(n)\); retaining every certified level and its provenance costs \(O(n)\) cache records, while an \(O(1)\) rolling-memory claim is valid only when correction, audit, alias, and later-reuse obligations do not require retained ancestors.

**[CT]** More generally, if summary size \(s_k=O(k^d)\) and \(\Phi\) costs \(O((s_k+s_{k-1})^p)\), then \(T(n)=O(n^{dp+1})\), subject to the output-size lower bound and the same sufficiency assumptions.

**[FT]** If summary size or composition cost grows polynomially in \(F_{n+1}\) rather than in the level \(n\), the claimed exponential-in-level compression does not follow and must be restated using the measured size bound.

### 7.3 Reuse, correction, and reopening

**[CT]** A correction to one occurrence invalidates only nodes reachable from that occurrence in the dependency DAG; in the simple Fibonacci level chain, a correction at level \(j\) invalidates dependent ancestors \(j+1,\ldots,n\) but not unrelated components.

**[CT]** A correction to a shared definition invalidates every dependent alias; an instance-specific correction must fork the instance or definition identity and cannot silently mutate a shared cache entry.

**[CT]** On the full subset lattice, with

\[
I_T=\sum_{R\subseteq T}(-1)^{|T|-|R|}E(R),
\]

**[AM]** changing exactly \(E(Q)\) by \(\delta\) changes

\[
\Delta I_T=
\begin{cases}
(-1)^{|T|-|Q|}\delta,&Q\subseteq T,\\
0,&Q\not\subseteq T.
\end{cases}
\]

**[AM]** because \(E(Q)\) occurs in the defining sum for \(I_T\) exactly when \(Q\subseteq T\); the affected coefficients form the upward cone of \(Q\).

**[CT]** On a separately accepted feasible poset, the corresponding change is \(\mu(Q,T)\delta\) only for comparable \(Q\preceq T\), with the result conditional on the poset’s own Möbius definition and closure proof.

**[CT]** Existing exposed interaction coefficients remain reusable only when their complete exposed subset values and semantics are unchanged; otherwise coefficients outside the mathematical upward cone remain algebraically unaffected, but boundary/evidence changes can still require revalidation.

**[EA]** Hidden cross-boundary factors, instance changes, altered initial state/history/horizon, new ports, unrecorded queues or commitments, changed removal semantics, or failed conservation/burden/settlement equivalence force reopening or signature enlargement.

## 8. Recursive interaction diagnostic \(J_n\)

**[PA]** Under one frozen protocol define normalized finite EBU and recursive surplus by

\[
R_n=E(M_n)-E(\varnothing),
\qquad
J_n=R_{n+1}-R_n-R_{n-1}.
\]

**[AM]** \(J_n=0\) if and only if \(R_{n+1}=R_n+R_{n-1}\), by rearrangement of the definition.

**[CT]** Under common boundary, units, state, history, removal, feasibility, preservation, resolver, loss, process-account, commitment, and settlement semantics, \(J_n>0\) is a positive same-protocol recursive surplus and \(J_n<0\) is a negative same-protocol recursive surplus.

**[EH]** Interpreting a positive value as cooperation or a negative value as destruction, congestion, incompatibility, loss, or shared-constraint burden is domain-dependent and requires mechanism-specific evidence; the sign alone is not a causal or physical decomposition.

**[CT]** The Fibonacci recurrence for \(R_n\) is valid across a declared range exactly when all corresponding \(J_n\) vanish under the frozen protocol; motif occurrence counts obeying Fibonacci recurrence do not imply this EBU recurrence.

**[EH]** Limits such as \(J_n\to J^\ast\), \(J_n/R_n\to\alpha\), scale-normalized \(J_n/F_{n+1}\), sign stability, and periodicity are candidate diagnostics, not accepted findings.

**[FT]** Any ratio diagnostic must declare a nonzero denominator domain, convergence mode, error/tolerance policy, observation range, comparator families, and multiple-comparison control before outcomes are inspected.

## 9. Exact relationship to the \(2^N\) problem

**[AM]** A complete Boolean subset table on \(N=F_{n+1}\) primitive occurrences contains \(2^{F_{n+1}}\) subset entries.

| Reduction | Exact benefit | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **[CT]** Canonical equivalence | Compute one representative per color- and layer-preserving relabelling orbit. | It does not merge nonisomorphic structures or equalize their performance. |
| **[FT]** Feasible posets | Replace invented inadmissible subsets by a separately defined admissible family of size \(\lvert\mathcal Q\rvert\). | The feasible domain and its Möbius calculus need their own proof; missing outcomes cannot be silently imputed. |
| **[CT]** Sparse interaction topology | Evaluate only declared/evidenced hyperedges when a theorem establishes omitted terms as zero or irrelevant. | Sparsity is not established by failure to measure an edge. |
| **[CT]** Recursive boundary compression | For the certified Fibonacci family, replace expanded primitive reevaluation by \(n-1\) new \(\Phi\) calls after two bases under the stated size/cost conditions. | Only queries factoring through sufficient summaries are compressed. |
| **[AM]** Möbius upward-cone locality | A correction at subset \(Q\) changes only supersets \(T\supseteq Q\); on a Boolean lattice the cone has \(2^{N-\lvert Q\rvert}\) members. | Locality does not imply smallness, especially when \(\lvert Q\rvert\) is small. |
| **[CT]** Structural additivity | If accepted factorization assumptions partition independent blocks, cross-block terms vanish and separate tables can cost \(\sum_i2^{\lvert B_i\rvert}\) instead of \(2^N\). | Every independence, boundary, constraint, history, and account assumption must be proved. |

**[CT]** The strong result is therefore limited but material: \(n+1\) certified motif levels can represent an \(F_{n+1}\)-occurrence expansion and answer all queries proved to factor through \(B_n\), while unrepresented cross-boundary subset questions still retain arbitrary exponential complexity.

**[IC]** Present this as a theorem-backed example showing how structured recursive systems can avoid exhaustive primitive-subset recomputation, never as a universal polynomial-time solution for arbitrary action families.
