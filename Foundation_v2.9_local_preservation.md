## 1. Motivation and the original project objective

The Energy Balance Project asks whether **purely local physical rules** can
keep a resource field alive — no central optimizer, no global objective, no
simulated future. Since V2.0 the model has been a scalar stock field on a
graph: each cell has a viable band, cells exchange stock over lossy edges, and
"health" is measured by a quadratic burden functional that is **never
optimized by the engine** — survival must emerge or fail on its own.

The project's stated long-term objective (V2.9 objective-alignment draft, §1)
is not maximal service or comfort. It is an **honest physical sustainability
signal**, in this priority order: (1) preserve the regenerative and planetary
foundation; (2) calculate and report the true sustainable allowance; (3)
record ecological debt whenever consumption exceeds that allowance; (4)–(6)
only then build the EBU accounting and economic layers on top. Two guardrails
constrain every future layer: **no laundering by price** (cost is never
permission for irreversible harm) and **no laundering by transfer** (moving
tokens redistributes responsibility; it does not reduce planetary debt).

V2.9 is the stage where item (1) — *preservation* — first gets a concrete,
testable, purely local mechanism, and item (2) gets its first concrete
formula: a **safe-export budget** a source can compute from its own frozen
state. Everything monetary remains deliberately unbuilt (§13).

**The central question of V2.9:**

> Can a spatially local process determine how much a regenerative source may
> safely export, preserve its certified reserve, and reveal infeasible demand
> — without evaluating the whole world or simulating its future?

The deterministic answer, in the fixtures tested here, is **yes for one-step
reserve preservation and honest rationing**, with sharp, explicitly recorded
limits: feasibility is a separate property that no export rule can create, and
nothing here proves long-horizon or stochastic viability (§14, §15).

## 2. What was known at V2.8

V2.7 (continuous time) proved an energy–dissipation identity for the idealized
Onsager transport law: along solutions of the continuous flow,

    dV/dt = Σ_i μ_i u_i − Σ_e ( J_e²/M_e + θ_e J_e ),

where `V` is the total burden (a sum of per-cell quadratic penalties), `μ_i =
∂V/∂x_i` is the local marginal, `u_i` is the local natural drive (supply +
regeneration − demand − leakage), and each edge `e` with mobility `M_e`,
threshold `θ_e`, and efficiency `η_e` carries flux `J_e = M_e [μ_i − η_e μ_j −
θ_e]_+`. Transport can only dissipate; only the drive can raise the burden.

V2.8 (discrete time) crossed half the continuous→discrete gap. For **Model
D0** — the *synchronous, frozen-state, unconstrained, loss-aware* explicit
Euler law

    x^{n+1} = x^n + Δt ( u(x^n) + S J(x^n) ),

it proved a finite-step burden inequality (V2.8 Theorem 4.4/6.1):

    V(x^{n+1}) − V(x^n) ≤ Δt μᵀu − Δt Σ_e ( J_e²/M_e + θ_e J_e )
                          + (L_V Δt²/2) ‖u + S J‖²,

drive minus dissipation plus an explicit step-size penalty, with a corrected
curvature constant `L_V` that **sums** simultaneously active penalty weights.
Crucially, V2.8 §11 lists what the theorem does **not** cover: clipping,
projection, spill, saturation, hard-reserve constraints, sequential live-state
updates. **Any constrained flow is outside the V2.8 theorem.** That exclusion
is exactly where V2.9 begins: a preservation controller must constrain flows,
so it must bring its own mathematics.

## 3. Why soft penalties are not hard preservation

The burden functional can include a reserve term `χ_i [R_i − x_i]_+²` that
penalizes dipping below a reserve `R_i`. It shapes the *force*: as the source
falls toward `R`, the reserve marginal `−2χ (R − x)_+` pushes against export.
But it is a **soft** mechanism, and the algebra says why it cannot guarantee
anything: the penalty and its derivative both **vanish at `x = R` itself**
(objective-alignment draft, Observation 5.1). At the boundary there is no
force left; one more finite tick of demand steps straight through. A soft
penalty can *delay* damage — and in D9 it measurably does (§10) — but it
cannot *forbid* it. Hard preservation needs a constraint, not a gradient.

## 4. The safe-export budget

Freeze one source cell at tick start: stock `x`, local drive `u`, timestep
`Δt`, certified effective reserve `R_eff`. If the source exports an aggregate
rate `Q` for one tick (and we ignore incoming flow, which only helps), its
successor is `x' = x + Δt(u − Q)`. Requiring `x' ≥ R_eff` and solving for `Q`
gives the largest reserve-safe aggregate export rate:

    Q_max = [ x + Δt·u − R_eff ]_+ / Δt.

With explicit physical measurement margins `ε_x` (stock) and `ε_u` (drive),
the **robust budget** (protocol Amendment 4, A4.5) is

    Q_max^rob = [ (x − ε_x) + Δt(u − ε_u) − R_eff ]_+ / Δt.

Every symbol is local: the source's own stock, its own drive, its own reserve
certificate, the tick length. No global state, no other cell's stock, no
future rollout. Two structural points matter (both from the independent
Gate-2.1B review, which corrected the first design):

- **The budget governs the sum, not each edge.** Giving the full budget to
  each of `k` edges would let the source export `k·Q_max`. The budget is
  aggregate per source, then shared among its edges (§5).
- **Feasibility is a separate hypothesis.** `Q_max = 0` does not mean "safe";
  it means "no export is allowed". If natural decline alone crosses the
  reserve — `x + Δt·u < R_eff` — then even zero export cannot preserve the
  boundary. The review's Observation 4.2 gives the counterexample: `g ≡ 0`,
  `u = −δ`, `x = R_eff` gives `x' = R_eff − Δt·δ < R_eff` at `Q = 0`. A
  controller must *report* that state, not claim to have prevented it.

This yields the action-time state classification (all from frozen local data):

| State | Condition | Export |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **P** (preservable) | `x ≥ R_eff` and `x + Δt·u ≥ R_eff` | up to `Q_max^rob` (regenerative sources) |
| **R** (recovery required) | `x < R_eff` | zero |
| **I** (locally infeasible) | `x ≥ R_eff` but `x + Δt·u < R_eff` | zero — and zero cannot save the boundary; reported explicitly |
| **F** (flow source) | external renewable flow | declared flow cap, no stock reserve |

Finite and irreversible stocks get a **zero** preservation-safe budget even in
state P: positive extraction of a non-regenerating stock is depletion, not
preservation.

## 5. Aggregate multi-edge allocation

When the source's edges request more than the budget — aggregate raw request
`Q_req = Σ_e q_e^req` exceeding `Q_max^rob` — P1C scales **all** of that
source's outgoing requests by one factor computed from frozen state:

    σ = min( 1, Q_max^rob / Q_req ),        q_e^acc = σ · q_e^req.

Proportional scaling keeps the decision local (one number per source), treats
edges symmetrically, and — because it is computed once from the frozen state,
not sequentially against live state — composes with the synchronous update
without ordering artifacts. Raw requests `q_e^req` are the unmodified
loss-aware D0 fluxes (`J = M[μ_i − η μ_j − θ]_+`), reused, not forked.

Uncommitted **incoming** flow is deliberately excluded from the budget: a
source must stay safe even if every upstream neighbor withholds this tick.
Delivered incoming flow appears only as a diagnostic.

## 6. The P1C tick sequence

One P1C tick (`p1c_v29.p1c_step`), in frozen order:

1. **Freeze** the state vector and compute each cell's local drive `u_i` from
   its own frozen stock (D0 semantics, unchanged).
2. **Gather** raw loss-aware edge requests `q_e^req` from frozen local views
   (the D0 local law, unchanged).
3. **Classify** each configured source (P/R/I/F) and compute its one
   aggregate safe-export budget `Q_max^rob` (§4).
4. **Scale** that source's requests proportionally when they exceed the
   budget (§5): `q_e^acc = σ q_e^req`.
5. **Apply** all accepted flows in one simultaneous update:
   `x_i^{n+1} = x_i + Δt ( u_i + Σ_in η_e q_e^acc − Σ_out q_e^acc )` —
   the source loses the full withdrawal `q_e^acc`; the destination receives
   only `η_e q_e^acc`.
6. **Count service after transport loss**: delivered service is `η_e
   q_e^acc`, never the pre-loss withdrawal.
7. **Report unmet demand explicitly**: the gap between requested and
   delivered service is a first-class output, never renamed and never hidden.

Everything on the decision path reads frozen local data only. The global
functional `V` is computed *after* the update, by the researcher harness, as
a diagnostic — never by the controller (enforced by AST tests).

## 7. The one-step preservation result

**Theorem (one-step aggregate reserve preservation; Gate-2.1B review, Thm
4.1).** Fix a tick. Suppose a protected regenerative source `i` satisfies, on
its frozen state:

1. `x_i ≥ R_i^eff` (at or above its certified reserve), and
2. `x_i + Δt·u_i ≥ R_i^eff` (its no-export successor remains feasible —
   state P), and
3. its aggregate accepted export satisfies
   `Q_i^acc ≤ Q_{i,max} = [ x_i + Δt·u_i − R_i^eff ]_+ / Δt`,

and the synchronous update of §6 step 5 is applied. Then

    x_i^{n+1} ≥ R_i^eff.

*Proof.* Incoming flow is non-negative, so
`x_i^{n+1} = x_i + Δt(u_i + Σ_in η q^acc − Q_i^acc) ≥ x_i + Δt·u_i − Δt·Q_i^acc`.
By hypothesis 3, `Δt·Q_i^acc ≤ [x_i + Δt·u_i − R_i^eff]_+`, which under
hypothesis 2 equals `x_i + Δt·u_i − R_i^eff`. Hence
`x_i^{n+1} ≥ x_i + Δt·u_i − (x_i + Δt·u_i − R_i^eff) = R_i^eff`. ∎

With the robust budget `Q_max^rob` the same algebra holds whenever the true
stock and drive lie within the declared margins `ε_x, ε_u` of the measured
values. The P1C implementation satisfies hypothesis 3 by construction
(`σ ≤ Q_max^rob / Q_req`), so every state-P tick is an instance of the
theorem; the harness records per-tick conformance (`theorem_eligible`,
`theorem_ok`).

**What this theorem is not.** It is a *one-step* algebraic statement under
explicit hypotheses. It is **not**: an infinite-horizon sustainability
theorem; a proof of global homeostasis; a proof for arbitrary (asynchronous,
sequential) actor scheduling; a proof under uncertain or mis-specified
ecological models beyond the declared `ε` margins; a proof of complete
service (P1C rations precisely when demand is unsafe); or an EBU accounting
theorem (no EBU exists in V2.9). Feasibility (hypothesis 2) is an assumption,
not a conclusion: in state I, zero export cannot guarantee preservation, and
P1C reports that state rather than masking it.

**Relationship to V2.8.** The V2.8 finite-step inequality holds for the
*unconstrained* D0 flux; capping the aggregate export changes the flux, so
that theorem does **not** transfer to P1C (`covered_by_v28_theorem = False`
on every P1C tick record). P1C carries its own one-step reserve algebra
(above) plus numerical conformance checks (`test_v29_p1c.py`, 83 checks).
The **joint** theorem — constrained preservation *and* a dissipation/descent
inequality for the capped flux — remains open (§15).

## 8. Feasibility: recovery-required and infeasible states

The P/R/I/F classifier (§4) makes the negative cases first-class outputs:

- **State R** (`x < R_eff`): the reserve is already breached. Export is zero.
  Recovery is up to the source's own regeneration; P1C makes no recovery
  claim.
- **State I** (`x ≥ R_eff`, `x + Δt·u < R_eff`): natural decline alone will
  cross the reserve this tick. Export is zero *and* the controller reports
  `zero_export_insufficient = true`. This is the honest-signal case: the
  world's demand structure is infeasible for this source, and no local export
  rule can fix it — the information must go up to whatever layer sets demand
  (in these toy worlds, nothing; in the intended architecture, the future
  regulatory/economic layer).

D10's demand axis deliberately crosses both boundaries: `d/g_max = 1.0` is
the logistic fold (maximum sustainable regeneration), and delivering demand
`d` through an edge of efficiency `η` requires `d ≤ η·g_max`. Points with
`η < d/g_max < 1` are regeneration-feasible but transport-infeasible: the
committed map (§11) shows P1C rationing exactly there — hypothesis H-D10-1,
which held at all tested points.

## 9. D1–D8: deterministic validation of the substrate

Before any preservation experiment, V2.9 Gate 2 ran a preregistered
deterministic "wind tunnel" (plan hash `af8f119b…`, 24 runs, fixtures D1–D8)
on the frozen D0 engine `d0_v29.py`, comparing P0 (no transport), P1 (exact
D0), P1K (diagnostic projection wrapper), and ablations P2 (loss-blind force),
P3 (sequential live-state), P4 (oversized step, deliberately unsafe). Headline
committed outcomes (all from `results/v2.9/deterministic/`):

- **D1** (lossless relaxation): P1 drives the burden from 17.0 to
  6.3×10⁻³⁰ with zero descent violations; P0 leaves it exactly constant.
- **D2** (lossy transport): P1 rests where the *loss-aware* force equals the
  threshold (0.0499…≈ θ = 0.05) while the loss-blind force is still 5.29;
  final burden P1 58.24 < P2 61.81, with 11 recorded descent violations for
  the loss-blind ablation. Loss-awareness changes the decision at rest.
- **D3** (three-cell relay): synchronous P1 propagates exactly one hop per
  tick (probe difference 0.0 at tick 1, first difference at tick 2);
  sequential P3 leaks two hops in one nominal tick (0.0162 at tick 1).
- **D4/D6/D7** (driven regenerative worlds, supply and demand shocks): P1
  serves 100% of feasible demand from admissible stock, stays admissible
  throughout, and recovers after shocks (recovery ticks 440 and 611); P0
  exits the physical domain, demonstrating the fixtures are not trivially
  safe.
- **D5** (Allee reserve pressure): **did not discriminate** — the reserve
  penalty ON and OFF arms produced identical headline outcomes (both
  converged, both served 150/150, no Allee crossings). The registered
  expectation that χ = 0 would collapse did not occur in this fixture. This
  negative result is retained and directly motivated D9's harder design.
- **D8** (negative control): the oversized step reproduces the V2.8
  Counterexample-A overshoot exactly at tick 1 (ratio 1.96) but did **not**
  grow monotonically for all 30 ticks — a partial hypothesis failure,
  recorded as such.

Interpretation limits are frozen in the plan: deterministic fixtures only; a
success is evidence the law *can* work in that fixture, never that it always
works.

## 10. D9: the Allee reserve-stress experiment

D9 (four arms, 200 ticks, `Δt = 0.2`) puts an Allee source (`A = 5`,
`K = 20`, certified `R_eff = 11`) under sustained demand 5.0 — deliberately
hard enough that unconstrained transport will breach the reserve. All numbers
below are the committed values from `results/v2.9/d9_d10/v29_d9_d10_summary.json`.

| Arm | Policy | Outcome | First reserve crossing | First Allee crossing | Physical over-use `O_physical` | Delivered (cum.) | Unmet (cum.) |
|-----|--------|---------|------------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------|------------------|--------------|
| D9-A | P1 (reserve-blind) | **collapse** | tick 8 | tick 17 | 201.845 | 189.69 | 10.31 |
| D9-B | soft (χ = 1) | **collapse** | tick 8 | tick 24 | 183.045 | 172.83 | 27.17 |
| D9-C | P1C (χ = 1, hard cap) | **safe rationing** | never | never | 1.5×10⁻¹⁵ (≤ tol 10⁻⁹) | 130.82 | 69.18 |
| D9-D | P1C (χ = 0, hard cap) | **safe rationing** | never | never | 1.5×10⁻¹⁵ (≤ tol 10⁻⁹) | 130.82 | 69.18 |

Readings:

- The soft penalty **delayed** the Allee crossing (tick 24 vs 17) and reduced
  over-use (183.0 vs 201.8) but crossed the reserve at the **same tick 8** —
  it slows damage; it does not prevent it (§3).
- Both P1C arms held the source at exactly `R_eff = 11` (final = minimum =
  11.0), with zero reserve and zero Allee crossings, the cap binding on
  193/200 ticks, and **zero observed one-step-preservation violations over
  200 eligible ticks**.
- P1C delivered less (130.8 vs 189.7) and reported the difference honestly as
  unmet demand (69.2): the demand was physically unsafe, and rationing — not
  silent depletion — is the designed behavior.
- **D9-C and D9-D are identical on every recorded metric.** The hard cap, not
  the penalty weight χ, provides preservation in this fixture.

## 11. D10: the service-versus-preservation phase map

D10 sweeps a logistic source–sink world (140 runs: an 80-run core map over
demand ratio `d/g_max ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.9, 1.0, 1.1}` × transport efficiency
`η ∈ {0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 1.0}`, plus 60 secondary-slice runs over `θ`, `δ/K`,
`χ`, `ρ`, `r_dt`), with four policies at every point. Committed
classification counts (35 runs per policy):

| Policy | collapse | safe_service | safe_rationing | runs with `O_physical > 10⁻⁹` |
|--------|----------|--------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| P0 (no transport) | 0 | 0 | **35** | 0 |
| P1 (unconstrained) | **25** | 10 | 0 | 25 |
| soft (reserve penalty) | **25** | 10 | 0 | 25 |
| P1C (hard cap) | **0** | **25** | **10** | **0** |

- P1 and soft produce **identical** classification maps: at these demand
  levels the soft penalty changes nothing that matters.
- Collapse for P1/soft tracks the analytic boundaries registered in the plan:
  every core point with `d/g_max ≥ 0.9` collapses except at `η = 1.0` with
  `d/g_max ≤ 0.9`, plus the transport-starved corner `(0.5, 0.5)`; safe
  service requires roughly `d/g_max ≤ η` (the deliverability boundary) and
  `d/g_max < 1` (the fold).
- P1C never collapses and never over-uses; where full service is deliverable
  it serves (25 points), where it is not it rations (10 points). Hypothesis
  H-D10-1 held at all tested points.
- **P0's 35 "safe rationing" runs are not success.** No transport trivially
  preserves stock by serving nothing. Preservation must always be read
  *together with service*: P1C matches P1's service wherever service was
  safe, and refuses only the unsafe remainder.
- 18 runs (P1/soft at the hardest corners) diverged and exited the physical
  domain; all 18 domain exits are **recorded, not dropped** (terminal status,
  exit tick, direction), and drive the serialization provenance of §12.

## 13. What failed, and why it is kept

Negative and partial results are retained as first-class outputs:

- **D5 failed to discriminate** (§9): the fixture was too easy; the reserve
  penalty was never stressed. D9 was designed from that failure.
- **The soft penalty failed in D9/D10** exactly as the algebra predicts (§3):
  delay, not prevention.
- **Unconstrained P1 collapses and over-uses** across 25/35 of the D10 map —
  the honest baseline that preservation must beat.
- **D8's monotone-growth sub-claim failed** (overshoot fired at tick 1 but
  did not grow monotonically); recorded, not repaired.
- **Attempt 1 of D9/D10 failed** on harness integrity and is preserved.
- **The stability diagnostics overflowed** on diverging runs — a
  serialization defect, fixed fail-closed (§12).
- Inherited from V2.6 and still open: the guarded EBU ledger is **exploitable
  on at least one topology** (+260 EBU while every source dies). Any future
  credit rule inherits that risk until re-audited.

## 14. Relationship to future EBU accounting

V2.9 is a **physical controller layer**, deliberately below any accounting.
The objective-alignment documents (committed in this branch) design — but do
**not** implement — the next layers: a **vector** ecological-debt ledger
`D = (D_carbon, D_water, …)` with an irreversible component that never
decreases and no netting between accounts; one-to-one EBU **restoration
credit** only for verified net physical restoration; and the two guardrails
G1/G2 (§1). The independent review's verdict on that design is "pass with
corrections", and it explicitly finds **scalar (fungible) EBU not currently
justified** — a first implementation would use resource-specific vector
accounts. None of this exists in code: V2.9 contains **no EBU issuance, no
ecological-debt ledger, no wallet, no exchange, no scalarisation, no actor
health or death, and no finite moving actor population.** `O_physical` is a
physical over-use *diagnostic*, not issued debt.

## 15. Limitations and falsification criteria

**Limitations (read before citing any result):**

- Deterministic toy worlds only (2–3 cells); no stochastic or confirmatory
  seed study for P1C yet; no random layouts.
- No finite moving actor population; no individual health or death; no
  complete society. No EBU issuance, debt ledger, wallets, exchange, or
  scalarisation (§13).
- The preservation theorem is one-step, synchronous, and margin-conditional
  (§7); reserve certification (`R_eff`) is assumed given, and long-horizon
  viability — the infinite-horizon kernel — remains open.
- No proof under arbitrary uncertainty: margins `ε_x, ε_u` cover declared
  measurement error only, not model mis-specification.
- Not peer reviewed. All checks (141 + 108 + 83 + 114 + 46 V2.9 checks, and
  all prior suites) are numerical validation at declared points, never proof.

**Falsification criteria** (registered in the alignment draft; the committed
fixture falsifiers are in the plans): the architecture is falsified by, among
others, (F-A) a trajectory labeled safe that in fact crosses a
non-substitutable reserve or increases irreversible debt; (F-B) a safe-export
certificate that, applied repeatedly under admissible drive, still drives a
source below its reserve; (F-C) wallet transfers that reduce recorded
physical debt; (F-D) damage-then-repair earning net positive credit without
ending above the pre-damage baseline. A certificate architecture that cannot
emit UNSUSTAINABLE/INFEASIBLE at all is rejected by construction. Within
V2.9's committed studies, the registered falsifiers (e.g. F-D10-1: P1C
collapsing while theorem assumptions hold) were **not** triggered.

## 16. Open problems

1. **Joint constrained theorem**: preservation *plus* a descent/dissipation
   inequality for the capped flux (the V2.8 analogue for P1C).
2. **Infinite-horizon controlled-invariant kernel** `K∞` and multi-step
   feasibility: when does one-step preservation compose forever?
3. Discrete overshoot margin for the reserve certificate (`δ`, `ε` sizing)
   and V2.7 Conjecture C-2 (reserve surrogate).
4. Multi-source, multi-edge joint invariance under proportional allocation
   (shared sinks, competing sources).
5. Stochastic and adversarial robustness of P1C (confirmatory seed study).
6. Ecological-debt attribution (overdraw → account map) and an operational
   "verified restoration" predicate; multi-actor credit attribution;
   scalarisation weights — all prerequisites for any EBU layer.
7. Reserve certification itself: who computes `R_eff`, how often, and with
   what non-local ecological model (the three-timescale design in the review
   is a sketch, not a result).
